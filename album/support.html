How Album works, in four steps

  1. Tap New album and give it a name.
  2. Pick the photos you want from your library.
  3. Pick a style — Classic, Mosaic, or Bold.
  4. Read the book, and drag photos around in Organize until the pages look right.

There are no page controls to learn. A page is worked out from the order your photos are in, so reordering photos is editing the book.

Common questions

A photo shows as unavailable. Why?

Album points at photos in your library rather than copying them. If a photo is deleted in the Photos app, there is nothing left to point at. Remove it from the book in Organize, or recover it from Photos → Recently Deleted.

Why did my page layout change when I moved one photo?

That is the design. Pages are recomputed from the photo order every time, so moving a photo can change which template a page uses. Nothing is lost — put the photo back and the old layout returns exactly.

What does “Mirror to Photos” do?

With it on, Album keeps an album in your Photos library with the same name and the same pictures as your book, so the set is available everywhere your photos are. Album adds and removes album membership only — it never deletes a photo. Turn it off in Settings at any time; the Photos album stays until you delete it yourself.

Album says it will not empty the Photos album.

Deliberate safety. If a sync would remove every photo from a Photos album, Album refuses and tells you instead. That prevents a mistake in the app from wiping an album you also use outside it.

How do I export a book?

Open the book and choose Export. You can produce a PDF of the whole book, or one image per page, at Standard or Print quality. The file goes to the iOS share sheet, so you can save it to Files, AirDrop it, or send it to a print service.

Will a print shop accept the PDF?

Usually, at Print quality — but every print service has its own requirements for bleed, page size and colour profile. Check their spec sheet and always order a proof first. Album does not do any printing itself.

Album only sees some of my photos.

iOS is limiting the app to a selection you chose. Open the iOS Settings app → Album → Photos and pick Full Access, or tap Select More Photos to add to the selection.

Does Album work offline? Does it need an account?

It works entirely offline and there is no account. Photos stored only in iCloud may need a connection for iOS to fetch the full-resolution original when you export.

Is it available in Arabic?

Yes. Album ships in English and Arabic, and follows your device language.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data. This removes every album Album stored. Your photos, and any album already created in the Photos app, are left alone.
