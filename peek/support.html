Common questions

Why won't toys appear in my room? Point the iPad down at an open patch of floor and move it slowly for a moment — Peek needs to detect a flat surface before toys can be placed. If the camera permission was denied, or the device doesn't support augmented reality, Peek automatically switches to a virtual toy room so your child can still play.

Is the camera recording us? No. The camera is used to detect the floor so toys can be placed in augmented reality, and to take a picture only when a grown-up taps the picture button. Nothing else is ever recorded, saved, or sent anywhere — see our Privacy Policy for details.

How does my child hear the words? Tap any toy after placing it, and Peek speaks its name out loud — first in the app's active language, then in the other, so every toy teaches both English and Arabic. Turn spoken names or sound effects off anytime in Settings.

What's inside Peek? 150 things to discover across 14 worlds: Home, Animals, Food, Vehicles, Numbers, Shapes & Colors, Letters, Arabic Letters, Nature, Clothes, My Body, Weather, Family, and Colors.

What are the games? Find it! asks your child to tap the toy that matches a spoken prompt, How many? turns counting into a tap-the-number quiz, Memory is a flip-and-match card game, First letter asks which toy starts with a given letter, and Trace it lets a finger follow a letter or number stroke by stroke. None of them has a fail state — a wrong tap just invites another try. Discoveries also earn badges, which sit on a shelf in My Collection.

Can my child hear my voice instead of the robot voice? Yes. Open the grown-ups' area, choose Record your voice, and record yourself saying each word. Peek will ask for microphone permission the first time. Your clips are stored on this iPad only, and are never uploaded or shared. Turn "Use my recordings" off to go back to the built-in voice without deleting anything.

How do I delete a voice recording? On the recording screen in the grown-ups' area, tap Delete recording for that word. Deleting the Peek app removes every recording at once.

Peek says it can't use the microphone. Open the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Microphone, and turn Peek on. (You can also find it under Settings → Peek.) Recording is entirely optional — Peek keeps working with its built-in voice if you leave the microphone off.

How do I save or share a picture of the scene? Tap the picture button while toys are out. Behind the grown-ups' gate you can save it to your photo library or send it with the normal iOS share sheet. Peek asks only for permission to add a photo — it can never see the photos you already have. If saving fails, check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos and allow Peek to add photos.

How do I limit how long my child plays? In the grown-ups' area, set Play time to 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, or 60 minutes — or leave it on No limit. When the time is up, Peek shows a gentle "time for a break" screen; a grown-up can grant more time. The timer covers one sitting and starts fresh each time a child is picked.

How do multiple kids share one iPad? Tap "Add a child" on the "Who's playing?" screen to create a profile with a name and an avatar. Each child keeps their own collection, badges, and progress; switch between them anytime from the avatar button on the Explore screen. A child can also be given their own language, so one sibling can learn in Arabic while another learns in English.

What does the widget show? Add the Word of the Day widget to your Home Screen, or a Peek accessory to your Lock Screen, for a new word each day plus the current child's name, avatar, and how many things they've found. It reads a small local snapshot the app saves for it and makes no network requests.

How do I get to Settings or see learning progress? Tap the small gear icon in the corner of the "Who's playing?" or Explore screen. You'll be asked a quick math question — a gentle gate to keep Settings, profile management, and recording away from little fingers, not an account or login.

How do I reset a child's collection? Open Settings (behind the grown-ups gate) and tap Reset collection. This clears every discovered sticker for the current child only.

Is there Arabic support? Yes — the entire app, including right-to-left layout and spoken audio, is available in English and Arabic from Settings, or from the language toggle during onboarding.
