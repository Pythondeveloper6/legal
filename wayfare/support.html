Common questions

Where are my trips stored? On your device only. Wayfare has no servers and no account — your routes, places, and photos never leave your iPhone or iPad.

Why does Wayfare ask for "Always" location access? So it can keep recording your route in the background after you lock your phone or switch apps, until you end the trip. Wayfare only uses location while a trip is recording.

How many places can I save for free? Up to two. Wayfare Pro is a one‑time purchase that unlocks unlimited saved places, plus full insights and route export/replay. Recording trips is always free.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here? Open Settings in Wayfare and tap "Restore Purchases."

How do I delete my data? Delete a single trip or place by swiping it, or remove everything in Settings → "Clear all data."
