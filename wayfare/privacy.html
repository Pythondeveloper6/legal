Wayfare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Wayfare is built so your travels stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Wayfare does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your trips, routes, saved places, photos, and notes are stored on your device. Wayfare has no servers and no account system.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Wayfare stores

Everything you record in Wayfare — trip routes, breadcrumb locations, saved places, waypoint notes, and photos — is saved locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on‑device storage. This data is controlled by you. The developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Location

Wayfare uses your location only while you are recording a trip, so it can draw your route. With your permission, it continues to record in the background until you end the trip, which is why Wayfare asks for "Always" location access. You can stop recording at any time, and your location history never leaves your device.

Motion

Wayfare uses motion data to automatically label a trip as walking, running, cycling, or driving. You can turn this off in Settings → Recording. Motion data is processed on device and is not stored or transmitted.

Photos & Camera

When you attach a photo to a waypoint, Wayfare only accesses the specific photos you choose through the system photo picker, or a photo you take with the camera. It does not read the rest of your photo library, and the photos you attach stay on your device.

Apple services Wayfare relies on

  • MapKit — to draw maps and route overlays, and to open turn‑by‑turn directions in Apple Maps. Apple handles map requests under its own privacy policy.
  • StoreKit — when you purchase Wayfare Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. Wayfare never receives your payment details.

Wayfare does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any trip or place at any time, or remove everything in Settings → "Clear all data."
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Wayfare does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
