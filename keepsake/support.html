Common questions

Where are my memories stored? On your device, and synced privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. No one else can see them.

How much does Keepsake cost? Keepsake is free. There are no subscriptions or in‑app purchases.

How do I add photos or videos to a memory? Tap the “+” button to create a memory, then “Add photos” to pick from your library.

What is a time capsule? When creating a memory you can seal it until a future date. It stays hidden until the date you choose.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual memories from each memory, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all data.”
