Keepsake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Keepsake is built so your memories stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Keepsake does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your memories — photos, videos, titles, stories, dates, places, and the people you tag — are stored on your device and, if you’re signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to them.
  • Spaces are private to you. Keepsake syncs a space between your own devices; it cannot give another person access to one, and nothing you write is visible to anyone else. Share cards you create deliberately leave out places and coordinates.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Keepsake stores

Everything you create in Keepsake (photos, videos, text, dates, locations, people, spaces, and albums) is saved locally using Apple’s on‑device database. If iCloud is enabled on your device, this data also syncs via Apple’s CloudKit to your private iCloud account so it’s available on your other devices. This data is controlled by you and your Apple ID; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services Keepsake relies on

  • iCloud / CloudKit — to sync and back up your memories within your own account.
  • MapKit — Keepsake can show your located memories on a map. Map rendering is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.
  • Geocoding — when you type a place name onto a memory, that text (and only that text) is sent to Apple’s geocoding service to look up a coordinate for the map. Keepsake never reads your device’s location and never asks for location permission. If the lookup fails, the name you typed is kept and the memory simply isn’t mapped.
  • Photos — Keepsake only accesses the specific photos and videos you choose through the system photo picker. It does not read your library beyond what you pick.
  • Notifications — optional reminders for anniversaries and “on this day” memories are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Keepsake does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any memory at any time, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all data.”
  • Settings → “Export my memories” writes a complete copy — readable JSON plus your original photos and videos — that you can save anywhere. Export is free and includes memories that exist only on this device.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. iCloud copies are managed through your Apple ID settings.

Children

Keepsake does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
