Accounts (optional)

Vroom offers an entirely optional Sign in with Apple button, reachable only from Settings behind the parent gate. Nothing about the core game requires it, and the app has no server-side account system of its own — your Apple identifier (and a name or email, if you choose to share them through Apple's sign-in sheet) is stored only in this device's Keychain. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored.

  • Signing out. Settings → Sign Out (behind the parent gate) removes the stored credential from this device. You can sign in again at any time with the same Apple ID.
