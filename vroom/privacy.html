Vroom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Vroom is made for children, so it is built to collect nothing about them. This policy describes exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device. It is written to be read by a parent in a couple of minutes, not by a lawyer.

Developer: Mahmoud Ahmed · Contact: apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com

The short version

  • Vroom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data — about a child or a grown-up.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. The only free-text field in the app is an optional name for a saved build in the Garage (like "Speedy Red"); it is never required, never leaves the device, and is not checked against anything.
  • The app makes no network requests at all. Every part, track and sound is bundled inside the download, and the app works with the device in Airplane Mode.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no trackers, no in-app purchases.
  • Everything the app saves — builds, stars, unlocked parts, settings — stays on that one device, and a grown-up can erase all of it at any time.

What Vroom stores on this device

  • Garage builds — the cars and rockets your child designs (which parts and paint are picked for each slot), plus their name if your child gives one a name.
  • Progress — stars earned, which parts and paints are unlocked, best results per track, and whether onboarding has been completed.
  • App settings — appearance (light/dark/system), language (English/Arabic), whether haptics are on, and a parent override that unlocks everything.
  • All of the above is saved together as one local file (progress.json) in an App Group container that Vroom shares only with its own Home Screen widget — both are made by the same developer, both run only on your device, and nothing in that container is ever transmitted anywhere. The only separate value is the haptics on/off switch, kept in the app's private UserDefaults. None of it is uploaded, backed up to a server of ours, or shared with anyone. It travels only inside your own Apple device backup, if you use one.

What Vroom never does

  • No network requests. The app contains no networking code, no web view, no remote configuration, and no way for a child to reach the internet, a store, an advertisement, or another person from inside it. Every race is simulated entirely on the device using a deterministic physics engine — the same build and track always produce the same run.
  • No permissions. The app declares no privacy-sensitive permission at all — no camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, health data, or advertising identifier — so iOS never shows your child a permission prompt.
  • No third-party code. There are no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting or social SDKs of any kind bundled in the app.
  • No purchases. There is no in-app purchase, no subscription, and no store of any kind inside the app.

Home Screen widget

Vroom's widget shows today's daily challenge and your best result on it so far. It reads that information from the same on-device progress.json the app writes — through the App Group container described above — and never makes a network request of its own. The widget shows nothing about your child beyond a number your family already earned on this device.

Children's privacy

Vroom is designed to be used directly by children, is submitted to the App Store Kids Category, and carries a 4+ age rating.

  • We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted, there is no profile of your child anywhere, and nothing about your child exists on any server of ours.
  • COPPA (US). The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act governs the online collection of personal information from children under 13. Vroom collects none: no name, no email address, no screen name, no photo, no voice recording, no location, and no persistent identifier used for tracking. There is therefore no parental-consent flow, because there is nothing to consent to.
  • GDPR-K (EU/UK) and similar laws. Article 8 of the GDPR (and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code) concerns the processing of children's personal data. Vroom performs no such processing: no personal data is collected, no profiling or automated decision-making takes place, no data is shared with any third party, and no data leaves the device, so there is no international transfer and no lawful basis is required.
  • No behavioural advertising and no tracking. The app shows no ads at all, of any kind, and never builds a profile of a child for any purpose.
  • No contact with strangers. There is no chat, no comments, no community, no user accounts, and no way for a child to send or receive anything.
  • No links out of the app without a parent. Settings — the only place in Vroom with an external link (rating the app, contacting support, or opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use) — sits behind a parent gate, so a child cannot open a web browser or leave the app on their own.
  • Your rights as a parent. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete from us. Everything your child builds is under your own control on your own device, and the control below erases it completely.

The parent gate

Settings sits behind a parent gate — a short challenge a young child can't read or solve. This is a soft barrier that keeps grown-up controls, and the app's only external links, away from little thumbs. It is not an account, it stores no password, and nothing entered there is transmitted anywhere.

Your control

  • Settings → Delete all data (behind the parent gate) — removes every saved build, every star, every unlocked part and paint, and resets all settings on this device. This cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data, including the widget's copy, from your device.

App Store privacy label

Vroom is declared on the App Store as Data Not Collected: no data used to track you, no data linked to you, and no data not linked to you.

Changes
