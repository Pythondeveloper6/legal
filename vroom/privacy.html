Vroom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Vroom is designed for children, and its core game — building and racing — still collects nothing about them at all. Two things in the app are entirely optional and live behind a parent gate: signing in with Apple, and buying Vroom Plus. This policy describes exactly what each of those does and does not do with information on your device. It is written to be read by a parent in a couple of minutes, not by a lawyer.

Developer: Mahmoud Ahmed · Contact: apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com

The short version

  • The core game — building, racing, stars, unlocks — collects nothing about a child or a grown-up, and never has.
  • Two optional, adult-facing features exist behind a parent gate: Sign in with Apple and Vroom Plus, an optional subscription. Both are entirely optional; the app works fully without either.
  • Signing in stores an Apple identifier only in the device Keychain. It is never transmitted anywhere — Vroom makes no network requests of its own and there is no server of ours.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; Vroom never sees a payment detail.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no trackers.
  • Everything the app saves — builds, stars, unlocked parts, settings, and the optional sign-in — stays on that one device, and a grown-up can remove all of it at any time.

What Vroom stores on this device

  • Garage builds — the cars and rockets your child designs (which parts and paint are picked for each slot), plus their name if your child gives one a name.
  • Progress — stars earned, which parts and paints are unlocked, best results per track, and whether onboarding has been completed.
  • App settings — appearance (light/dark/system), language (English/Arabic), whether haptics are on, and a parent override that unlocks everything.
  • All of the above is saved together as one local file (progress.json) inside Vroom's own private app container on your device, and nothing in that container is ever transmitted anywhere. The only separate value is the haptics on/off switch, kept in the app's private UserDefaults. None of it is uploaded, backed up to a server of ours, or shared with anyone. It travels only inside your own Apple device backup, if you use one.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Settings — behind the parent gate — offers an optional Sign in with Apple button. Nobody has to use it; every part of Vroom, including Garage builds, progress and Vroom Plus, works exactly the same whether or not anyone signs in.

  • What's stored. The stable identifier Apple assigns this person for this app is saved to the device's Keychain. If the grown-up chooses to share a name or email with the app through Apple's own sign-in sheet (Apple only offers that choice the very first time an Apple ID authorizes Vroom), that name is saved to the Keychain too. None of it is written to progress.json, to UserDefaults, or anywhere else.
  • Never transmitted. Vroom makes no network requests of its own, has no server, and sends the Apple identifier, name or email nowhere. They exist only in that device's Keychain, for that one device.
  • Why it exists. Purely to identify the grown-up in charge of the device, for a future purchase restore. It does not unlock anything by itself, and it never appears anywhere a child can see it.
  • Sign Out. A visible Sign Out control, behind the parent gate, clears the stored Apple identifier and name from the Keychain. There is no server-side account to leave behind — the same Apple ID can sign in again at any time.
  • Delete Account. A separate, visible Delete Account control, behind the parent gate and its own confirmation, also clears the Keychain entry. Because there is no account of ours on any server, deleting it locally is the whole of what "delete" can mean here — there is nothing left on our side to remove.
  • Automatic re-check. Every time the app launches, Vroom asks Apple whether a stored credential is still valid, and signs out locally on this device if the grown-up revoked Vroom's access from Apple ID settings elsewhere.

Vroom Plus (optional subscription)

Vroom Plus is an optional, auto-renewable subscription reachable only from behind the parent gate (in Settings, or when choosing certain paint colours). It unlocks cosmetic paint colours only — it never sells stars, progress, tier unlocks, hints, or any accessibility or data control, and the whole learning loop (building, racing, stars, unlocking parts) stays free whether or not anyone subscribes.

  • Two plans: a monthly subscription and a yearly subscription, each with the same 7-day free trial and the same entitlement — only the billing period differs. Exact current prices and trial terms are shown on the in-app paywall and on the App Store product page before you buy.
  • Apple handles the purchase. Buying, renewing, and cancelling Vroom Plus all go through Apple's own in-app purchase system and your Apple Account. Vroom never collects, sees, or stores any payment detail (no card number, billing address, or similar) — that information stays with Apple.
  • What Vroom knows. The app checks with Apple (via StoreKit) whether the signed-in Apple Account currently holds an active Vroom Plus entitlement, so it can unlock the paint colours. Vroom does not receive or store your name, email or payment information as part of that check, and it makes no network request of its own to do it — the check happens entirely through Apple's on-device StoreKit framework.
  • Restore Purchases. A Restore Purchases control on the paywall asks Apple to re-sync this device's entitlements — useful after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. It does not require signing in with Apple.
  • See the Terms of Use for the full auto-renewal, cancellation and trial terms.

What Vroom never does

  • No network requests of its own. The app contains no networking code, no web view, no remote configuration, and no way for a child to reach the internet, an advertisement, or another person from inside it. Every race is simulated entirely on the device using a deterministic physics engine — the same build and track always produce the same run. Sign in with Apple and Vroom Plus purchases are handled by Apple's own system frameworks (AuthenticationServices and StoreKit), not by any server or network code of ours.
  • No permissions beyond Apple's own frameworks. The app declares no privacy-sensitive permission at all — no camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, health data, or advertising identifier — so iOS never shows your child a permission prompt.
  • No third-party code. There are no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting or social SDKs of any kind bundled in the app.

Home Screen widget

Vroom's widget shows today's daily challenge — the track and goal of the day. That challenge is worked out from the date alone, so the widget reads no saved progress at all: it holds no stars, no scores, no builds, no settings, and no sign-in state, and it never makes a network request. Nothing about your child, or about a grown-up's account or purchases, appears on the Home Screen.

Children's privacy

Vroom is designed to be used directly by children and carries a 4+ age rating with a parent gate in front of Settings, every external link, sign-in and purchases. Vroom is not submitted to the App Store Kids Category, because that category does not permit account creation or data collection of any kind (App Store guideline 5.1.4) — and Vroom now offers an optional account and an optional purchase, both confined behind the parent gate and both aimed at the grown-up, never the child.

  • The core game collects no personal information from anyone. Building, racing, stars and unlocks involve no data collection, no profile, and nothing transmitted, whether the player is a child or an adult.
  • The optional adult-facing features collect a minimum, and only from the grown-up who chooses to use them. Signing in with Apple stores an identifier (and, optionally, a name/email) in the device Keychain only; Vroom Plus purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Neither is reachable without first passing the parent gate, and neither is ever transmitted to us.
  • COPPA (US). The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act governs the online collection of personal information from children under 13. Vroom itself operates no server and transmits nothing it stores to anyone; the only account-like feature (Sign in with Apple) sits behind a parent gate, is optional, and is intended for the grown-up managing the device, not the child.
  • GDPR-K (EU/UK) and similar laws. Article 8 of the GDPR (and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code) concerns the processing of children's personal data. Vroom's gameplay performs no such processing at all: no personal data is collected from play, no profiling or automated decision-making takes place, and no gameplay data leaves the device. The optional sign-in and purchase live behind the parent gate and are designed for the adult, not the child.
  • No behavioural advertising and no tracking. The app shows no ads at all, of any kind, and never builds a profile of anyone for advertising purposes.
  • No contact with strangers. There is no chat, no comments, no community, and no way for a child to send or receive anything from or to another person.
  • No links out of the app without a parent. Settings — where sign-in, Vroom Plus, and every external link (rating the app, contacting support, or opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use) live — sits behind a parent gate, so a child cannot open a web browser, sign in, buy anything, or leave the app on their own.
  • Your rights as a parent. Because gameplay collects nothing, and the optional account/purchase data is confined to your own device's Keychain and to Apple's own systems, there is nothing held by us to request, correct, or delete. Sign Out and Delete Account (both behind the parent gate) clear the local Apple credential immediately, and Settings → Delete all data erases everything else.

The parent gate

Settings sits behind a parent gate — a short challenge a young child can't read or solve. This is a soft barrier that keeps grown-up controls — sign-in, Vroom Plus, and the app's external links — away from little thumbs. The gate itself is not an account, stores no password, and nothing entered there is transmitted anywhere.

Your control

  • Settings → Sign Out (behind the parent gate) — clears the stored Apple identifier and name from this device's Keychain. You can sign in again at any time with the same Apple ID.
  • Settings → Delete Account (behind the parent gate, with its own confirmation) — also clears the Keychain entry. There is no server-side account, so this is the complete deletion available.
  • Settings → Delete all data (behind the parent gate) — removes every saved build, every star, every unlocked part and paint, and resets all settings on this device. This cannot be undone.
  • Cancelling Vroom Plus — see the Terms of Use for how to manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account. Cancelling does not delete anything Vroom stores.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data, including the Keychain entry, from your device.

App Store privacy label

Vroom's App Store privacy label declares:

  • Data used to track you: None.
  • Data linked to you: Identifiers (the Sign in with Apple user identifier) and Contact Info (name and email, only if you choose to share them through Apple's own sign-in sheet). Both are used for App Functionality only, and both stay in this device's Keychain.
  • Data not linked to you: None.
