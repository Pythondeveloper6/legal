Common questions

Does Vroom need an internet connection? No. Vroom makes no network requests at all — every part, track and sound ships inside the download, and the app works with the device in Airplane Mode.

How much does Vroom cost? It's free. No subscription, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

How does building and racing work? Pick a car or a rocket, then choose a part for each slot — wheels, body, engine, wings and more. Vroom explains what each choice trades off (more grip but less top speed, more thrust but more weight, and so on) before you race. Tap Race to run a simulation on the chosen track and see how your build performed.

Why did my child's race turn out the same way twice? Vroom's physics simulation is deterministic — the same build on the same track always produces the same run, so your child can change one part, race again, and see exactly what that part changed.

How do stars and unlocks work? Each track awards up to three stars based on how your build performs. Earning stars unlocks new parts and paint colours to try in future builds.

What is the Garage? It's where saved builds live. Save a build you like, give it a name, and come back to it any time to tweak or race it again.

What is the daily challenge? A track-and-goal combination that changes once a day. It shows up on the Home screen and on the Home Screen widget. The widget works the challenge out from the date alone — it reads no saved progress and never fetches anything over the network.

Where is my child's progress stored? On that device only, in the app's own private storage. There are no accounts and no server — nothing about a build or a race ever leaves the iPhone or iPad it was made on.

How do I change appearance, language, or haptics? Open Settings from behind the parent gate. Vroom is fully bilingual — English and Arabic, including right-to-left layout — and can follow the system appearance or be set to light or dark.

How do I get to Settings? Settings sits behind a parent gate — a short challenge a young child can't read or solve. This keeps grown-up controls, and Vroom's only external links, away from little thumbs; it isn't an account or a login.

How do I delete my child's builds, or reset the app? Behind the parent gate: Settings → Delete all data removes every saved build, every star, every unlocked part and paint, and resets all settings on this device. That one can't be undone.

Does the app ask for the camera, photos, microphone or location? No. Vroom requests no permissions at all, so your child will never see a permission prompt.

Are there ads, or anything my child could tap out of the app into? No. There are no ads, no chat, no community, and no store. The only external links in the app (rating Vroom, contacting support, or reading this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use) live in Settings, behind the parent gate.
