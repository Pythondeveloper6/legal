Vroom — Support

Need a hand with Vroom? We're happy to help.

Common questions

Does Vroom need an internet connection? No. Vroom makes no network requests of its own — every part, track and sound ships inside the download, and building, racing, stars and unlocks all work with the device in Airplane Mode. Signing in with Apple and Vroom Plus purchases go through Apple's own on-device system frameworks, not through anything of ours.

How much does Vroom cost? Vroom is free to download, and the whole building-and-racing game — every track, every star, every part unlock — is free to play. Vroom Plus is an optional subscription that unlocks extra cosmetic paint colours; see below. There are no ads.

How does building and racing work? Pick a car or a rocket, then choose a part for each slot — wheels, body, engine, wings and more. Vroom explains what each choice trades off (more grip but less top speed, more thrust but more weight, and so on) before you race. Tap Race to run a simulation on the chosen track and see how your build performed.

Why did my child's race turn out the same way twice? Vroom's physics simulation is deterministic — the same build on the same track always produces the same run, so your child can change one part, race again, and see exactly what that part changed.

How do stars and unlocks work? Each track awards up to three stars based on how your build performs. Earning stars unlocks new parts and paint colours to try in future builds — this is entirely separate from Vroom Plus, and works the same whether or not anyone has subscribed.

What is the Garage? It's where saved builds live. Save a build you like, give it a name, and come back to it any time to tweak or race it again.

What is the daily challenge? A track-and-goal combination that changes once a day. It shows up on the Home screen and on the Home Screen widget. The widget works the challenge out from the date alone — it reads no saved progress, no sign-in state, and never fetches anything over the network.

Where is my child's progress stored? On that device only, in the app's own private storage. There is no server of ours — nothing about a build or a race ever leaves the iPhone or iPad it was made on.

How do I sign in? Sign in with Apple is entirely optional and lives in Settings, behind the parent gate — it's for the grown-up managing the device, not required for any part of gameplay. Open Settings, solve the parent gate, and tap the Sign in with Apple button. Your Apple identifier (and a name or email, only if you choose to share them through Apple's own sheet) is saved to this device's Keychain only, and is never sent anywhere — see the Privacy Policy for the details.

How do I sign out? Settings → Sign Out (behind the parent gate). This clears the stored Apple credential from the device; you can sign in again anytime with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete Account (behind the parent gate, with its own confirmation). There is no account on any server of ours, so this simply clears the local Apple credential from the device's Keychain — the same thing Sign Out does, offered as its own explicit action.

What does Vroom Plus include? Vroom Plus is an optional, auto-renewable subscription that unlocks extra cosmetic paint colours. It never affects stars, progress, part unlocks, hints, or accessibility or data controls — the whole building-and-racing game stays free either way. It's offered as a monthly plan (com.mahmoudahmed.vroom.plus.monthly, $2.99/month) or a yearly plan (com.mahmoudahmed.vroom.plus.yearly, $19.99/year), each with a 7-day free trial for eligible accounts. It's reachable only from behind the parent gate.

How do I restore a Vroom Plus purchase? Open the Vroom Plus screen (Settings, behind the parent gate) and tap Restore Purchases. This asks Apple to re-sync this device with your Apple Account's existing purchases — handy after reinstalling the app or setting up a new device. You don't need to sign in with Apple to restore a purchase.

How do I cancel Vroom Plus, or stop it from renewing? On your device, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Vroom Plus, and cancel or turn off auto-renew there. Vroom Plus subscriptions auto-renew unless turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Payment is processed entirely by Apple, and the developer has no way to see or process a payment directly. Cancelling doesn't delete any Garage build, star or unlocked part — it only stops future Plus paint colours from being available once the current period ends.

How do I change appearance, language, or haptics? Open Settings from behind the parent gate. Vroom is fully bilingual — English and Arabic, including right-to-left layout — and can follow the system appearance or be set to light or dark.

How do I get to Settings? Settings sits behind a parent gate — a short challenge a young child can't read or solve. This keeps grown-up controls — appearance, language, sign-in, Vroom Plus, and Vroom's only external links — away from little thumbs; it isn't an account or a login.

How do I delete my child's builds, or reset the app? Behind the parent gate: Settings → Delete all data removes every saved build, every star, every unlocked part and paint, and resets all settings on this device. That one can't be undone. It doesn't affect an active Vroom Plus subscription — cancel that separately through your Apple Account if you want to stop it too.

Does the app ask for the camera, photos, microphone or location? No. Vroom requests no such permissions at all, so your child will never see a permission prompt for them.

Are there ads, or anything my child could tap out of the app into? No. There are no ads, no chat, no community, and no store visible outside the parent gate. The only external links in the app (rating Vroom, contacting support, or reading this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use), plus sign-in and Vroom Plus, all live in Settings, behind the parent gate.
