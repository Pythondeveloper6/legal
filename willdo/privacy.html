Willdo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Willdo is a daily planner built so your plans stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Willdo has no server and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your tasks, lists, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • You can delete your account and all of your data at any time in Settings.

Sign in with Apple

Willdo asks you to sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app an account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address). Willdo stores these on your device only: the identifier in the iOS Keychain, and the name and email in the app's local settings. They are used to keep you signed in and to greet you by name. They are never sent to the developer or to any other party, because Willdo has no server to send them to.

What Willdo stores

Everything you create in Willdo, including tasks, lists, estimates, plans, and settings, is saved locally using Apple's on-device database. The developer cannot see or retrieve it. Your tasks are stored only on this device; only the optional calendar events sync, through your calendar account.

Apple services Willdo relies on

  • Calendar (EventKit): with your permission, Willdo reads events from the calendars you choose so it can show how much of your day is already committed. Willdo only writes to your calendar if you turn on Show commitments in Calendar (part of Willdo Plus). Then it adds each commitment as an event in a calendar of its own that it creates, named "Willdo" (or, if you choose a separate calendar for each Area, one named after that Area, such as "Willdo – Work"), and moves or removes those events when the commitment changes. If you already have a calendar with that name, Willdo leaves it alone and creates its own beside it. It never edits or deletes any other event, and turning the setting off removes Willdo's events while Willdo has full calendar access. Commitment events sync through the calendar account they're stored in, which is iCloud by default (On My iPhone when iCloud Calendar is off), like any event in that account. Willdo itself sends event details nowhere.
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, Willdo schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent through a push server.
  • Apple Intelligence: features such as the weekly plan run with Apple's on-device models. Your tasks are processed on your device, and no data leaves it.
  • StoreKit: if you buy Willdo Plus, the purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details. Willdo checks your purchase status with StoreKit on your device.
  • Widgets and Live Activities: these read a snapshot of your day stored in an App Group container on your device, shared only between Willdo and its own widget.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: these run inside Willdo on your device and read and add to your tasks in its local database. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Willdo includes no non-Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • Delete account: in Willdo's Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. This removes all tasks, lists, areas, and evening reviews, and your stored name, email, and sign-in from your device, and returns you to the sign-in screen. Your app preferences, such as working hours, stay. Because everything is local, nothing remains on a server. To also stop Willdo from using your Apple ID, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security, then Sign in with Apple, choose Willdo, and tap Stop using Apple ID.
  • Sign out: in Settings, choose Account, then Sign out.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Willdo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
