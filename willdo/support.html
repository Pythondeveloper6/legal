Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device only. Willdo has no server and does not sync through iCloud. No one else can see it.

Why does Willdo ask me to sign in? Sign in with Apple creates a local account on your device. Your identifier, name, and email stay on your device and are never sent to the developer.

Why does Willdo ask for calendar access? So it can show how much of your day is already committed. Access is read-only; Willdo never changes your calendar.

How do reminders work? Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing leaves your device.

How do I delete my account and data? In Willdo's Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. Your tasks, lists, groups, evening reviews, and stored name and email are removed from your device. To also remove Willdo from your Apple ID, go to iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security, then Sign in with Apple, choose Willdo, and tap Stop using Apple ID.

How do I cancel Willdo Plus? Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Willdo, and tap Cancel Subscription. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase and has nothing to cancel.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the Plus screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.
