Common questions

What is Home World? Home World is a gentle first tour of the house for young children. Tap through five rooms — Kitchen, Bathroom, Living Room, Bedroom, and the cleaning cupboard — and meet 35 everyday things, each a friendly 3-D model that speaks its name and shows what it does.

How does my child hear the words? Tap any thing to hear its name spoken out loud in the app's active language, and press "Show me!" to watch it work. You can turn narration or sound effects off, or change the voice speed, any time in Settings.

Is there Arabic support? Yes — the whole app, including right-to-left layout and spoken audio, is available in English and Arabic. Switch languages from Settings, or with the language toggle during onboarding.

Does the app need the internet? No. Home World works completely offline. There is no account and no sign-in, and the app makes no network requests at all.

How much does it cost? Home World is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

Is it safe for my child alone? Yes. There is no way for a child to reach the internet, a store, another person, or an advertisement from inside the app. Settings sit behind a parent gate.

How do I get to Settings? Tap the small gear icon on the home screen. You'll be asked a quick multiplication question — a gentle gate to keep Settings for grown-ups, not an account or a login.

How do I clear my child's progress? Open Settings (behind the grown-ups gate), then tap Data → Delete all data. This clears the saved list of things your child has explored. Home World stores no personal data, so there is nothing else to remove.

What is the "thing of the day" widget? Add the Home World widget to your Home Screen and it shows one thing from the app's catalogue, chosen from the date alone. It reads no personal data and makes no network requests.

Is my child's data private? Yes. Home World collects nothing, has no ads or trackers, and sends nothing off the device — see the Privacy Policy for details.
