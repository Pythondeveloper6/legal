Lux — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Lux is built so that controlling your displays never costs you your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Lux does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Everything Lux does happens on your Mac. Your settings, presets, schedules, and rules are stored locally. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third‑party trackers.

What Lux stores

Lux saves your preferences — display presets, brightness schedules, per‑app rules, on‑connect rules, KVM input rules, and saved display arrangements — as a single JSON file in your Mac's Application Support folder (~/Library/Application Support/Lux/). This file never leaves your device.

Lux talks to your monitors directly over DDC/CI (the display control channel built into the cable) to set things like brightness, contrast, volume, input, and power. These commands go to the hardware on your desk; they do not pass through any server.

System permissions Lux may ask for

  • Accessibility — only if you turn on "use the keyboard brightness keys for external displays." This lets Lux see the F1/F2 brightness key presses so it can forward them to your external monitor. Lux does not log keystrokes or read any other input.
  • Launch at login — handled by macOS through SMAppService when you enable it.

You can revoke either permission at any time in System Settings. Lux requests nothing else — no camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, or files.

Networking

Lux makes no network connections. It has no analytics or update service that phones home.

Your control

  • Change or remove any preset, schedule, or rule at any time in Lux's settings.
  • Use Settings → About → "Reset all settings" to return Lux to a clean state.
  • Deleting the app and removing ~/Library/Application Support/Lux/ erases everything Lux has stored.

Children

Lux does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
