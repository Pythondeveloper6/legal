Common questions

My monitor doesn't appear in Lux. Lux controls external displays over DDC/CI on Apple‑silicon Macs. Make sure the monitor is connected directly (some hubs and adapters block DDC/CI), that DDC/CI is enabled in the monitor's on‑screen menu, and that it isn't the Mac's built‑in display. The Mac's own screen is handled by macOS, not Lux.

The brightness keys (F1/F2) don't control my external display. Turn on "Use the keyboard brightness keys for external displays" in Settings → General, then grant Lux the Accessibility permission when prompted (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility).

Where are my settings stored? In a single file on your Mac at ~/Library/Application Support/Lux/. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I start Lux automatically? Settings → General → "Launch Lux at login."

How do I reset everything? Settings → About → "Reset all settings."
