Common questions

Where is my writing stored? On your device only. Daybook has no accounts and no server — your day pages, ink, and settings never leave your Mac or iPad.

Why can't I draw with the pencil on Mac? The handwritten ink layer uses PencilKit, which is an iPad‑only feature. On Mac (via Mac Catalyst) you can still type on the ruled lines of every day page — just the freeform ink toggle is unavailable.

Why does Daybook ask for Calendar access? Daybook shows your events on the day timeline next to your writing, and lets you add, edit, or delete events without leaving the app. Choose which calendars appear in Settings → Calendars; you can change your mind anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

How do I change how the pages look? Settings → Page Design lets you switch between Oxblood Leather, Minimal White, Dark Mode, and Kraft/Dotted. Settings → Appearance sets light/dark independently, and Settings → Text lets you pick the page font and text size.

How do I see the Analytics dashboard? Tap the chart icon on the right‑hand rail to open Analytics — a breakdown of your events by time range (week, month, 30 days, year), completed vs. upcoming, busiest weekdays, and per‑calendar time.

How do I zoom the planner in or out? Use ⌘+ / ⌘‑ to zoom, ⌘0 to reset, or the zoom slider in Settings → Text.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual day pages or events inside the app, or delete the app itself to remove its local database and settings from your device.
