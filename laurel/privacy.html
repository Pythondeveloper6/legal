Laurel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Laurel is built so your achievements stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Laurel does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your achievements — titles, notes, categories, dates, and significance — are stored on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers, and no account to create.

What Laurel stores

Everything you create in Laurel (achievements, notes, categories, dates, and your appearance and reminder preferences) is saved locally on your device. The data lives in the app's own storage so the home‑screen widgets can read it. This data is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it. Laurel does not upload your achievements to any server.

Apple services Laurel relies on

  • StoreKit — when you purchase Laurel Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. Laurel never receives your payment details.
  • Notifications — if you turn on the optional daily reminder, Laurel schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent off the device.

Laurel does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any achievement, or all achievements, at any time inside the app (Settings → "Delete all data").
  • You can export your achievements at any time as CSV (Settings → "Export achievements"), free of charge and with no Pro purchase required; Laurel Pro adds a full versioned JSON backup that also includes your categories.
  • If Laurel's saved file is ever unreadable, it is moved aside rather than overwritten and the app tells you where it went, so the data can still be recovered.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Children

Laurel does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
