Common questions

Where are my achievements stored? On your device. Laurel doesn't use an account or a server, and no one else can see your data.

What does Laurel Pro include? Laurel Pro is a one‑time purchase that unlocks premium extras like custom accent colors, alternate app icons, and data export. Logging achievements is always free.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here? Open Settings in Laurel and tap "Restore Purchases."

How do I back up or move my data? Settings → "Export data" lets you save everything as JSON (a full backup) or CSV.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual achievements from each entry's menu, or remove everything in Settings → "Delete all data."
