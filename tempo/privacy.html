Tempo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Tempo is a time tracker built so your data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Tempo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Everything you track — sessions, titles, notes, categories, tags, and goals — is stored on your Mac. The developer has no access to it.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Tempo stores

Everything you create in Tempo is saved locally using Apple's on‑device database (SwiftData) in your app's Application Support folder. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or any server.

Apple capabilities Tempo uses

  • Notifications — if you turn on reminders, Tempo schedules local notifications on your Mac. They are generated on‑device and are not sent through any server.
  • Login Items (SMAppService) — if you enable "Launch at login," Tempo registers itself with macOS so it can start automatically. macOS manages this; no data leaves your Mac.
  • Idle detection — Tempo reads how long it has been since your last keyboard or mouse activity (a single system idle‑time value) to offer to discard idle minutes. It does not record what you type or click, and it never sees the contents of any input.
  • Global hotkey — Tempo registers a system hotkey (⌃⌥Space) to start/stop tracking. It does not read your keystrokes.

Tempo does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any session at any time in History.
  • You can erase everything in Settings → Reset → "Delete all data."
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your Mac.
  • You can export your data at any time as CSV or JSON.

Children

Tempo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
