Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your Mac, in the app's local database. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and the developer can't see it.

How do I start or stop tracking quickly? Use the menu bar stopwatch, or press the global hotkey ⌃⌥Space from anywhere.

What does "idle detection" do? If you walk away while tracking, Tempo notices you've been idle and offers to discard those minutes when you return. It only reads a single system idle‑time value — never what you type or click.

How do I get Tempo to launch automatically? Open Settings (⌘,) and turn on Launch at login.

How do I export my data? Open Settings → Data and choose Export CSV or Export JSON.

How do I delete everything? Open Settings → Reset and choose Delete all data.
