Common questions

What is ElementsWorld? ElementsWorld is an iPad app for kids ages 3–9 to play with ten everyday elements — water, air, fire, earth, ice, electricity, light, sound, metal, and plants — through hands-on touch scenes, then discover what happens when two elements combine in the Mix Lab.

How do I switch between English and Arabic? Open Settings (the gear icon on the Home garden) and pick a language under the Language section — the whole app, including layout direction, updates immediately. You can also choose a language on the last page of onboarding.

My child doesn't have headphones — is ElementsWorld quiet enough to use around others? Yes. Every sound, spoken word, and haptic is optional. Open Settings and turn off Sound effects, Narration, and Haptics independently; every scene still shows its full visual reaction, since sound and haptics are always decoration layered on top of the visuals, never required to understand what's happening.

What's the Facts Drawer's "Stay safe" note? Water, Fire, Ice, and Electricity each include a real, plain-language safety note (e.g. "never touch outlets with wet hands") alongside the fun facts — the play scenes themselves are safe, abstract depictions, but we want kids to also learn how to be careful around the real things.

How does Settings stay away from little hands? The gear icon opens a simple math question ("what's 6 × 7?") before Settings appears — easy for a grown-up, a real wall for a young child.

What's the Sticker Book for? The Collection tab shows a sticker for every element played and every Mix Lab combination discovered (46 total). It's just a friendly way to see progress — there are no scores, timers, or competition.

Does ElementsWorld need the internet, or collect any data about my child? No. ElementsWorld works fully offline and makes no network requests. It stores a few small preferences (played elements, discovered combos, language, appearance, sound/haptics/narration settings) only on the device. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

I found a bug, or something isn't working right — what do I do? Email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com with the element or screen name and what happened. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.
