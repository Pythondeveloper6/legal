ElementsWorld — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

ElementsWorld lets kids play with water, air, fire, earth, and six other everyday elements, and it's built to be trustworthy for parents by never leaving the device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

  • ElementsWorld does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, from children or adults. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to type.
  • ElementsWorld makes no network requests. It works fully offline and always has.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no trackers of any kind are included in the app.
  • Everything ElementsWorld remembers lives only in the app's local storage on your iPad, in Apple's standard UserDefaults mechanism, and never leaves it.

What ElementsWorld stores, and where

ElementsWorld saves a handful of small preferences on-device so the app remembers your child's progress between visits:

  • Which of the 10 elements have been played, and which Mix Lab combinations have been discovered (drives the Sticker Book)
  • The chosen language (English or Arabic)
  • The chosen appearance (light, dark, or system)
  • Whether narration, sound, and haptic feedback are turned on
  • Whether onboarding has been completed

All of this is stored using UserDefaults, Apple's standard on-device settings storage, scoped to ElementsWorld's own app sandbox. None of it is a name, email, photo, or any other personally identifiable information — it's simply which elements and combos your child has already discovered.

On-device services

A few features use built-in iOS services, entirely locally:

  • Spoken narration (AVSpeechSynthesizer) — tapping a fact in the Facts Drawer, the speaker on a Mix Lab result, or the speaker on a quiz riddle reads that text aloud using the system's built-in, on-device voices. This is text-to-speech only; no audio is recorded, no microphone is used, and nothing is uploaded. It can be switched off in Settings, and every one of those screens is fully usable with it off.
  • Haptic feedback and sound effects are synthesized on the device in real time; no audio files ship in the app and none are transmitted.

No internet, no cloud, no accounts

ElementsWorld does not make network requests, does not sync to iCloud, does not use HealthKit, and does not request your location, camera, photo library, or contacts. There is nothing to log into and nothing to lose if you're offline — the app doesn't know the difference.

Children's privacy

ElementsWorld is designed for children roughly ages 3–9 and is built accordingly: it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, and contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no external links a child could tap into, and no chat or user-generated content features. A parent-gated Settings screen (a simple math question) keeps young children from changing settings or leaving the app unsupervised.

The home-screen widget

ElementsWorld's optional "Element of the Day" widget shows one element per day, picked by a fixed formula from the date alone. It reads no progress, no identifiers, and nothing about your child, and it stores nothing of its own. Tapping it simply opens that element's play screen in the app.

Your control

Because nothing leaves the device, there is nothing to request, export, or delete from us. Inside the app, the parent-gated Settings screen has a Delete All Data button that clears everything ElementsWorld remembers about your child's play — every played element and every discovered Mix Lab combination — immediately and permanently. Deleting the app from your iPad also removes all of its local storage, preferences included.
