Trove — Support

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Trove is a home-inventory app for iPhone and iPad. It answers four questions fast: where is it, is it expiring or running low, what do I own, and how do I add something in seconds.

If anything below does not solve your problem, email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com and describe what happened, what you expected, your device and your iOS version. Every message is read.

Getting started

How do I add my first item? Tap the + button on the Home or Browse screen. Give the item a name, then add whatever you want to track: a photo, a location, a quantity, a value, a purchase or expiry date, tags, a low-stock threshold, or notes. Only the name is required.

What are locations? Locations are the places things live, and they nest: Home › Kitchen › Cabinet. Item counts and total value roll up through the tree, so you can ask "what is in the garage" and get an answer for the whole branch. Create them in Browse.

How do I find something? Use Search. It looks across item names, notes, tags and locations, and it has filters for what is expiring and what is low on stock.

Trove Plus

What is free? Up to 10 items, with 1 photo per item. Nested locations, search and filters, expiry and low-stock alerts, the Home Screen widget and private iCloud sync are all free, with no limits.

What does Trove Plus add? Unlimited items, unlimited photos per item, barcode scanning, receipt and label photos, CSV export, and the home value report.

What does it cost? Three options, all unlocking the same features:

  • Monthly — US$2.99 a month
  • Yearly — US$19.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers
  • Lifetime — US$49.99 once, no subscription

Prices outside the United States are the App Store's local equivalent. The app always shows the price you will actually be charged before you confirm.

Why am I not being offered the free trial? Apple allows one introductory offer per Apple Account per subscription group. Trove's monthly and yearly plans share one group, so if you have used the trial before — on either plan, on any device signed in to your Apple Account — Apple will not offer it again. The app asks the App Store about your eligibility and shows the trial only if you qualify. This is Apple's rule and the developer cannot override it.

How do I restore a purchase I already made? Sign in to the same Apple Account you bought with, then open Settings → Restore, or tap Restore at the bottom of the Trove Plus screen. It is free and you can do it as often as you like. Purchases shared with you through Family Sharing restore the same way.

If Restore does not find your purchase: check you are signed in to the correct Apple Account (iOS Settings → your name → Media & Purchases), make sure you have a connection, and try again. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them, not to the device.

How do I cancel? Cancellation is handled by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Trove → Cancel Subscription, or https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Trove Plus until the period you already paid for runs out.

What happens to my stuff when my subscription ends? Nothing is deleted and nothing is locked. Every item, photo, receipt and location you created stays on your device, stays readable, and stays exportable. Only the free-tier limits come back — you cannot add new items past the free limit, or use the Plus-only features, until you subscribe again.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled entirely by Apple, at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. The developer has no ability to issue one.

The paywall says "Pricing unavailable". That means the App Store could not return prices — usually no connection, or a temporary App Store outage. Nothing was charged. Close the screen, check your connection and try again.

Signing in

Do I need an account to use Trove? No. Trove has no accounts on any server and every single feature works signed out, including everything in Trove Plus. There is no sign-in wall anywhere.

Then why is Sign in with Apple there? Purely so you can attach a name to the app if you like having one. It is optional, it syncs nothing, and it unlocks nothing. Skipping it costs you no functionality at all.

Where does that information go? Into the Keychain on your device, and nowhere else. Trove has no network code, so it cannot transmit it. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete Account, inside the app. That erases the stored identifier, name and email from your device immediately.

Because Trove has no server, it cannot ask Apple to revoke the sign-in on your behalf — that requires a backend, and Trove has none. To also clear Apple's own record, go to iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Trove → Stop Using Apple Account.

Deleting your account does not touch your inventory. If you want that gone too, use Settings → Data → Delete all items & locations.

Sync and backup

How does iCloud sync work? If iCloud is on, Trove syncs through your own private iCloud database. The developer has no access to it. Sync is automatic; there is nothing to configure.

My other device is not showing the same items. Check, in order:

  1. Both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account — iOS Settings → your name.
  2. iCloud Drive is on — iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.
  3. Trove is allowed to use iCloud — iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud.
  4. Both devices have a working connection.
  5. Open Trove on both devices and leave it in the foreground for a moment. Trove syncs when the app launches and when it comes back to the foreground, so a device that has not been opened will not be up to date yet.

Large photo libraries take longer on a first sync. If a device still looks stale after a few minutes with the app open, try quitting and relaunching Trove on both.

Can I back up or export my inventory? Yes, with Trove Plus: Settings → Data → Export as CSV produces a file you can save or share anywhere. The export is yours and stays readable if you ever stop subscribing.

What happens if I delete the app? The local database goes with it. If iCloud sync was on, your data is still in your own iCloud account and comes back when you reinstall and sign in to the same Apple Account.

Reminders

How do expiry reminders work? Trove schedules a local notification a set number of days before an item's expiry date. Change the lead time in Settings → Reminders. Low-stock alerts fire when an item's quantity drops to or below the threshold you set.

I am not getting notifications. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Trove and make sure notifications are allowed. Also confirm the item actually has an expiry date, that the date is far enough in the future for the lead time to still be ahead of it, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not suppressing them.

Reminders are entirely on-device. Trove has no push notification service, so nothing is sent from or to a server.

Data and privacy

Where is my inventory stored? On your device, and in your own private iCloud account if iCloud is on. It is never sent to the developer. There are no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no third-party SDKs anywhere in the app.

How do I delete everything? Individual items and locations: swipe, or open and delete. Everything at once: Settings → Data → Delete all items & locations.
