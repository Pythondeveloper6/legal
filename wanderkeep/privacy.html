Wanderkeep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Wanderkeep is built so your health and activity data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Wanderkeep does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your activity data (steps, active energy, distance, and workouts) is read from Apple Health on your device and never leaves it. The developer has no access to it.
  • Your kingdom — buildings, resources, streaks, and settings — is stored on your device. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

Health data

Wanderkeep asks permission to read your steps, active energy, walking/running distance, and workouts from Apple Health. This data is used only to grow your in‑app kingdom and to draw your activity charts. It is processed on your device and is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared. Wanderkeep does not write any data back to Health.

You can review or revoke Wanderkeep's Health access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

What Wanderkeep stores

Your kingdom (placed buildings, resources, daily activity snapshots, streak, and your preferences) is saved locally on your device using Apple's on‑device database. This data is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services Wanderkeep relies on

  • HealthKit — to read the activity metrics that grow your kingdom, on your device.

Wanderkeep does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data, and there are no accounts, ads, or trackers.

Your control

  • You can remove everything Wanderkeep has stored at any time inside the app (Settings → "Delete all data").
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.
  • You can revoke Health permissions at any time in the Health app.

Children

Wanderkeep does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
