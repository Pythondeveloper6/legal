Common questions

How does my kingdom grow? Wanderkeep reads your daily movement from Apple Health. Steps become wood, active energy becomes coins, distance reveals new land, and workouts earn gems. Hit your daily goal and the village auto‑grows a new building. Rest days never cause decay.

Where is my data stored? Everything stays on your device. Your activity is read from Apple Health and your kingdom is saved locally. Nothing is uploaded and no one else can see it.

Wanderkeep isn't reading my steps — what do I do? Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Wanderkeep and make sure Steps, Active Energy, Distance, and Workouts are turned on.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in Wanderkeep and tap "Delete all data" to reset your kingdom. Deleting the app also removes its local data.
