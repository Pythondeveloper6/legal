Doodly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Doodly is a kids' learning app, and it's built so a child's activity never leaves the family's device. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

  • Doodly does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data, from children or from parents.
  • Every child profile, progress record, drawing, and colored page is stored only on the device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData database.
  • Doodly works completely offline. It makes no network requests at all.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers or SDKs.

What Doodly stores, and where

When a parent creates a child profile, Doodly saves locally on the device:

  • The child's name, chosen avatar, and birth year (used only to pick age-appropriate content)
  • An optional daily session time limit
  • Progress: which activities and items a child has completed, and the stars and stickers they've earned
  • Drawings made in Draw and pages finished in Color, saved to the in-app Gallery
  • Parent-facing settings such as speech language mode and sound on/off, stored in UserDefaults

None of this is ever sent off the device — there is no server, no cloud sync, and no CloudKit account involved. Doodly does not read or request access to contacts, location, camera, photo library, or microphone.

On-device speech

Doodly speaks item names and praise in English and Arabic using Apple's built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer. This runs entirely on the device; no audio or text is sent anywhere.

Parent gate

The gear icon leading to the parent area is protected by a simple one-digit multiplication check. This is a light deterrent to keep a young child from wandering into settings, not an account or identity system — Doodly still collects nothing.

How long it's kept (retention)

Doodly keeps what it saves until you remove it. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing expires on a schedule of ours:

  • Reset Progress (Parent Area → Children) clears one child's completed items, stars, and stickers, and keeps their artwork.
  • Delete Child (Parent Area → edit a child) permanently removes that child's profile together with all of their progress, stickers, artwork, and session history.
  • Delete All Data (Parent Area → Privacy & Data) empties the app: every child profile and everything tied to them, including the app's memory of which activity each child last opened.
  • Deleting the app removes the entire on-device database with it.

Session history (how many minutes a child used the app on a given day) is shown to parents for the last 30 days and is deleted with the child it belongs to.

Your control

  • Parents can edit or delete a child profile, and everything tied to it (progress, stars, stickers, gallery items), from the parent area at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from the device.

Children

Doodly is designed for children ages 1–10 to use directly, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children — nothing is ever transmitted off the device, so there is nothing to collect.
