Common questions

Does Doodly need an internet connection? No. Doodly works completely offline — everything runs on the device.

Can more than one child use Doodly? Yes. Add a profile for each child from the child picker (or the parent area), with their own avatar and birth year. Each child has separate progress, stars, stickers, and gallery.

How does content unlock? Content is grouped into age bands based on a child's birth year. Within a band, a few items are open at a time and more unlock as earlier ones are completed — there's always something new without everything appearing at once. A parent can override a child's band from the parent area if needed.

How do I reach the parent area? Tap the small gear icon on the home screen and answer the one-digit multiplication check. From there you can manage child profiles, set a daily session time limit, choose the speech language mode, and turn sounds on or off.

What happens when my child gets something wrong? Nothing negative — Doodly has no fail states a child can reach. A wrong tap just wiggles gently and the activity stays open for another try.

Where are my child's drawings saved? Drawings and finished coloring pages are saved automatically to the Gallery, stored only on the device.

How do I delete a child's data? Open the parent area, select the child, and delete their profile — this removes their progress, stars, stickers, and gallery items from the device.
