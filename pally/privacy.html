Pally — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Pally is built so that everything it does stays on your Mac. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Pally does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your configuration — profiles, the actions on your overlay, your macros, themes, and usage history — is stored only on your Mac.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third‑party trackers. Pally has no servers and makes no network connections of its own.

What Pally stores, and where

Everything you set up in Pally is written to a single configuration file in your account's Application Support folder:

~/Library/Application Support/Pally/

This includes your profiles, the actions pinned to each overlay shape, your custom macros, your theme, sound preferences, and a local log of which actions you ran (used only to power the in‑app Insights tab). This data never leaves your Mac. The developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Automation, Accessibility, and system permissions

Pally controls your Mac the same way you would — but to do so it needs macOS permissions you grant explicitly in System Settings → Privacy & Security:

  • Accessibility — lets macros send synthetic keystrokes (for example, triggering Mission Control or Show Desktop).
  • Automation (Apple Events) — lets actions tell apps like System Events, Finder, and Shortcuts to do things (toggle Dark Mode, empty the Trash, run a Shortcut).
  • Screen Recording — used only by the interactive Screenshot action.

These permissions are used entirely on your Mac, in real time, to perform the action you asked for. Pally does not record your keystrokes, read your screen, log what other apps contain, or send any of this anywhere. You can review or revoke each permission at any time in System Settings, and Pally surfaces a clear prompt if a permission it needs is missing.

Custom shell and AppleScript actions

Pally lets you build your own actions, including ones that run a shell command or an AppleScript. These run locally with your own user privileges, exactly as if you'd typed them yourself in Terminal. You are responsible for the commands you add. Pally does not transmit them or their output anywhere.

Your control

  • Remove individual actions, macros, or profiles at any time in Settings.
  • Use Settings → About → Reset all settings to erase your entire Pally configuration and return to a clean state.
  • Deleting the app and the ~/Library/Application Support/Pally/ folder removes all of its data from your Mac.

Children

Pally does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
