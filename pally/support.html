Common questions

What is Pally? A small menu‑bar app that puts an always‑on‑top overlay of quick actions one shortcut away — launch apps, toggle system settings, fire media keys, and run your own macros.

How do I open the overlay? Press the global shortcut (⌘ ⌥ Space by default), or use the menu‑bar icon. Open Settings… from the menu‑bar icon to customize your actions.

Why does Pally ask for Accessibility / Automation / Screen Recording? Those macOS permissions are what let Pally control your Mac on your behalf — sending keystrokes, telling other apps to do things, and capturing an interactive screenshot. Pally only asks for what the actions you've added actually need, and it never sends any of this data anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details. You can grant or revoke each permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security, and Pally's Settings → Permissions tab links straight to each one.

A permission stopped working. macOS sometimes resets permissions after an update. Open Settings → Permissions, press Refresh status, and re‑grant anything shown as missing.

Why isn't Pally on the Mac App Store? Pally controls other apps and your system (Accessibility + Apple Events), which the App Store sandbox forbids. So it's distributed directly as a notarized, Developer ID–signed app.

How do I start over? Open Settings → About → Reset all settings to erase your configuration and return to a clean state.
