Common questions

What is Sky World? A bilingual English-and-Arabic sky and weather explorer for children. Adjust the Live Sky, discover clouds, storms, seasons, rainbows and auroras, and hear facts read aloud.

Does it need the internet? No. Sky World works entirely offline, including its animations, facts, narration and Home Screen widget.

Does it collect information or show ads? No. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, purchases, or network requests. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change language, sound, or appearance? Tap the gear button, complete the parent gate, then open Settings. You can select English, Arabic, or your device language, and change narration, sound effects and appearance.

Can it read facts aloud? Yes. Narration uses the voices installed on your Apple device. Turn it on or off in Settings.

What does the widget do? The Home Screen widget shows the day's featured sky topic. Tap it to open that topic in Sky World.

How do I remove local progress? Deleting the app removes its local data from the device.
