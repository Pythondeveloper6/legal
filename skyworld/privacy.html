Sky World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Sky World is made for children and is designed to collect nothing about them. This policy explains exactly what the app stores on a device and what it never does.

The short version

  • Sky World does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.
  • There are no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, trackers, third-party SDKs, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
  • The app makes no network requests. Weather scenes, facts, sound effects and translations are bundled in the download.
  • Only preferences and exploration progress are saved locally on the device.

What is stored on the device

  • Settings: selected language, appearance, narration and sound-effect preferences, and whether onboarding has been completed.
  • Exploration progress: identifiers of weather topics viewed, favourite topics, a local daily-sky streak and the last local day used for that feature. These are app catalogue identifiers and settings, not names, contact details, recordings, location, or other personal information.
  • This information is stored only in the app's local UserDefaults. It is never sent to the developer or any other party. It may be included in your own device backup under Apple's backup settings.

Permissions and audio

Sky World does not request or access the camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, health data, notifications, advertising identifier, or any other privacy-sensitive permission. When narration is enabled, Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer reads bundled facts aloud; Sky World does not upload text or audio to create narration.

Children

Sky World is intended for children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Settings are behind a simple parent gate, but the app does not ask for or store a password, name, age, email address, or payment information.

Your control

You can change settings inside the app. Deleting Sky World removes its local app data from that device. Because the developer receives no data, there is no remotely held data to access, correct, or delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated with the corresponding release. Questions about privacy can be sent to apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com.
