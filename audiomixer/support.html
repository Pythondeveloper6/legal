Common questions

Why does AudioMixer ask for Microphone access? macOS requires Microphone permission before any app can read another app's audio stream. AudioMixer uses that mechanism (Core Audio process taps) only to adjust each app's volume. It never records, stores, or transmits any audio.

An app isn't showing up — why? Only apps that are currently producing (or have recently produced) audio appear in the list. Start playback in the app and it will appear. Turn on Debug in the footer to see every audio process macOS reports.

Where are my volume settings stored? Locally on your Mac, in the app's preferences. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I start fresh? Open Settings (⌘,) and choose Reset all volumes.

Does it launch at startup? Optionally. Turn on Launch at login in Settings.
