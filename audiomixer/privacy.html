AudioMixer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

AudioMixer is a menu-bar utility that gives each app its own volume slider. This policy explains exactly what it does — and the many things it deliberately does not do — with your information.

The short version

  • AudioMixer does not record, save, or transmit any audio. Ever.
  • AudioMixer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Everything happens on your Mac. There are no servers, no accounts, and no network connection of any kind.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

About the microphone permission

macOS asks for Microphone permission before any app is allowed to read another app's audio stream — which is the mechanism AudioMixer uses (Core Audio process taps) to adjust per-app volume. Granting this permission lets AudioMixer attach a volume control to each app's audio.

AudioMixer never records, stores, listens to, or transmits that audio. It only applies a gain (a volume multiplier) on its way to your speakers or headphones. The microphone prompt is a side effect of how macOS gates this capability; it does not mean the app is using your microphone.

What AudioMixer stores

The only thing AudioMixer saves is your preferences — the per-app volume levels, mute states, and chosen output devices — kept locally in macOS UserDefaults on your Mac. This never leaves your computer.

What AudioMixer does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not read files, contacts, location, or your photo library.
  • It does not include any non-Apple SDKs.

Your control

  • Reset every saved volume and preference at any time from Settings → Reset all volumes.
  • Removing the app deletes its preferences along with it.

Children

AudioMixer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
