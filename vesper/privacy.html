Vesper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Vesper is a science-based sleep companion built so your health data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Vesper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your sleep and health data is read from Apple Health and analyzed entirely on your device. The developer has no access to it.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Health data

With your permission, Vesper reads sleep and overnight vitals from Apple Health — including sleep stages, heart rate, heart-rate variability (HRV), respiratory rate, and wrist temperature — plus optional daytime signals you choose to enable (such as time in daylight, activity and workouts, mindful minutes, caffeine and alcohol, and cycle phase).

This data is used only on your device to calculate your sleep score, trends, and coaching. Vesper never sends your health data off the device. Apple Health access is controlled by you in the Health app and the iOS Settings app, where you can revoke it at any time.

Vesper does not write data back to Apple Health.

What Vesper stores

The notes and factors you log in the Journal, plus your analysis cache and preferences, are saved locally on your device using Apple's on-device database. This data stays on your device and is controlled by you.

Apple services Vesper relies on

  • HealthKit — to read the sleep and vitals data you authorize, on your device.
  • Notifications — if you enable reminders, wind-down and wake nudges are scheduled locally on your device.

Vesper does not include any non-Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can disable any optional data source in Settings → Data sources.
  • You can delete everything Vesper has stored at any time in Settings → "Delete all Vesper data."
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Your Apple Health data is unaffected and remains in the Health app.

Not a medical device

Vesper is informational and educational. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Talk to a qualified professional about any health concern.

Children

Vesper does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
