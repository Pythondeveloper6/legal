Common questions

Where does my sleep data come from? Vesper reads sleep and overnight vitals from Apple Health, with your permission. It analyzes them on your device. You can grant or revoke access in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Does my health data leave my device? No. Your health data is analyzed entirely on device and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Why don't I see any data? Vesper needs sleep data recorded in Apple Health (for example from Apple Watch or a compatible tracker), and Health permission granted. Give it a night or two of recorded sleep, then reopen the app.

Is Vesper a medical device? No. Vesper is informational and educational only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in Vesper and tap "Delete all Vesper data." This clears everything the app has stored on your device. Your Apple Health data is unaffected.
