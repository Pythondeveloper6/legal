4. Accuracy of the estimates

Incline shows the equation behind each figure precisely because the figure is an estimate. You should read every number in the app with that in mind.

  • Distance, elevation gain, and pace are computed from the speed and incline you enter. If your treadmill's display is mis-calibrated, or if what you enter does not match what the belt is doing, Incline's figures will be wrong in the same way.
  • Active-energy figures are model estimates for a group, not measurements of you. Real individual energy cost varies with efficiency, gait, fitness, temperature, footwear, handrail use, and much else. Each published equation carries its own reported error, which Incline shows.
  • Each equation has a validated speed and grade range. When your session falls outside it, Incline marks the result as lower-confidence and says so, rather than silently substituting a different model.
  • Estimated active energy excludes resting and basal energy. It is not the same as, and will not match, a figure from any device that measures heart rate or gas exchange.
  • Incline is not a replacement for a treadmill's own instrumentation, a heart-rate monitor, or laboratory measurement.

The equations Incline uses are cited in full in the app, with author, journal, DOI, and PubMed identifier. We may correct or update a model; when we do, past sessions keep the provenance they were recorded with, so an old figure is never silently re-attributed.
