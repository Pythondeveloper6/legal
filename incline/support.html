What Incline is

Incline is a companion for manual treadmill sessions. You tell it the speed and incline you have set on the belt; it records the interval profile as you change them, and estimates distance, elevation gain, pace, and active energy from published treadmill equations. It does not connect to your treadmill, and it does not need to.

Getting started

  1. Open Incline and finish the short first-run flow: choose metric or imperial, and enter your body weight (this scales the energy estimate, which is published per kilogram of body mass).
  2. Tap Start on the Today tab.
  3. Set the belt on your treadmill, then set the same speed and incline in Incline with the large one-hand controls.
  4. Every time you change either one, Incline closes the current interval and opens a new one. That is what builds the terrain ribbon.
  5. Tap Finish when you are done to see the summary and save the session.

Frequently asked

Does Incline connect to my treadmill?

No. This version has no Bluetooth or FTMS connection. You enter speed and incline yourself, which means it works with any treadmill, including one with no smart features at all.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Incline is an iPhone app and does not require a Watch. It does not measure heart rate.

Where do the calorie numbers come from?

From two named, peer-reviewed equations, applied to the speed, incline, time, and body weight you entered:

SpeedModel
Up to 7.0 km/hLankford et al. (2020), Eur J Appl Physiol — PMID 32656608, DOI 10.1007/s00421-020-04428-z
From 7.6 km/hLooney et al. (2025) RE3, Eur J Appl Physiol — PMID 41057734, DOI 10.1007/s00421-025-05999-5, with published correction DOI 10.1007/s00421-026-06235-4
Between the twoA continuous blend of both published rates, evaluated at the same speed and grade

The full detail — coefficients, validated ranges, reported error, and the model version — is in the app under Settings → Science and sources, and every saved session records which model produced its figures.

Why does a session say “outside the validated range”?

Each published equation was validated over a specific speed and grade range. If your session went beyond it, Incline still evaluates the equation, but marks the result as lower-confidence and tells you so, instead of quietly swapping in a different model to make the number look confident. That warning is part of the app and is never hidden behind a subscription.

Why is my calorie figure different from my treadmill's / my Watch's / another app's?

Because they are answering different questions with different methods. Incline reports active energy only — it excludes the resting energy your body would have used anyway — and estimates it from published equations rather than from heart rate or motion sensors. Treadmill consoles often include resting energy and rarely disclose their formula. All of these are estimates; Incline's contribution is telling you exactly which estimate it made and how uncertain it is.

The estimate feels consistently high or low for me.

Use Settings → Calorie calibration. It scales the model estimate between 70% and 130% in 5% steps, and the value in effect is stored with each session so past figures stay reproducible. This is a personal preference control, not a measurement — Incline cannot tell you the “correct” number for your body.

What are the input limits?

  • Speed: 0–30 km/h
  • Incline: 0–20% grade
  • Body weight: 20–300 kg (44.1–661.4 lb)
  • Calorie calibration: 70%–130% of the model estimate

Does time spent paused count?

No. Paused time contributes no distance, no elevation, and no energy. Speed zero contributes no energy either — Incline reports the model as unavailable rather than inventing a positive number.

Apple Health

What does Incline write to Apple Health?

With your permission, and with an active Incline Plus subscription, one indoor running workout per finished session, plus that session's active energy and walking + running distance. It never writes resting or basal energy, and never changes data written by another app.

Will Apple Fitness show my incline intervals?

No. Apple Fitness and the Health app display standard workout data — duration, distance, active energy. The interval profile, the terrain ribbon, and the model provenance are Incline's own record and live in Incline.

The Health permission prompt is not appearing again.

iOS shows the Health permission sheet once. Once you have answered it, Incline reports the decision rather than asking again, so retrying can never turn into repeated prompting. To change it: open the Health app → your profile → Privacy → Apps → Incline. Settings → Apple Health in Incline shows the real current status and a link to get there.

A session did not reach Apple Health.

The session is still safely saved in Incline, with every figure intact — the Health write is a separate step. Open the session in History and retry. Retrying a session that Health already accepted does nothing, so you cannot create a duplicate workout by trying again.

How do I delete something Incline wrote to Apple Health?

In the Health app. Incline deliberately requests no permission to read your workouts, which means it cannot see or delete them — not even its own. Deleting a session inside Incline removes it from Incline only.

Incline Plus

What do I get?

Unlimited saved sessions (the free plan saves 8 finished sessions per calendar month) and the Apple Health write. US$9.99 per year, with a 14-day free trial for eligible Apple Accounts. Everything else — live workouts, all your history, all Insights, and all of the science and confidence information — is free and always will be.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Incline Plus → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I subscribed but Incline says I am on the free plan.

Open Incline Plus in Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If Incline simply cannot reach the App Store, it keeps the last status it saw and does not lock you out.

I want a refund.

All purchases go through Apple, so refunds are Apple's to give. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my sessions if I stop subscribing?

Nothing is removed. Every session you already saved stays saved and fully readable. The monthly cap applies again to new sessions, and new sessions are no longer written to Apple Health.

Your data

Incline stores everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no third-party SDK — the app has no networking code of its own. Deleting a session in History removes it permanently; deleting the app removes everything Incline stored. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem with the science

If you believe a coefficient, a unit conversion, a validated range, or a citation in Incline is wrong, please tell us and cite the source. Getting these right is the point of the app, and a correction will be shipped rather than argued with. Email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Apple Health is optional. No internet connection is needed to record or review a workout; a connection is needed only to buy or restore a subscription.

Health and safety

Incline is not a medical device and gives no medical or training advice. Its figures are estimates from published equations, not measurements of your body. Consult a physician before starting or changing an exercise programme, and stop and seek help if you feel unwell. See the Terms of Use.
