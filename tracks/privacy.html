Tracks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Tracks is built so your work stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Tracks does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your projects, task cards, checklists, notes, details, tags, and calendar events are stored on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers, and Tracks has no accounts to sign up for.

What Tracks stores

Everything you create in Tracks (projects, cards, checklist items, notes, details, tags, and calendar events) is saved locally using Apple’s on‑device database. The data is held in an app group container so the Tracks home‑screen widget can show your tasks. It stays on your device and is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services Tracks relies on

  • Notifications — if you turn on a reminder for a task, Tracks asks the system to show a local notification at the time you choose. Reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server.
  • WidgetKit — the optional home‑screen widget reads your tasks from the same on‑device store.

Tracks does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data. Tracks does not use location, your photo library, your contacts, your microphone, or your camera.

Your control

  • You can delete any project, task, or event at any time inside the app.
  • You can remove everything at once in Settings → “Delete all data.”
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Tracks does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
