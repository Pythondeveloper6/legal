Common questions

Where are my tasks stored? On your device. Tracks has no accounts and no servers — your projects, cards, and events never leave your device, except where you choose to back them up yourself.

How much does Tracks cost? Tracks is free. There’s no subscription and no in‑app purchase.

How do reminders work? Turn on “Remind me” for a task with a due date, and Tracks schedules a local notification ahead of time. You can set the lead time in Settings.

There’s a widget? Yes — add the Tracks widget to your home screen to see your tasks at a glance.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual projects, tasks, or events inside the app, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all data.”
