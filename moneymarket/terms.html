About the money in the app

Money Market teaches Egyptian pounds and US dollars. Every coin and note in it is drawn by the app, in code, at the moment it appears — there are no image assets of currency anywhere in the product. Each piece carries only its own value on a plain disc or rounded rectangle: no portrait, no issuing authority, no serial number, no signature, no security features, and colours chosen for legibility rather than to match the real article. Nothing in the app is, or is intended to be, a reproduction or facsimile of real currency, and nothing in it is legal tender or has any monetary value.

The app is an educational toy. It teaches how coins and notes add up and how change is made; it is not financial advice, and denominations in circulation can change without the app changing with them.
