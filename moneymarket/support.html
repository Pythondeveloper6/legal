Common questions

What is Money Market? Money Market is a pretend market stall for children roughly ages 4–8, on iPhone and iPad. It teaches real money: what each coin and note is worth, how to add money up, how to pay for something, and how to give change. There are four things to do — Meet the Money (free play, tap a piece to hear its name and value), Count the Money, Pay the Shopkeeper, and Make Change — plus a Money Book that fills in as your child meets each coin and note.

What does it cost? Nothing. Money Market is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads. Everything in the app is available from the moment it is installed.

Which money does it teach? Two real currencies: Egyptian pounds (25pt, 50pt and £1 coins; £5 to £100 notes) and US dollars (1¢, 5¢, 10¢ and 25¢ coins; $1 to $20 notes). Pick one in Settings → Currency; the whole app, including the Money Book and the widget, switches to it. Changing currency doesn't erase anything.

How do I switch between English and Arabic? Settings → Language. The whole app changes, including the layout direction — Arabic is fully right-to-left, not just translated. You can also pick a language during onboarding on first launch.

Why does the app ask me a multiplication question? That's the parent gate. Settings, the progress report, adding or deleting a child, "delete all data", and every link that leaves the app are behind it, so a child can't change your setup or wander out of the app on their own. It's required for apps in the Kids Category. Answer wrong and you get a fresh question, so it can't be guessed by repetition.

How do I add another child? Settings → parent gate → Manage children. Each child gets a name and an emoji avatar, and each child's stars, Money Book and open levels are kept separately — so a younger sibling can't reset an older one's progress. On a very first run, before any child exists, adding the first one is not gated; everything after that is.

Where do the stars come from, and how do levels unlock? Every finished round earns one, two or three stars depending on how it went, and a star can never be taken away. Each graded mode has three tiers, and the next tier opens once your child has earned 9 stars in the one before it. If you'd rather skip the wait, Settings → parent gate → turn on "unlock all levels".

Is there a way to lose? No. There's no timer, no score shown to the child, and no fail state. A wrong answer just gets a gentle nudge and another try, and after the answer the app draws the sum out — the pieces, the operators, the total — so a child who guessed right still sees why.

What's in the parent report? Settings → parent gate → the report: stars earned, rounds played, the share of rounds finished with no mistakes, which levels are open, and how much of the Money Book is filled in — for the child who is currently selected. Every number is worked out on your device from that child's own saved progress. It lives behind the gate on purpose: it's written about a child, never shown to them.

What is the widget? A small or medium Home Screen widget showing a "coin of the day" — one coin or note, chosen from the calendar date, in your chosen currency and language. Tap it and the app opens on the wallet. Add it the usual way: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, and find Money Market.

My widget is showing the wrong currency or language. The widget follows what you picked in Settings. If it hasn't caught up, open the app once and change back to the Home Screen — that nudges the widget to redraw. If it is still showing Egyptian pounds in English after that, it means the widget could not read the app's settings on your device; the app itself is unaffected, and we'd like to hear about it at the address above.

Can I turn the voice off? What about the sounds? Yes, independently. Settings → Sound: turn the narrator off, or leave it on and slide it slower or faster; turn sound effects off; turn haptics off. Every round works perfectly in silence — the visuals never depend on hearing anything.

Why doesn't it speak Arabic on my device? The narrator uses the voices installed on your iPhone or iPad. If an Arabic voice isn't installed, the app falls back to English rather than going silent. You can add one in the device's own Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. All of the app's Arabic text is there regardless.

Does Money Market need the internet, or collect any data about my child? No, on both counts. The app makes no network requests at all — there is no networking code in it — and it works completely offline. Everything it remembers (child profiles, stars, the Money Book, settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDK, and no server for anything to go to. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

How do I delete my child's data? Settings → parent gate → Manage children removes one child and their progress. Settings → parent gate → Delete all data removes every child, every star and the Money Book, and returns the app to its first-run state. Deleting the app removes everything too. There is no copy anywhere else to ask us about.

Does progress move to a new iPhone or iPad? No — deliberately. Money Market has no cloud sync and no account, which is the same decision that keeps your child's name off every server in the world. Stars live on the device that earned them.

I found a bug — what do I do? Email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com with what you were doing (which mode, which currency, which language) and what happened. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.
