Cadence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Cadence is built so your training data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Cadence does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your sessions, goals, achievements, and profile are stored on your device.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.
  • There are no accounts — you never sign up or log in.

What Cadence stores

Everything you create in Cadence — logged cycling and treadmill sessions, weekly goals, unlocked achievements, XP, and your profile (name, age, weight) — is saved locally using Apple’s on‑device database (SwiftData). This data lives on your device and is controlled by you. The developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Cadence reads your indoor cycling and treadmill running/walking workouts, and the health data that powers its live metrics and zones, so it can show and import your sessions. Cadence can also save sessions you complete back to Health. This happens only with your explicit permission, which you can review or revoke at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Specifically, Cadence may read:

  • Heart rate
  • Active energy burned
  • Walking + running distance
  • Step count
  • Running speed
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart‑rate recovery (one‑minute)

and may write:

  • Cycling and running/walking workouts
  • Heart rate
  • Active energy burned
  • Walking + running distance

Cadence reads only what it needs to display, log, and calculate your sessions — including its incline‑aware calorie and pace calculations for treadmill sessions — and never sends Health data anywhere off your device.

Apple services Cadence relies on

  • HealthKit — to read your cycling and treadmill workouts, heart rate, and related metrics, and to save sessions you complete, all within your own device and Apple Health.
  • WatchConnectivity — to stream live heart rate, zones, and other live metrics from your Apple Watch to your iPhone or iPad during a session. This stays between your own paired devices.

Cadence does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any session, or all sessions, at any time inside the app (Settings → “Delete all sessions”).
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.
  • Apple Health access can be turned off at any time in the Health app.

Children

Cadence does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
