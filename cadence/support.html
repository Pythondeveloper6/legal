Common questions

Where are my sessions stored? On your device. Cadence has no accounts and no servers — your training data never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to save sessions to Apple Health.

How do I track heart rate during a session? Connect Apple Health in Settings, then start a ride or a run. With an Apple Watch, Cadence streams your live heart rate and zones to your iPhone or iPad while you train.

Do I need an Apple Watch? No. You can log sessions manually or import cycling and treadmill workouts from Apple Health. An Apple Watch adds live heart‑rate tracking during a session.

Why do I have to enter speed and incline myself on the treadmill? Cadence doesn't connect to your treadmill's console directly, so it has no other way to know what the belt is doing. Entering speed and incline on the console strip — with quick steppers and editable presets — is what lets Cadence calculate your exact distance, pace, elevation gain, and an incline‑aware calorie estimate instead of guessing from your heart rate alone.

Why does Cadence's treadmill distance differ from my treadmill console or my Apple Watch? Cadence trusts different sources for different things. Distance, pace, and elevation gain come from the speed and incline you log — which is why they can differ from your console if its display isn't perfectly calibrated, or from your Watch, whose wrist‑based distance is known to drift indoors (especially if you hold the handrails). Heart rate and zones always come from your Watch. The session detail screen shows both the console‑derived and Watch‑derived figures so you can see the gap yourself.

What happens if I forget to log a speed or incline change? Cadence falls back to your Apple Watch's distance and calories for that session and reports no elevation gain, since elevation gain can only be calculated from a logged incline. Nothing is lost — logging a change later in the session still improves the rest of it.

Is Cadence free? Yes. There’s no subscription and no in‑app purchase.

How do I delete my data? Delete an individual session from its detail screen, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all sessions.”
