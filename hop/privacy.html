Hop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Hop shares one keyboard and mouse across the computers on your desk. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

The short version

  • Hop does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Hop has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Keyboard and mouse activity is relayed only to the peer computers you pair with, directly over your local network — never to the developer or any third party.

How Hop works

Hop discovers other Hop computers on your local network using Bonjour, and lets you pair with them. Once paired, the computer acting as the controller relays keyboard and mouse events to the peer your pointer is currently on. This relay happens only between the machines you have explicitly paired, over your LAN, using the bundled Input Leap engine. Traffic can be secured with TLS between peers.

Hop never sends your keystrokes, mouse movement, clipboard, or any other input to the developer or to any remote service. There is no cloud component.

What Hop stores

Hop keeps a small amount of configuration on your device using the standard macOS preferences store: your chosen mode (controller or peer), screen-arrangement settings, and whether Hop launches at login. That's it. This never leaves your Mac.

System permissions Hop needs

To share input, macOS requires you to grant Hop two permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security:

  • Accessibility — so Hop can move the pointer and synthesize key presses on peers.
  • Input Monitoring — so Hop can read keyboard and mouse events to relay them.
  • Local Network — so Hop can discover and connect to peers on your LAN.

These permissions are used solely to share input between the computers you pair. Hop does not log, store, or transmit what you type beyond the live relay to the active peer.

Your control

  • You can change or remove pairings at any time in Hop.
  • You can revoke Accessibility, Input Monitoring, or Local Network access at any time in System Settings.
  • "Reset Hop" in Settings clears all of Hop's stored preferences.
  • Deleting the app removes its local preferences from your device.

Children

Hop does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
