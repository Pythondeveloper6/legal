Common questions

What does Hop do? Hop lets you use one keyboard and mouse across several computers on the same network — move your pointer to the edge of the screen and it "hops" to the next machine. It works across Mac, Windows, and Linux peers.

Why does Hop ask for Accessibility and Input Monitoring permissions? macOS requires those two permissions before any app can read keyboard/mouse events and control the pointer. Hop uses them only to relay input between the computers you pair. Grant them in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Where does my typing go? Only to the peer your pointer is currently on, directly over your local network. Nothing is sent to a server or to the developer. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Hop can't find my other computer. Make sure both machines are on the same local network, that Hop (or the Hop companion) is running on the other machine, and that Hop has Local Network permission. Some networks block Bonjour/multicast — a normal home or office LAN works best.

How do I stop Hop from launching at login? Open Settings (⌘,) → General and turn off "Launch Hop at login."

How do I reset everything? Settings (⌘,) → About → "Reset Hop" clears all of Hop's stored preferences.
