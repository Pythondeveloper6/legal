Price and Orbit Plus

Orbit is free to download, and the free app stays free. The full 3D solar system, every planet, moon and surface page, the quiz, the explorer passport, badges, the fact of the day, and four activities — Rocket Mission, Gravity Drop, Seasons, and Bedtime calm mode — along with all settings, English and Arabic with right-to-left layout, the accessibility behaviour, and the Session Time play limit are included at no cost and are not removed later.

Orbit Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription that unlocks the nine Explorer Tools: Compare sizes & distances, Real scale, Time machine, Orbit Race, Fit Inside, Moon phases, Telescope, the Build-your-own Sandbox, and AR "See it in your room" room mode.

  • Price — US$3.99 per month.
  • Free trial — new subscribers get 14 days free. Unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it converts automatically into a paid month.
  • Auto-renewal — the subscription renews each month until you cancel it.
  • Cancelling — cancel any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
  • Payment — purchases are processed by Apple and billed to your Apple Account. Orbit never sees or stores your payment details.
  • Where it lives — the subscription screen sits behind the grown-ups' math check, so a child cannot reach a price on their own.

If a subscription ends or lapses, the Explorer Tools lock again, but nothing your child has already earned — visited worlds, quiz scores, passport stamps, badges — is taken away, and the whole free app keeps working.

Intended audience

Orbit is designed for children roughly ages 6–12, typically used with a parent or guardian nearby. It contains no ads, no chat or messaging, no way to enter personal information, and no links to external websites or stores. The only purchase in Orbit is the optional Orbit Plus subscription described above, which is presented by Apple behind the grown-ups' math check.
