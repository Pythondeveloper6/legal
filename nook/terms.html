Nook Plus and Nook Pro Lifetime

Nook offers a free tier and two ways to go further, described here as they actually work in the app and in the App Store:

Free tier. Up to 15 items, up to 8 places, and 1 photo per item. Search, tags, places, and the widget are never limited.

Nook Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, offered in one subscription group ("Nook Plus") with two duration options:

  • Nook Plus Monthly (com.nook.app.plus.monthly) — billed monthly.
  • Nook Plus Yearly (com.nook.app.plus.yearly) — billed annually, and offered with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers. Introductory-offer eligibility is determined by Apple and applies once per subscription group per Apple Account — if you have already used a trial on another product in this group, or on this product before, you may not be offered one again. Nook shows you the trial only if your Apple Account is currently eligible for it.

Either subscription unlocks: unlimited items, unlimited places, up to 8 photos per item, CSV export & backup, and extra widget sizes.

Nook Pro Lifetime (com.nook.app.pro.lifetime) is a one-time, non-consumable purchase — no subscription, no recurring charge, and no trial — that unlocks the same set of features as Nook Plus, permanently, for as long as you own the app.

Prices are always shown in the app before purchase, exactly as Apple's App Store reports them for your storefront; this document does not restate them because the price you see in the app, localized to your currency, is authoritative.

How billing works

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • A Nook Plus subscription automatically renews unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Your Apple Account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device, or in the App Store. Nook itself has no separate billing system to manage — everything runs through your Apple Account.
  • Free trial terms: the 1-week trial offered on Nook Plus Yearly, where you are eligible for it, converts automatically into a paid annual subscription at the price shown when the trial ends, unless you cancel before it does. Any unused portion of a free trial period is forfeited when you purchase a subscription, where applicable (for example, if you had an active trial and separately purchased Nook Pro Lifetime).
  • Restore Purchases is always available on the Nook Plus / Nook Pro screen — including before a product has finished loading from the App Store — to recover a purchase made on another device or a previous install, using the same Apple Account.

If a subscription lapses

Nothing you have already saved in Nook is deleted, hidden, or locked if a subscription lapses or is cancelled. Every item, place, photo, tag, and note you created stays exactly as it is. What changes is only forward-looking: you return to the free tier's limits (15 items, 8 places, 1 photo per item) for adding new content, and CSV export, extra widget sizes, and the higher photo count stop being available until you resubscribe or purchase Nook Pro Lifetime. If you were already over those limits when the subscription lapsed, nothing already stored is removed — you simply cannot add further items or places until you're back under the free limit or subscribed again.

Your Nook account, and deleting it

If you sign in with Apple, you can delete your Nook account at any time from Settings → Nook Account → Delete Account. This removes the local account record Nook created and the data tied to it, after a confirmation step.

Deleting your Nook account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely through your Apple Account, independently of any Nook account. If you also want to stop being billed, cancel your subscription first in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, before or separately from deleting your Nook account — deleting the account alone will not stop billing.

Because Nook has no server, deleting your Nook account also cannot revoke the Sign in with Apple grant on Apple's side (that requires a server-side call Nook is not able to make). To remove Apple's own record of having signed in to Nook, use Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple → Nook → Stop Using Apple Account on your device.
