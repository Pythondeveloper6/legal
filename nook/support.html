Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device, and synced privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. Nook has no server, so no one else — including us — can see it.

How many items can I keep for free? Up to 15 items, 8 places, and 1 photo per item. Search, tags, places, and the widget are never limited, even on the free tier.

What do Nook Plus and Nook Pro Lifetime add? Both unlock the same features: unlimited items, unlimited places, up to 8 photos per item, CSV export & backup, and extra widget sizes.

  • Nook Plus is an auto-renewing subscription — monthly, or yearly with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers where you're eligible.
  • Nook Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase — no subscription, no trial, unlocks everything permanently.

How do I restore a purchase I made on another device? Open Settings in Nook and tap "Restore Purchases." It uses the Apple Account you're currently signed into on this device — make sure it's the same one you purchased with.

How do I cancel or manage my Nook Plus subscription? Nook has no billing of its own — all subscriptions run through your Apple Account. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device, or manage it from the App Store, and select Nook. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not remove anything you've already saved in Nook (see "What happens if my subscription ends?" below).

What happens if my subscription ends? Nothing you've already saved is deleted, hidden, or locked. You return to the free tier's limits for adding new items or places, and CSV export, extra widget sizes, and extra photos stop being available until you resubscribe or buy Nook Pro Lifetime.

Do I need to sign in to use Nook? No. Signing in with Apple is entirely optional. Every feature, including all purchases, works fully while signed out. Signing in only ties a purchase to your Apple Account for an easier restore on a new device.

How do I delete my Nook account? If you've signed in with Apple, go to Settings → Nook Account → Delete Account and confirm. This removes the local account record and its data. It does not cancel an App Store subscription (cancel that separately, above) and it does not remove Apple's own record of the sign-in — for that, use Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple → Nook → Stop Using Apple Account on your device.

How do I delete all my data? Delete individual items or places from the app, or remove everything at once from Settings → "Delete all data."

How do I export my data? CSV export & backup is available on Nook Plus and Nook Pro Lifetime, from Settings. It exports your items, places, and their details to a spreadsheet file you can save or share yourself.
