Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device. Macromate has no accounts and no servers — everything you log stays on your iPhone or iPad. The app works fully offline.

Is Macromate free? Yes. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads. Every feature is included.

Does Macromate use Apple Health? Only if you turn it on in You → Apple Health. With permission it writes the nutrients you log to Health and reads your active energy and steps so the calorie budget reflects your day. You can revoke access in iOS Settings → Health.

How do I track fasting? Open the Fasting tab, pick a protocol (such as 16:8), and start a fast. The ring and body-stage timeline update live.

How do I delete my data? Go to You → Delete all data. This permanently erases every entry and returns the app to its first-launch state. Deleting the app also removes all data.
