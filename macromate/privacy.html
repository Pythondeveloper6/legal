Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-25

Short version

Macromate does not collect your data. Everything you log — your profile, food entries, water, weight, fasts, and goals — is stored on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising. The app works fully offline.

What Macromate stores

Macromate keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad, in Apple's on-device database (SwiftData):

  • Your profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal).
  • Food, water, weight, and fasting entries you create.
  • Your targets, meals, custom foods, and app preferences.

This information never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable an Apple service below.

Apple services Macromate uses

  • Apple Health (HealthKit) — only if you turn it on. With your permission, Macromate writes the calories and nutrients you log to Health, and reads your active energy burned and steps so the calorie budget reflects your day. This data is exchanged directly with Apple Health on your device. Macromate never sends it anywhere else.
  • Local notifications — optional meal reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • WidgetKit / Live Activities — widgets and the Live Activity read a small snapshot of today's totals through an app group on your device.

Macromate does not use CloudKit, MapKit, or any third-party SDK.

Apple Health data

Health data is governed by your Health permissions. You can review or revoke Macromate's access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Macromate. Macromate does not use Health data for advertising and does not share it with anyone.

User control

You own your data. You can edit or delete any entry inside the app, and you can erase everything at once with You → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes all of its on-device data.

Children

Macromate is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from children. Because it collects no information at all, no data about any user is gathered.

Changes
