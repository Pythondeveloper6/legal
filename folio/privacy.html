Folio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Folio is built so your writing stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Folio does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your documents — their text, titles, tags, and annotations — are stored on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Folio stores

Documents you create in Folio are saved locally using Apple’s on‑device database. If you open folders or files from elsewhere on your device or in iCloud Drive, Folio reads those files in place; it does not copy or upload them anywhere. This data is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Files you open

When you open a folder or a file through the system file picker, Folio accesses only the items you choose. It keeps a secure bookmark so the file can reopen later, and it never reads beyond what you pick.

Apple services Folio relies on

  • StoreKit — Folio currently has no in‑app purchases. If that changes, any transaction would be processed by Apple, and Folio would never receive your payment details.

Folio does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any document, or all documents, at any time inside the app (Settings → “Delete all documents”).
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Folio does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
