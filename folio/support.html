Common questions

Where are my documents stored? On your device, in Folio’s private library. Folders and files you open from elsewhere stay where they are — Folio reads them in place.

Does Folio support Markdown? Yes. Folio is a Markdown editor with live preview, syntax highlighting, an outline, math (KaTeX), diagrams (Mermaid), and PDF export.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual documents from each document’s menu, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all documents.”

Is there an iPad version? Yes. Folio runs on both iPhone and iPad, with a split‑view library and editor on iPad.
