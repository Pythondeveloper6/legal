Twin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Twin is built to respect your privacy completely. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Twin does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.
  • Every scan, comparison, and deletion happens locally on your Mac.
  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

How Twin works

Twin is a sandboxed macOS app. You choose a folder using the system's Open panel, and Twin remembers that choice as a security-scoped bookmark so it can return to it on future launches — this bookmark, and the list of folders you've granted, are stored locally in UserDefaults, inside Twin's own app container.

When you run a scan, Twin walks the folder you picked, reads basic file metadata (size, creation and modification dates) and computes hashes to find byte-identical duplicates. If you turn on near-duplicate detection, it also compares images, documents, audio, or video for similarity, using on-device analysis only. Every scan's results — the files found, their paths, sizes, and hashes — are written to a local SQLite database at ~/Library/Application Support/Twin/twin.sqlite, so you can revisit past scans from History. None of this ever leaves your Mac.

Twin makes no network connections. It does not use iCloud, CloudKit, or any remote server, and it does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional "Remind me to scan" setting, Twin schedules a local notification through macOS Notification Center to nudge you to open the app again. This uses no network connection and sends no data anywhere — the reminder is generated and delivered entirely on your device.

What Twin can access

Twin only sees the folders you explicitly grant it access to via the Open panel (App Sandbox com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write). It cannot browse your whole disk, and it never touches system files or app bundles. If you enable "Protect Photos library" in Settings (on by default), Twin also avoids selecting files inside your Photos library for deletion.

Deletions

Twin moves files to the Trash by default, so anything it removes can be recovered until you empty the Trash. Permanent deletion is a separate action that always asks for confirmation first.

Your control

  • Revoke a folder's access at any time from Settings.
  • Add folders or files to the ignore list so Twin skips them during scans.
  • Delete Twin's local scan history and database by removing ~/Library/Application Support/Twin, or by deleting the app.
  • Turn off scan reminders at any time in Settings.

Children

Twin collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
