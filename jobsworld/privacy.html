JobsWorld — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

JobsWorld is a pretend-play app for children. It is built to keep a child's activity on the family's device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

  • JobsWorld does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data from children or adults.
  • There are no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, third-party SDKs, trackers, or in-app purchases.
  • The app works offline and makes no network requests of its own.
  • Progress and settings are stored only on the device so the app can remember a child's play.

What is stored on the device

JobsWorld stores a small amount of app state using Apple's on-device UserDefaults, including completed helper badges, per-job play counts, the selected language and appearance, sound and haptic preferences, optional playtime/bedtime settings, and whether onboarding was seen. A child may optionally name the in-app mascot; that name stays only on the device.

Badge progress, the selected language, and the daily-helper streak may also be copied to an on-device Apple App Group so the JobsWorld widget can display them. This is still local to the family's device; it is not cloud sync and is never sent to the developer.

JobsWorld does not request or access the camera, microphone, photo library, location, contacts, health data, notifications, advertising identifiers, or any other privacy-sensitive permission.

Speech, sound, and sharing

When narration is enabled, JobsWorld uses Apple's built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer to speak English or Arabic on the device. No spoken text or audio is sent to us. A parent may choose to share a completion certificate through Apple's system share sheet; JobsWorld does not receive the chosen destination or any content shared.

Parent gate and external links

External links, including this policy, the terms, support email, and certificate sharing, are placed behind a simple arithmetic parent gate. The gate is a child-safety measure, not an account or password, and answers are not saved or transmitted.

Your control and retention

A parent can reset the sticker book from the grown-ups area, which clears local badge and play progress. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Because JobsWorld has no server and collects no data, there is no account data for us to access, correct, or delete.

Children

JobsWorld is designed for children ages 2–8 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use where appropriate.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated in the same release as the changed behavior.
