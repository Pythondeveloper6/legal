Common questions

What is JobsWorld?
JobsWorld is a bilingual English-and-Arabic pretend-play app for children ages 2–8. Children explore a friendly town, step into community-helper jobs, complete gentle hands-on tasks, and collect badges.

Does it need the internet?
No. JobsWorld works entirely offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases.

Is it safe for children?
The app has no open web, social features, advertising, or purchases. Links, support email, certificate sharing, and reset controls are behind a simple parent gate.

How do I switch language?
Choose English or Arabic in Settings. The layout switches to right-to-left for Arabic and narration uses the device's built-in voice.

Can I turn off sound?
Yes. Settings lets you control narration, sound effects, music, haptics, and larger touch targets.

How do I reset play progress?
Open Settings, enter the grown-ups area using the parent gate, then choose Reset Sticker Book. This removes badges and replay counts from the device.

Why is play locked?
A parent may have enabled a daily playtime limit or bedtime window in the grown-ups area. A parent can change those settings there.

Does JobsWorld work with VoiceOver?
Yes. Interactive controls have accessibility labels, decorative art is hidden from VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion is respected.
