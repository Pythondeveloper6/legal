Eve — Privacy Policy

Effective 4 September 2026

Eve is a private wellbeing companion. It holds some of the most personal information a person keeps — cycle dates, pregnancy, sleep, notes about your own life — so the app was built on a single rule: that data is yours, it stays on your devices, and the developer never receives it.

There is no Eve server. Not a small one, not an anonymous one. There is nothing on our end for your data to be sent to.

The short version

  • Eve does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The developer never sees it.
  • Everything you log is stored on your device, and — if you have iCloud turned on — synced through your own private iCloud account, which belongs to you, not to us.
  • Eve reads sleep data from Apple Health, read-only and on device, only after you grant permission. It is never exported, shared, or sent anywhere by Eve.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Eve contains no non-Apple SDKs.
  • You can export everything to a file, or delete everything, from inside the app at any time.

What Eve stores, and where it stays

Every one of these exists for one purpose: making Eve work for you. None of it is used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or anything else.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available to the app, Eve syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit private database so your own iPhone and iPad stay in step. A private database is exactly that: a container inside your iCloud account, governed by your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it and no ability to read anything in it.

If iCloud isn't available, Eve falls back to storing everything locally on the device and keeps working normally.

Apple Health

Eve asks for read-only access to your sleep data in Apple Health, so your rest can appear on your Today view alongside the rest of your day. That is the only Health category Eve requests.

  • The permission is optional, asked for explicitly, and can be revoked at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.
  • Sleep data is read and displayed on your device. It is never uploaded, exported, shared with third parties, or sent to the developer.
  • Eve does not write anything back into Apple Health.
  • Eve does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or any use-based data mining, and does not disclose it to anyone.

Face ID, Touch ID, and app lock

You can turn on App lock so Eve asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before it opens. This uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework: iOS performs the check and tells Eve only "yes" or "no." Your face and fingerprint data never leave the Secure Enclave, and Eve never sees them.

If your device has no passcode set, Eve turns app lock off rather than risk locking you out of your own data.

Discreet mode and notifications

Reminders are optional. If you turn one on, Eve asks for notification permission and then schedules the reminder on your device. No reminder is routed through a server — there is no push service, no remote scheduling, and nothing about your cycle ever travels to send you a notification.

Discreet mode is on by default. With it on, nothing outside the unlocked app — not your Today view preview, not a notification on your lock screen, not a widget — names cycle tracking or pregnancy. The onboarding shows you the exact wording a reminder would use before you agree to any of it.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is entirely optional. Every feature of Eve, including Eve Plus, works fully while signed out.

If you choose to sign in, Apple provides Eve with a stable identifier and, on your first sign-in only, your name and either your real email address or an Apple private-relay address. Eve keeps these on your device. No password is ever created, seen, or stored by the developer, and Eve has no server to send any of it to.

To remove it: sign out inside Eve, or use Settings → Delete everything. To also revoke Eve's access to your Apple Account itself, go to your device's Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple and remove Eve from the list. That step belongs to Apple and is separate from deleting your data in the app.

Apple services Eve relies on

  • iCloud / CloudKit — syncs your data inside your own private iCloud account.
  • HealthKit — read-only sleep, on device, with your permission.
  • LocalAuthentication — Face ID / Touch ID for app lock.
  • UserNotifications — local reminders, scheduled on device.
  • StoreKit — Apple processes every Eve Plus purchase. Eve never receives your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail.

Apple is the only party any of this ever touches, and only through the systems built into your own device. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What Eve does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry SDK of any kind.
  • No advertising SDK, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No crash-reporting service that receives your data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information — there is nothing to sell, because nothing arrives.

Your control over your data

  • Export my data (Settings → Your data) writes everything Eve holds to a JSON file you can save or send wherever you like.
  • Delete everything (Settings → Your data) permanently erases every period, log, note, to-do, workout, habit and family entry on the device — and, if iCloud sync is on, from your private iCloud too. It is immediate and cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
  • Turning a module off simply hides it. Nothing is deleted.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Data already synced to your own iCloud is removed with "Delete everything," or by managing Eve's storage in your iCloud settings.

Children

Eve is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not a children's app, is not offered in the Kids Category, and does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used Eve, note that any data involved lives only on that device and your own iCloud, and can be erased with Settings → Delete everything.
