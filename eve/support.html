Common questions

What is Eve? A private wellbeing companion for iPhone and iPad, on iOS 17 and later. It brings together cycle tracking and pregnancy mode, sleep, to-dos, notes, gym and workouts, habits, learning and family — in one calm place that only you can see.

Where is my data kept? Can you see it? On your device, and — if iCloud is on — in your own private iCloud account. That's it. There is no Eve server, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent to us. No analytics, no ad SDKs, no third-party trackers. The Privacy Policy spells out every category.

Does Eve work without iCloud, or offline? Yes. If iCloud isn't available, Eve stores everything locally and carries on. It needs no internet connection of its own; the only network traffic it can cause is Apple's, handling a purchase you started.

How do I get my data onto a new iPhone or iPad? Sign the new device in to the same iCloud account with iCloud on, and Eve syncs through your own private iCloud. You can also use Settings → Export my data to save a JSON copy of everything.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Your data → Delete everything. It permanently erases every period, log, note, to-do, workout, habit and family entry on the device — and from your private iCloud too if sync is on. It's immediate and can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

What is discreet mode? It's on by default. With it on, nothing outside the unlocked app names what you track: your notifications, lock-screen previews and widgets stay in neutral wording. Onboarding shows you the exact wording a reminder would use before you agree to it. You can toggle it in Settings → Privacy.

How do I lock the app? Settings → Privacy → App lock. Eve then asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before it opens. Your biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave and Eve never sees it. If your device has no passcode set, Eve keeps app lock off so you can't be locked out of your own data.

Why does Eve want my Health data? Only to show your sleep on your Today view. It's read-only, sleep only, on device only, and entirely optional. Revoke it any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps. Eve never writes to Apple Health and never sends Health data anywhere.

Why won't Eve tell me my cycle phase? Because it doesn't have enough logged history yet to be confident. Eve would rather say nothing than guess. Log a few cycles and the estimates appear — labelled as estimates, which is what they are.

Are the predictions reliable enough to use as birth control? No. Eve's predictions are statistical estimates from the dates you logged, not a measurement of your body, and Eve must never be used as contraception or to diagnose anything. Please read the Medical Disclaimer — it matters.

Why aren't my reminders arriving? Reminders need notification permission. If you declined it, turn Eve's notifications back on in the Settings app → Eve → Notifications, then re-enable the reminder in Eve. Period and PMS heads-ups also wait until there's enough logged history to estimate from, and Eve never sends more than one a day.

Do I need an account? No. Eve works fully without one. Sign in with Apple is offered purely as an option and unlocks nothing on its own — no password is ever created or held by the developer.

What is Eve Plus, and what does it cost? Eve Plus unlocks cycle-aware insights, pregnancy mode, home-screen widgets and unlimited habits. It comes as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your own currency before you confirm anything. Full terms are in the Terms of Use.

How do I cancel a subscription? In the App Store, not inside Eve: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Eve → Cancel Subscription on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, or the next period is charged. You keep Eve Plus until the period you've already paid for runs out, and deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What happens to my data if Plus ends? Nothing is deleted or hidden. Everything you've logged stays exactly where it is; only the Plus features lock again, and they come straight back if you resubscribe or buy lifetime.

I bought Eve Plus — how do I get it on another device? Tap Restore on the Eve Plus screen, on a device signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. It's free, works any number of times, and needs no sign-in to Eve.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we can't issue one. Request it at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If something in the app is genuinely broken, please email us too, so we can fix it.

Can I hide modules I don't use? Yes — Settings → Modules. Turning a module off just hides it; nothing is deleted, and you can turn it back on whenever you like. You can also pin up to three modules as their own tabs.
