Common questions

Where is my subscription data stored? On your device. Recur has no accounts and no server — nothing leaves your phone.

How much does Recur cost? Recur is free, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

How do reminders work? Turn on "Renewal reminders" in Settings and Recur schedules a local notification a few days before each subscription renews — you choose how many days and what time of day. You can also override the timing per subscription when you add or edit it.

What if I stop paying for something but want to keep the record? Open the subscription and tap "Pause subscription." It stays in your list and history but stops counting toward your totals and reminders until you resume it.

What billing cycles does Recur support? Weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly. Insights convert everything to a monthly-equivalent so your totals stay comparable.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual subscriptions inside the app, or remove everything at once in Settings → "Delete all subscriptions."
