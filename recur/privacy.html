Recur — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Recur is built to track what you pay for without ever reporting on you. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Recur does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Every subscription, amount, category, and reminder setting is stored on your device. The developer has no access to it.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What Recur stores

Everything you add in Recur — subscription names, amounts, billing cycles, categories, next charge dates, notes, and reminder timing — is saved locally using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData), inside a shared app group container. That container exists so a future Recur home-screen widget can read the same data; today it is used only by the app itself, and nothing in it ever leaves your device.

Reminders

If you turn on renewal reminders, Recur schedules local notifications using Apple's notification framework, timed a few days before each charge at the hour you choose. These reminders are generated and delivered entirely on your device — no reminder content is sent to any server.

Recur does not include any non-Apple SDKs, does not use your location, your contacts, your camera, your photo library, or HealthKit, and makes no network requests of any kind.

Your control

  • Edit or delete any subscription at any time inside the app.
  • Pause a subscription instead of deleting it if you just want it out of your totals for a while.
  • Remove everything at once in Settings → "Delete all subscriptions," which also cancels its scheduled reminders.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Recur does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
