Number World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Number World is made for children, so it is built to collect nothing about them. This policy describes exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

The short version

  • Number World does not collect, transmit, sell, or share data about a child, parent, or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, sign-in screens, advertisements, analytics, trackers, third-party SDKs, or in-app purchases.
  • The app works entirely offline and makes no network requests.
  • Progress and settings stay only in the app's local storage on your device.

What Number World stores on the device

  • Learning progress — which number and shape entries have been opened, stored as fixed identifiers from the app's built-in catalogue. They are not names, scores, or information about a child.
  • Settings — language, narration and sounds preferences, appearance choice, and whether onboarding has been completed. These are app configuration only.
  • Number of the Day — the app and optional Home Screen widget calculate a daily featured number from the date on your device. No information leaves the device.

This local information is kept with UserDefaults. It is never uploaded to a developer server or shared with anyone. It may be included in your own Apple device backup if you use one.

Device features

When narration is enabled, Number World uses Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer to read built-in learning content. No child audio is recorded, and no text or audio is sent to a server. The app does not access the camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, health data, or advertising identifiers.

Parents and children

Settings are behind a simple parent gate. It is not an account or password system, and nothing entered there is transmitted. A parent can reset progress from Settings; deleting the app also removes its local data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. For privacy questions, email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com.
