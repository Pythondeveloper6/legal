Common questions

What can my child learn? Number World offers tap-to-count activities from 0–20, eight familiar shapes, and visual addition within 10.

Does it need the internet? No. Everything is included in the download and the app makes no network requests.

Is it free and ad-free? Yes. There are no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Does it support Arabic? Yes. Every activity is available in English and Arabic, including right-to-left layout.

How do I change language, sounds, or appearance? Open Settings through the parent gate, then choose language, narration, sounds, and light, dark, or system appearance.

How do I reset progress? Open Settings through the parent gate and choose the reset option. Deleting the app also removes its local data.
