Stay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

Stay is built so that what happens on your Mac stays on your Mac.

The short version

  • Stay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • Stay does not require any special permission — it only posts a synthetic, one-pixel cursor move via the standard CGEvent API, which does not need Accessibility access.

What Stay stores

Your selected jiggle speed and which displays are eligible for jiggling are stored in the app's local preferences (UserDefaults) on your Mac. This data never leaves your device.

Children

Stay does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
