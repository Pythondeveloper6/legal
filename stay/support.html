Common questions

Does Stay need any special permission? No. Stay only posts a standard one-pixel synthetic cursor move, which macOS does not gate behind Accessibility or any other permission prompt.

Why didn't my cursor move on my second monitor? Check Displays in the menu-bar menu — only checked displays are eligible for jiggling. This lets you exclude a display you're screen-sharing on.

How do I change how often it jiggles? Open the menu-bar icon → Speed and choose Slow / Normal / Fast.

How do I make Stay start automatically? Turn on Launch at login in Settings → General.
