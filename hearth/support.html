Common questions

Where is my family's data stored? On your device, and only there. Hearth has no accounts, no servers and makes no network requests, so nothing leaves the phone or iPad you use it on.

Does Hearth sync between our phones? Not yet. Hearth is a single‑device household organiser: set it up on the device your family shares (the kitchen iPad, the family iPhone) and everyone uses it there. It is shared with Hearth's own widget and Apple Watch app on that device.

How do I add family members? Settings → your household → "Add family member". Adding or changing members is a parent action, so it asks for the parent passcode.

What is the parent passcode? Profiles in Hearth are personas on one shared device, not separate accounts — anyone can tap "I am…" and pick a name. The parent passcode is what makes parent controls real: approving chores, managing rewards, changing members and deleting data all ask for it, and it relocks when you leave the app. Set or change it in Settings → Parent controls.

Can a child approve their own chores? No. Approving a chore that carries stars requires parent authority, which means the parent passcode has been entered on the device — picking a parent profile is not enough. And an approval can only ever pay out once, however many times it is tapped.

Can I keep chore names off the lock screen? Yes — Settings → Reminders → "Discreet reminders".

How do stars and rewards work? Completing tasks can earn stars for a family member. You can create rewards in the Rewards screen that members redeem with the stars they've earned.

How do I delete my data? Remove individual items from their menus, or wipe everything in Settings → "Delete all data." That clears the household, the star history, the widget and Watch data and Hearth's pending reminders. There is no cloud copy to delete.
