Hearth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Hearth is built so your family's information stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Hearth does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Everything you create — household members, tasks, lists, calendar events, meals, grocery items, rewards and star history — is stored on your device only. Hearth makes no network requests at all, so the developer has no access to it and neither does anyone else.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Hearth stores

Everything you create in Hearth (member profiles, tasks, lists, events, meal plans, grocery items, star balances and the star history behind them) is saved locally using Apple's on‑device database, in an app group container shared only with Hearth's own widget and Apple Watch app on the same device.

Hearth is a single‑device app

Hearth does not sync your household between family members' devices. There is no account, no server, no iCloud sync and no household sharing. Everyone in your family uses Hearth on the one device you set it up on.

Apple services Hearth relies on

  • Local notifications — reminders are scheduled on your device. Hearth does not use push servers and does not transmit reminder content anywhere. You can turn on "Discreet reminders" in Settings to keep chore and event names off the lock screen.

That is the entire list. Hearth includes no non‑Apple SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests of any kind.

Children

Hearth is designed for family use and may include child profiles that parents create. These profiles are just names, colours, avatars and star balances you enter yourself; Hearth does not collect personal information from children or anyone else, and no data ever leaves the device.

Profiles are convenience personas on a shared device, not separate secured accounts. Parent controls — approving chores, managing rewards, changing members and deleting data — are protected by a parent passcode you set, which is stored on the device only as a salted hash and is never transmitted.

Your control

  • You can delete any item, or all data, at any time inside the app (Settings → "Delete all data"). That removes your household, members, lists, tasks, calendar, meals, groceries, rewards and the whole star history, clears the widget and Watch data, cancels Hearth's pending reminders and resets the parent passcode. Because Hearth stores nothing remotely, there is no second copy left anywhere.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
