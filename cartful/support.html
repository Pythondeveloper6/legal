Common questions

Where are my lists stored? On your device. Cartful has no accounts and no server — nothing leaves your phone.

How much does Cartful cost? Cartful is free, with no subscription and no in‑app purchases.

Can I set a budget? Yes. Give a list a budget and Cartful totals your items and warns you before you go over.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual lists or items inside the app, or remove everything in Settings → "Delete all data."
