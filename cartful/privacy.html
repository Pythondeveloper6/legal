Cartful — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Cartful is built so your shopping lists stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Cartful does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your lists, items, prices, budgets, and categories are stored on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Cartful stores

Everything you create in Cartful (lists, items, quantities, estimated prices, budgets, priorities, and categories) is saved locally using Apple's on‑device database. A small summary is also kept in a shared app group container so the Cartful home‑screen widgets can display it. All of this stays on your device and is controlled by you.

Apple services Cartful relies on

  • WidgetKit — to show your lists in home‑screen widgets, using only data already on your device.

Cartful does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data. It does not use your location, your contacts, your camera, or your photo library.

Your control

  • You can delete any list or item at any time inside the app.
  • You can remove everything at once in Settings → "Delete all data."
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Cartful does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
