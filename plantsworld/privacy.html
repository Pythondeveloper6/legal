Plants World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Plants World is a local-first educational atlas of Earth's plants, made for curious children. It is built so it can be used without an account, an internet connection, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

The short version

  • Plants World does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data — about a child or a grown-up.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no field anywhere in the app that accepts personal information.
  • The app makes no network requests at all. Every plant, photo, fact and nature recording is bundled inside the download, and the app works with the device in Airplane Mode.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no trackers, no in-app purchases.
  • The only things saved are which plants have been visited and a few preferences, stored on the device, and a grown-up can erase them at any time.

What Plants World stores on the device

  • Progress — the identifiers of the plants that have been visited (for example saguaro, lavender), so each biome can show how much has been explored. These are fixed identifiers from the app's own catalogue of 64 plants; they are not names, not scores, and not anything about the child.
  • Settings — the selected language (English or Arabic), whether facts are read aloud, whether nature sounds play, the day/night appearance, and whether the welcome screen has been seen. This is app configuration, never personal information.
  • All of the above is kept in the app's own local UserDefaults on that one device. It never leaves the app's sandbox, is never uploaded, and is not available to the developer. It travels only inside your own Apple device backup, if you use one.
  • No camera, no photo library, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no notifications, no health data, no identifiers for advertisers are requested or accessed. The app declares no privacy-sensitive permissions at all, so iOS never shows your child a permission prompt.

Reading facts aloud, and nature sounds

When narration is on, the app speaks the plant's name and fact using Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. No text or audio is sent off the device to produce that speech. Biome ambience is played from sound files bundled inside the app. Plants World never records audio and never uses the microphone.

Photos and recordings

Every plant photograph and every ambience recording ships inside the app under a free licence (Creative Commons or public domain). Nothing is fetched from the internet while your child is using the app. Each author, licence and source is listed under Settings → Photo credits. The source addresses are shown there as plain, selectable text on purpose — nothing in this app opens a web browser.

The grown-ups area

Settings, credits and the erase control sit behind a parent gate: a randomly generated multiplication question that a young child cannot solve, which generates a new question after a wrong answer. This is a soft barrier that keeps grown-up controls away from little thumbs. It is not an account, it stores no password, and nothing entered there is transmitted anywhere.

Your control

  • Settings → Erase all local data permanently removes the preferences and the plant-visit progress described above.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from the device.

Children

Plants World is designed for children ages 4–8, with a parent or guardian in control of the device, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There is no way to type a name, birthdate, photo or any other identifying detail anywhere in the app, and no way for a child to reach the internet, another person, a store, or an advertisement from inside it. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it, in the same release as the behaviour it describes.
