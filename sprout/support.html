Common questions

Where is my children's health data stored? On your device, and synced privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. No one else — including the developer — can see it.

Is Sprout medical advice? No. Sprout helps you organize records. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

How many children can I track for free? Up to two. Sprout Pro is a one‑time purchase that unlocks unlimited children, photo attachments, and PDF health reports.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here? Open More in Sprout and tap "Restore purchases."

How do I delete my data? Delete individual records from each list, remove a child from More, or remove everything in More → "Delete all data."
