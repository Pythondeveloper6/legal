Sprout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Sprout is a private health log for your children. It is built so your family's medical information stays yours. This policy explains exactly what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

The short version

  • Sprout does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data.
  • Your children's health information — profiles, symptoms, medications and doses, growth measurements, vaccines, allergies, conditions, appointments, emergency contacts, and any photos you attach — is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to it.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

Health data stays on your device

Sprout is a medical-logging app, so we are especially clear about this: everything you record about your children is saved locally using Apple's on‑device database. If iCloud is enabled, the same data syncs via Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud account so it is available on your other devices. This data is controlled by you and your Apple ID. The developer cannot see, retrieve, or recover it, and it is never sent to any server we operate.

Apple services Sprout relies on

  • iCloud / CloudKit — to sync and back up your log within your own account.
  • StoreKit — when you purchase Sprout Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. Sprout never receives your payment details.
  • Local notifications — medication and appointment reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Sprout does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Photos

Sprout only accesses the specific photos you choose through the system photo picker, or that you capture with the camera when attaching them to a log. It does not read your photo library beyond the items you pick.

Your control

  • You can delete any record, an entire child's log, or all data at any time inside the app (More → "Delete all data").
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. iCloud copies are managed through your Apple ID settings.

Children

Sprout is a tool for parents and caregivers to keep records about their own children. It does not collect personal information from children, and it has no accounts, sign‑in, or social features.

Not medical advice

Sprout helps you organize information. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
