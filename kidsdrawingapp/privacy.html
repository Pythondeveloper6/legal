Kids Drawing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Kids Drawing is built for children, so it is built to collect nothing about them. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

The short version

  • Kids Drawing does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data — about a child or a parent.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to enter personal information anywhere in the app.
  • Finished drawings and app settings are stored only on the device, inside the app's own local storage.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no trackers of any kind.
  • Color names and encouragement are spoken on-device — nothing is sent to a server to generate them.

How Kids Drawing works

  • Finished artwork — when a child finishes coloring a page, the app renders the picture and saves it (plus a thumbnail) as image files in the app's local Application Support folder, alongside a small local index. Each picture is labelled with the on-device player profile that made it, so each player only ever sees their own gallery. Nothing about the artwork ever leaves the device.
  • Unfinished pages — if a child leaves a page part-coloured, the colours chosen so far and how much of the tracing is done are saved locally so they can pick up where they left off. This is cleared as soon as the page is finished, and by any of the delete controls below.
  • Player profiles — a profile is an animal avatar picked from a fixed list. It stores only that choice; there is no name, age, photo or any other detail, and no free-text field anywhere in the app to type one into.
  • App settings — sound on/off, appearance (light/dark/system), language (English/Arabic), and whether onboarding has been completed are saved locally using UserDefaults. This is app configuration only, never personal information.
  • Spoken feedback — color names, tracing encouragement, and the parental-gate audio prompt use Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. No audio or text is sent off the device to produce this speech.
  • No camera, no photo library, no microphone, no location, and no contacts are accessed by the app.
  • No network requests are made by the app at all — Kids Drawing works completely offline.

How long artwork is kept

Each player keeps their newest 60 finished pictures on the device. When a player saves a 61st, the oldest one — and both of its image files — is removed automatically, so the app cannot grow without bound on a shared family iPad. Nothing is archived elsewhere first: removed means deleted from the device.

The parents' corner

Settings, the activity summary, artwork export and every delete control sit behind a parental gate (a spelled-out-number challenge a young child can't read or solve). Repeated wrong answers pause the gate for a minute. This is a soft barrier to keep the grown-up area for grown-ups — it is not an account system, and nothing typed there is transmitted anywhere.

Your control

All of these live behind the parental gate, so a child cannot delete a sibling's work — or their own — by accident: - Grown-ups → Manage artwork — delete a single picture, or every picture belonging to one player. - Grown-ups → Manage artwork → Export — save a player's pictures to Files or Photos, or share them yourself. The app never shares anything on its own; there is no in-app posting, messaging or community feature of any kind. - Grown-ups → Settings → Erase everything — removes every player's pictures, every unfinished page, and all settings (including appearance and language) from this device. This cannot be undone. - Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device.

Children

Kids Drawing is designed for children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There is no way to enter a name, birthdate, photo, or any other identifying detail anywhere in the app.
