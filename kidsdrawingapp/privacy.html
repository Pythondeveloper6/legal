Kids Drawing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Kids Drawing is made for children, so it is built to collect nothing about them. This policy describes exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device. It is written to be read by a parent in a couple of minutes, not by a lawyer.

Developer: Mahmoud Ahmed · Contact: apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com

The short version

  • Kids Drawing does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data — about a child or a grown-up.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no free-text field anywhere in the app that could accept a name or any other personal detail.
  • The app makes no network requests at all. Every page, colour and sound is bundled inside the download, and the app works with the device in Airplane Mode.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no trackers, no in-app purchases.
  • Everything the app saves — drawings, unfinished pages, settings — stays on that one device, and a grown-up can erase all of it at any time.

What Kids Drawing stores on this device

  • Finished artwork — when a child finishes colouring a page, the app renders the picture and saves it (plus a small thumbnail) as image files in the app's own Application Support folder, alongside a local index file. Each picture is labelled with the on-device player profile that made it, so each player only ever sees their own gallery. Nothing about the artwork ever leaves the device.
  • Unfinished pages — if a child leaves a page part-coloured, the colours chosen so far and how much of the tracing is done are saved locally so they can pick up where they left off under "Keep going". This is cleared as soon as the page is finished, and by any of the delete controls below.
  • Player profiles — a profile is an animal avatar chosen from a fixed catalogue built into the app (fox, panda, rocket, bear, owl, whale, dinosaur, bee). A profile stores only which avatar was picked. There is no name, no age, no birthdate, no photo, and no free-text field anywhere in the app to type one into. A device can hold at most six profiles — it is a family device, not an account system.
  • An age band — during first-time setup a grown-up is asked "How old is your child?" and picks one of three broad bands (2–3, 4–5, 6–8) so the app can pitch itself appropriately. Only that one coarse band is stored, as a fixed value like 4-5. No birthdate or exact age is ever requested or stored, the band is not tied to any individual profile, and it is never transmitted anywhere.
  • App settings — sound on/off, appearance (light/dark/system), language (English/Arabic), and whether onboarding has been completed. This is app configuration, never personal information.
  • All of the above is kept in the app's own local storage on that one device — image files in Application Support, everything else in the app's private UserDefaults. It is never uploaded, never backed up to a server of ours, and never shared with anyone. It travels only inside your own Apple device backup, if you use one.

What Kids Drawing never does

  • No network requests. The app contains no networking code, no web view, no remote configuration, and no way for a child to reach the internet, a store, an advertisement, or another person from inside it.
  • No permissions. The app declares no privacy-sensitive permission at all — no camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, health data, or advertising identifier — so iOS never shows your child a permission prompt.
  • No third-party code. There are no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting or social SDKs of any kind bundled in the app.
  • No purchases. There is no in-app purchase, no subscription, and no store of any kind inside the app.

Spoken feedback

Colour names, tracing encouragement, and the read-aloud prompt on the grown-ups gate are spoken using Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. No text or audio is sent off the device to produce that speech, and the microphone is never used — the app only speaks, it never listens.

How long artwork is kept

Each player keeps their newest 60 finished pictures on the device. When a player saves a 61st, the oldest one — and both of its image files — is removed automatically, so the app can never grow without bound on a shared family iPad. Nothing is archived elsewhere first: removed means deleted from the device.

Children's privacy

Kids Drawing is designed to be used directly by children, is submitted to the App Store Kids Category, and carries a 4+ age rating.

  • We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted, there is no profile of your child anywhere, and nothing about your child exists on any server of ours.
  • COPPA (US). The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act governs the online collection of personal information from children under 13. Kids Drawing collects none: no name, no email address, no screen name, no photo, no voice recording, no location, and no persistent identifier used for tracking. There is therefore no parental-consent flow, because there is nothing to consent to.
  • GDPR-K (EU/UK) and similar laws. Article 8 of the GDPR (and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code) concerns the processing of children's personal data. Kids Drawing performs no such processing: no personal data is collected, no profiling or automated decision-making takes place, no data is shared with any third party, and no data leaves the device, so there is no international transfer and no lawful basis is required.
  • No behavioural advertising and no tracking. The app shows no ads at all, of any kind, and never builds a profile of a child for any purpose.
  • No contact with strangers. There is no chat, no comments, no community, no user accounts, and no way for a child to send or receive anything.
  • No links out of the app. The app opens no web browser and no external link from any child-facing screen.
  • Your rights as a parent. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete from us. Everything your child creates is under your own control on your own device, and the controls below erase it completely.

The grown-ups area

Settings, the activity summary, artwork export and every delete control sit behind a parental gate: a four-digit challenge spelled out in words that a young child can't read or solve. Five wrong answers pause the gate for a minute. This is a soft barrier that keeps grown-up controls away from little thumbs — it is not an account, it stores no password, and nothing entered there is transmitted anywhere.

Your control

All of these live behind the parental gate, so a child cannot delete a sibling's work — or their own — by accident.

  • Grown-ups → Manage artwork — delete a single picture, or every picture belonging to one player.
  • Grown-ups → Manage artwork → Export — hands a player's pictures to the standard iOS share sheet, which you drive: from there you may save them to Files or Photos, AirDrop them, or send them wherever you choose. The app never shares anything on its own, and there is no in-app posting, messaging or community feature of any kind.
  • Grown-ups → Settings → Erase everything — removes every player's pictures, every unfinished page, every profile and all settings (including appearance and language) from this device. This cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device.

App Store privacy label

Kids Drawing is declared on the App Store as Data Not Collected: no data used to track you, no data linked to you, and no data not linked to you.
