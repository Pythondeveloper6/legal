Kids Drawing — Support

Need a hand with Kids Drawing? We're happy to help.

Contact

Email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Telling us your device (iPhone or iPad), your iOS version and the app version — Grown-ups → Settings → About → Version — helps us answer faster.

Common questions

Does Kids Drawing need an internet connection? No. Kids Drawing makes no network requests at all — every page, colour and sound ships inside the download, and the app works with the device in Airplane Mode.

How much does Kids Drawing cost? It's free. No subscription, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

Where are my child's drawings stored? On that device only, in the app's own private storage. There are no accounts and no server — nothing about a drawing ever leaves the iPhone or iPad it was made on.

Can more than one child use the same iPad? Yes. Add a player from the "Who's playing?" screen — each player is an animal avatar (fox, panda, rocket, bear, owl, whale, dinosaur, bee) with their own private gallery and their own unfinished pages. A device can hold up to six players. There is no name to type: avatars are the whole profile, on purpose.

What is the smart palette? When your child taps a part of the picture — the sky, the grass, an animal's fur — the colour bar only offers colours that are realistic for that part. Each colour names itself out loud as it's chosen. Tap Free colours any time for the full rainbow instead.

What does "Make it real" do? It fills the whole page with true-to-life colours in one tap — a fun finishing move, or a quick way to see what a page "should" look like. Like every other mark, it can be undone.

My child left a page half-finished. Is it lost? No. Unfinished pages are saved automatically and are waiting under Keep going the next time that player picks up the iPad.

How do I get to Settings or see how my child is doing? Tap the lock marked Grown-ups. You'll be asked to read a four-digit number spelled out in words and type it in — a gentle gate to keep grown-up controls away from little thumbs, not an account or a login. Five wrong answers pause the gate for a minute; tap "Hear it again" to have the number read aloud.

How do I delete drawings, or reset the app? Behind the grown-ups gate: Manage artwork deletes a single picture or every picture belonging to one player, and Settings → Erase everything removes every player's pictures, every unfinished page, every profile and all settings from the device. That one can't be undone.

How do I get my child's drawings off the iPad? Grown-ups → Manage artwork → Export hands that player's pictures to the standard iOS share sheet, so you can save them to Files or Photos, AirDrop them, or send them wherever you like. The app never shares anything on its own.

Why do my child's older drawings disappear? Each player keeps their newest 60 finished pictures. When a 61st is saved, the oldest is removed automatically so the app can't fill up a shared family iPad. Export the ones you want to keep first.

Is there sound, and does the app speak Arabic? Yes. Kids Drawing speaks colour names and encouragement out loud using the device's own built-in voices, and the whole app — including full right-to-left layout — is available in English and Arabic from Grown-ups → Settings. Sound can be turned off there too.

Does the app ask for the camera, photos, microphone or location? No. Kids Drawing requests no permissions at all, so your child will never see a permission prompt.

Are there ads, or anything my child could tap out of the app into? No. There are no ads, no links out of the app, no web browser, no chat, and no store — nothing a child can tap that leads off the page they're colouring.
