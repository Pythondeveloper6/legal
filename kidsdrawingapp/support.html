Common questions

Where are my child's drawings stored? On the device only. Kids Drawing has no accounts and no server — nothing about a drawing ever leaves the iPad or iPhone it was made on.

How much does Kids Drawing cost? Kids Drawing is free, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

What is the smart palette? When your child taps a part of the picture — the sky, the grass, an animal's fur — the color bar only offers colors that are realistic for that part. Tap Free Colors any time for the full rainbow instead.

What does "Make it real" do? It fills the whole page with true-to-life colors in one tap — a fun finishing move, or a quick way to see what a page "should" look like.

How do I get to Settings or see progress? Tap the lock icon marked "Grown-ups" on the avatar or page-picker screen. You'll be asked to read a spelled-out number and enter it — a gentle gate to keep settings away from little thumbs, not an account or login.

How do I delete drawings or reset the app? Delete a single drawing from the gallery, or clear everything at once from Settings → Reset (behind the grown-ups gate).

Is there sound and Arabic support? Yes — Kids Drawing speaks color names and encouragement out loud, and the whole app (including right-to-left layout) is available in English and Arabic from Settings.
