Common questions

What is Pass for? Pass keeps the small things you’d lose in chats — codes, plans, recipes, account details — as tidy, searchable cards.

Where are my cards stored? On your device, and synced privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. No one else can see them unless you choose to share a card.

How many cards can I keep for free? Up to three. Pass Pro is a one‑time purchase that unlocks unlimited cards.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here? Open Settings in Pass and tap “Restore purchase.”

How does sharing work, and is it private? Sharing is optional and off by default. Cards you never share never leave your device and iCloud. When you share a card by username, only that card’s title, notes, and the permission you grant are sent so the recipient can receive it. You can pause or stop sharing at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual cards from each card’s menu, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all cards.”
