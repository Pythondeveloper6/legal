Lexicon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Lexicon is a local-first markdown editor. Your writing stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Lexicon does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your documents are plain markdown files that live on your Mac, in the folders you choose. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • Lexicon has no account system and does not require an internet connection.

What Lexicon stores

Lexicon edits the markdown files in the workspace folder you open. It reads and writes those files directly on disk; it never copies them anywhere else. A small amount of preference data — your chosen theme, editor zoom, and the list of recently opened workspaces — is stored locally in the app's preferences. None of this leaves your Mac.

Apple and system services Lexicon relies on

  • WebKit (WKWebView) — the editing canvas runs locally inside a WebKit view bundled with macOS. No remote content is loaded.
  • git — when a workspace is a git repository, Lexicon runs the local git command read-only (git status) to show change badges. It never commits, pushes, or transmits anything.
  • pandoc — if you export to Word, EPUB, or PDF and have pandoc installed, Lexicon invokes your local pandoc binary. Export runs entirely on your Mac.

Lexicon does not include any data-collecting SDKs.

Your control

  • Your documents are ordinary files: move, back up, or delete them with Finder.
  • You can clear the recent-workspaces list and reset preferences any time from Settings → Privacy → Reset.
  • Removing the app leaves your markdown files untouched on disk.

Children

Lexicon does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
