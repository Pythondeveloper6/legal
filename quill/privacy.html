Quill — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Quill is an offline notebook app for iPad. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

The short version

  • Quill does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.
  • Every notebook, folder, day entry, todo, and planner item lives only in a local database on your iPad.
  • There is no account, no server, no sync, and no analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.

How Quill works

Quill stores everything you create — folders, notebooks, weekly-notebook day pages (journal text, highlights, todos), home-planner modules and their items, and plain notes — in a local SwiftData database that lives inside the app's own sandboxed container on your device. Nothing you write is ever sent off the iPad; Quill has no network code at all.

A small number of app preferences — your chosen appearance (system, light, or dark), whether haptic feedback is on, and whether you've completed the first-run onboarding — are stored using Apple's UserDefaults, again entirely within the app's own container. These preferences are never transmitted anywhere.

Your control

Because everything stays on your iPad, you're always in control of it. You can edit or delete any notebook, folder, day entry, todo, or note directly in the app. Settings includes a "Delete all data" option that permanently removes every notebook and folder — including everything inside them — in one step.

Uninstalling Quill removes its local database and preferences along with the app, per standard iOS behavior.

Children

Quill collects no personal information from anyone, including children — there is nothing to collect, since the app never leaves your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
