Common questions

Does Quill work without an internet connection? Yes — Quill is entirely offline. It doesn't need Wi-Fi or cellular data to work, and nothing you write ever leaves your iPad.

Does Quill sync between my devices? Not yet. Quill currently stores everything locally on the one iPad you're using. There's no iCloud sync or multi-device support in this version.

What's the difference between the three notebook types? A weekly notebook is a year-long daily journal that always opens to today, with a journal entry, a highlight, and todos per day (unfinished todos carry forward automatically). A home planner is a board of checklist modules — Groceries, Meals, Travel, Chores, and more — that you add or remove as your household needs change. A plain note is just a title and a free-form body for anything else.

I finished a todo but it disappeared — where did it go? Finished todos move to a "Done" section on the day page rather than vanishing — check below the active todos. Unfinished todos from your most recent earlier entry carry forward to today's page automatically.

Can I change what modules are on my home planner? Yes — open the planner and use the module gallery to add or remove modules (Groceries, Shopping, Meals, Travel, Chores, Home maintenance, Budget notes, Wishlist, Projects, or a Custom list) at any time. Removing a module deletes its items.

How do I find something I wrote weeks ago? Use Search from the sidebar — it looks across every notebook's titles, note bodies, journal entries, highlights, todos, and planner items at once. Tapping a result jumps straight to that day or notebook.

How do I erase everything and start fresh? Open Settings and use "Delete all data." This permanently removes every notebook and folder, including everything inside them, and can't be undone.

Does Quill work on iPhone? Not currently — Quill is designed specifically for iPad's larger canvas.
