Common questions

Where are my lessons saved? Wherever you choose — CourseCanvas saves standard .cvs files through the normal macOS Save panel. There's no built‑in library or cloud sync; treat .cvs files like any other document and back them up however you back up the rest of your Mac.

Does CourseCanvas autosave? Yes. Once a document has been saved at least once, CourseCanvas writes your changes to disk automatically about 1.5 seconds after you stop editing. Brand‑new, never‑saved documents still need an initial ⌘S (or Save As) to pick a location.

How do I turn my canvas into a slide deck? Add a Slide (press F, or use the + button next to "Slides" in the left sidebar). Each frame becomes one stop in Presenter Mode — press ⌘P to enter, then ⌘→ / ⌘← to move between frames with an eased zoom transition. Press Esc to exit.

What are the laser pointer, spotlight, and zoom‑to‑region for? They're live‑teaching aids that don't touch your canvas content: hold the ` key for a fading laser trail, press Shift+S to dim everything but a spotlight around your cursor, and hold Z while dragging to ease the camera into a region (press Esc to return). Press ? any time to see the full shortcut sheet.

Can I add real, runnable‑looking code to a lesson? Yes — add a Code block from the right sidebar (or press the Code tool). It's a full syntax‑highlighted editor supporting JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Swift, Go, Rust, SQL, Shell, HTML, CSS, JSON, and Markdown.

How do I export a lesson to share it? Use File → Export → PNG…, SVG…, or PDF… (⌘⇧E for PNG) to save a copy outside the .cvs format.

Is my work backed up or synced anywhere? No. CourseCanvas makes no network connections and has no accounts — your .cvs files are the only copy, stored wherever you saved them. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I uninstall it? Quit the app and drag it to the Trash. Nothing is installed system‑wide.
