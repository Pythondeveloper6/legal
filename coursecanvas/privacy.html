CourseCanvas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

CourseCanvas is built to stay out of your way and out of your data. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • CourseCanvas does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.
  • Your lessons live in a single .cvs file on your own disk, opened and saved through the standard macOS Open/Save panels.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third‑party trackers.
  • The app makes no outbound network connections of its own.

How CourseCanvas works

CourseCanvas is an infinite‑canvas whiteboard for live teaching. Everything you draw, type, or paste — shapes, text, sticky notes, code blocks, and slide frames — is held in memory by the editor and written to a .cvs file (a JSON document containing a snapshot of the canvas) only when you save, either manually (⌘S) or through the built‑in autosave, which writes to disk about 1.5 seconds after you stop making changes to a document that already has a location on disk.

Opening a file, saving a file, and exporting to PNG, SVG, or PDF all go through native macOS file dialogs (NSOpenPanel / NSSavePanel) triggered by your explicit action. CourseCanvas only ever reads or writes the specific file path you chose. It keeps no separate copy anywhere else and does not scan your disk for other files.

CourseCanvas opens no internet connection. It does not phone home, check for updates over the network, or send telemetry of any kind.

What CourseCanvas stores

  • Your document — the .cvs file you choose to save, at the location you choose.
  • Recent documents — macOS's own "Open Recent" list (app.addRecentDocument), stored by the operating system, not by CourseCanvas.
  • UI preferences — your last‑used theme and canvas background are saved inside each document's own metadata, not in a separate account or cloud profile.

CourseCanvas stores no personal information, no usage logs, and nothing outside the files you explicitly save.

Apple services

CourseCanvas uses no Apple online services — no iCloud sync, no analytics, no StoreKit. The app bundles no non‑Apple SDKs.

Your control

  • Quitting the app stops all activity immediately.
  • Deleting a .cvs file deletes that lesson; nothing else references it.
  • Removing the app removes it entirely. Nothing is installed system‑wide.

Children

CourseCanvas collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
