Overlap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Overlap is built so your match data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Overlap does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your matches — heart rate, distance, speed, location traces, and the reports built from them — are stored on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Overlap stores

Every match you record is saved locally on your iPhone using Apple’s on‑device database, inside a private App Group container shared only with Overlap’s own widgets. This data is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Health, Location, and Motion

  • Health (HealthKit) — Overlap reads your heart rate and energy data to build your post‑match performance report, and can save your matches as workouts in Health. This data is provided by Apple’s Health app under your control and is never sent anywhere by Overlap.
  • Location — while you record a match on your Apple Watch, Overlap reads your position on the pitch to measure distance and build your movement heatmap. The trace stays in the match recording on your device. Recording positions is optional: you can turn it off before kickoff on the Watch and still get heart rate, effort and timeline for the match. A GPS route is the most revealing thing Overlap holds — it says exactly where you physically were — so it gets its own controls, described below.
  • Motion — on your Apple Watch, motion data is used to measure your sprints and distance during a match.

Overlap requests these permissions only to record and analyze your matches, and asks for them in context (primarily on your Apple Watch, where recording happens).

Apple services Overlap relies on

  • HealthKit — to read effort data and to save matches as workouts within your own Health database.

Overlap does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data. There is no iCloud sync, no account, and no sign‑in.

Your control

  • Record without positions. Before a match starts, the Watch offers a “Record pitch position” switch. Turned off, no location is recorded at all for that match.
  • Delete one match's GPS route. Open a match and choose “Delete GPS route.” The raw positions and the heatmap are removed permanently; the distance already measured from them, plus heart rate, calories and the timeline, are kept.
  • Delete every GPS route. Settings → Privacy → “Delete all GPS routes.”
  • Automatic route deletion. Settings → Privacy → “Keep GPS routes” can delete raw routes automatically after 7, 30 or 90 days. Overlap applies this every time you open the app.
  • Delete matches. Swipe a match away in the list, or remove every match at once in Settings → “Delete all matches.”
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

What Overlap does not claim

Overlap is a training aid, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or assess cardiac fitness. Heart-rate recovery is reported as the simple drop in beats per minute measured in the minute after your peak, and only when that minute was actually recorded — otherwise Overlap says so rather than showing a number. Distance and heatmaps come from wrist GPS, which loses accuracy near stands and in bad weather; every match report shows how much of it Overlap could actually see, and leaves numbers out when the signal does not support them.

Children

Overlap does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
