Common questions

How do I record a match? Open Overlap on your Apple Watch and tap Start Match. When you finish, your full report syncs to your iPhone automatically.

Why don’t I see any matches on my iPhone? Matches are recorded on your Apple Watch and sync over when they finish. Make sure Overlap is installed on your Watch and that you’ve allowed Health and Location there.

Where is my data stored? On your device. Overlap keeps your matches in a private on‑device database. No one else can see them, and there is no account to sign in to.

How do I set my max heart rate? Open Settings in Overlap and adjust “Max heart rate.” Leave it on Auto to use the highest rate recorded in each match.

How do I delete my data? Swipe a match away in the list to remove it, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all matches.”
