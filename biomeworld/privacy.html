Biome World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Biome World is designed for children, and exploring it — spinning the globe, meeting creatures, tracing food webs, playing the quiz — collects nothing about them at all. Two things in the app are entirely optional and live behind a parent gate: signing in with Apple, and subscribing to Biome World Plus. This policy describes exactly what each of those does and does not do with information on your device. It is written to be read by a parent in a couple of minutes, not by a lawyer.

Developer: Mahmoud Ahmed · Contact: apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com

The short version

  • Exploring the atlas — biomes, creatures, food webs, the quiz, the daily food chain — collects nothing about a child or a grown-up, and never has.
  • Two optional, adult-facing features sit behind a parent gate: Sign in with Apple and Biome World Plus, an optional subscription. Both are entirely optional; the app works without either.
  • Signing in stores an Apple identifier only in the device Keychain. It is never transmitted anywhere — Biome World makes no network requests of its own and there is no server of ours.
  • There is a visible Delete my account action inside the app that clears that stored identifier.
  • Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple; Biome World never sees a payment detail and receives only an entitlement status.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no trackers.
  • Everything the app saves — which creatures have been visited, settings, and the optional sign-in — stays on that one device, and a grown-up can remove all of it at any time.

What Biome World stores on this device

  • Exploration progress — the identifiers of the creatures your child has opened at least once, and a per-biome bookmark so the app can offer to carry on where they stopped. These are fixed identifiers from the app's own catalogue of 94 species; they are not names, not scores, and not anything about the child.
  • App settings — language (English / Arabic), narration on or off, sounds and ambience on or off, night mode, and whether the welcome screen has been seen. This is app configuration, never personal information.
  • The daily quiz allowance — on the free tier, a count of how many quiz rounds have been started today and the date it belongs to, so the allowance can reset each morning. It is a number and a date on this device, nothing more.
  • All of the above is kept in the app's own private storage (UserDefaults and the app's own container) on that one device. It is never uploaded, never backed up to a server of ours, and never shared with anyone. It travels only inside your own Apple device backup, if you use one.
  • Quiz answers are never written down. How many questions a child got right is held in memory for the current round only, to show a friendly "N right out of M tries" line, and disappears when the round ends. Nothing about a child's performance is saved to disk or sent anywhere.
  • No camera, no photo library, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no notifications, no health data, no identifiers for advertisers are requested or accessed. The app declares no privacy-sensitive permission at all, so iOS never shows your child a permission prompt.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Settings — behind the parent gate — offers an optional Sign in with Apple button. It is the only sign-in method in the app, and nobody has to use it: every part of Biome World, including exploration progress and Biome World Plus, works exactly the same whether or not anyone signs in.

  • What's stored. The stable user identifier Apple assigns this person for this app is saved to the device's Keychain. If the grown-up chooses to share a name or email with the app through Apple's own sign-in sheet (Apple only offers that choice the very first time an Apple ID authorizes Biome World), that is saved to the Keychain too. None of it is written to UserDefaults, to the progress file, or anywhere else.
  • Never transmitted. Biome World makes no network requests of its own, has no server, and sends the Apple identifier, name or email nowhere. They exist only in that device's Keychain, for that one device.
  • Why it exists. Purely to identify the grown-up in charge of the device, so a purchase can be recognised later. It unlocks nothing by itself, and it never appears anywhere a child can see it.
  • Sign Out. A visible Sign Out control, behind the parent gate, clears the stored Apple identifier and name from the Keychain. There is no server-side account to leave behind — the same Apple ID can sign in again at any time.
  • Delete my account. A separate, visible "Delete my account" control, behind the parent gate and its own confirmation, also clears the Keychain entry, immediately and permanently. Because there is no account of ours on any server, deleting it locally is the whole of what "delete" can mean here — there is nothing left on our side to remove, and no request to us is needed. You can also revoke the app's access entirely from iOS Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple → Biome World.
  • Automatic re-check. Each time the app launches it asks Apple whether a stored credential is still valid, and signs out locally on this device if the grown-up revoked Biome World's access from Apple ID settings elsewhere.

Biome World Plus (optional subscription)

Biome World Plus is an optional, auto-renewable subscription reachable only from behind the parent gate. The free tier includes three complete biomes — Rainforest, Savanna and Wetlands — with all of their creatures, their full food webs and three quiz rounds a day. Plus opens the remaining five biomes (Desert, Temperate Forest, Coral Reef, Polar Tundra and Mountains), removes the daily quiz limit, and unlocks the full food-web explorer across all eight. It never sells answers, hints, accessibility settings, privacy controls, or the ability to delete data — those are always free.

  • Two plans: a monthly subscription and a yearly subscription, with the same entitlement — only the billing period and price differ. Exact current prices are shown on the in-app paywall and on the App Store product page before you buy, and may vary by region.
  • Apple handles the purchase. Buying, renewing, and cancelling Biome World Plus all go through Apple's own in-app purchase system and your Apple Account. Biome World never collects, sees, or stores any payment detail — no card number, no billing address, no name on the card. That information stays with Apple.
  • What Biome World knows. The app asks Apple (through StoreKit) a single question: does the Apple Account signed in on this device currently hold an active Biome World Plus entitlement? It receives back only that yes-or-no entitlement status and the plan it belongs to. It does not receive your name, email, payment information or purchase history, and it makes no network request of its own to ask — the check happens through Apple's own on-device StoreKit framework.
  • Restore Purchases. A Restore Purchases control on the paywall asks Apple to re-sync this device's entitlements — useful after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. It does not require signing in with Apple.
  • See the Terms of Use for the full auto-renewal, cancellation and refund terms.

What Biome World never does

  • No network requests of its own. The app contains no networking code, no web view, no remote configuration, and no way for a child to reach the internet, an advertisement, or another person from inside it. Every biome, species photo, fact and sound ships inside the download, and the atlas works with the device in Airplane Mode. Sign in with Apple and Plus purchases are handled by Apple's own system frameworks (AuthenticationServices and StoreKit), not by any server or network code of ours.
  • No third-party code. There are no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting or social SDKs of any kind bundled in the app.
  • No profiling. Nothing the app stores is used to build a profile, to personalise advertising, or to make an automated decision about anyone.

Speaking the facts aloud

When narration is on, the app reads a creature's name and facts using Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. No text or audio is sent off the device to produce that speech, in either English or Arabic, and the microphone is never used or requested.

Children's privacy

Biome World is designed to be used directly by children and carries a 4+ age rating, with a parent gate in front of Settings, every external link, the sign-in button and every purchase. Biome World is not submitted to the App Store Kids Category, because that category does not permit account creation or data collection of any kind (App Store guideline 5.1.4) — and Biome World offers an optional account and an optional subscription, both confined behind the parent gate and both aimed at the grown-up, never the child.

  • No personal information is collected from children. Exploring biomes, creatures, food webs and the quiz involves no data collection, no profile, and nothing transmitted. There is no field anywhere in the child-facing part of the app that accepts a name, birthdate, photo or any other identifying detail.
  • The optional adult-facing features collect a minimum, and only from the grown-up who chooses to use them. Signing in with Apple stores an identifier (and, optionally, a name/email) in the device Keychain only; Plus purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Neither is reachable without first passing the parent gate, and neither is ever transmitted to us.
  • COPPA (US). The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act governs the online collection of personal information from children under 13. Biome World operates no server and transmits nothing it stores to anyone; the only account-like feature (Sign in with Apple) sits behind a parent gate, is optional, and is intended for the grown-up managing the device, not the child.
  • GDPR-K (EU/UK) and similar laws. Article 8 of the GDPR (and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code) concerns the processing of children's personal data. Biome World's exploration performs no such processing at all: no personal data is collected from play, no profiling or automated decision-making takes place, and nothing leaves the device. The optional sign-in and purchase live behind the parent gate and are designed for the adult, not the child.
  • No behavioural advertising and no tracking. The app shows no ads at all, of any kind, and never builds a profile of anyone for advertising purposes.
  • No contact with strangers. There is no chat, no comments, no community, and no way for a child to send or receive anything from or to another person.
  • No links out of the app without a parent. Settings — where sign-in, Biome World Plus, the photo credits and their source links, and every external link live — sits behind a parent gate, so a child cannot open a web browser, sign in, buy anything, or leave the app on their own.
  • Your rights as a parent. Because exploration collects nothing, and the optional account and purchase data are confined to your own device's Keychain and to Apple's own systems, there is nothing held by us to request, correct or delete. Sign Out and Delete my account (both behind the parent gate) clear the local Apple credential immediately, and "Erase my child's progress" removes everything else.

The parent gate

Settings sits behind a parent gate — a simple multiplication question a young child cannot yet solve. This is a soft barrier that keeps grown-up controls — sign-in, Biome World Plus, photo-credit source links and the app's external links — away from little thumbs. The gate itself is not an account, stores no password, and nothing entered there is transmitted anywhere.

Your control

  • Settings → Sign Out (behind the parent gate) — clears the stored Apple identifier and name from this device's Keychain. You can sign in again at any time with the same Apple ID.
  • Settings → Delete my account (behind the parent gate, with its own confirmation) — also clears the Keychain entry, immediately. There is no server-side account, so this is the complete deletion available.
  • Settings → Erase my child's progress (behind the parent gate) — removes every visited-creature record and bookmark on this device. This cannot be undone.
  • Cancelling Biome World Plus — see the Terms of Use for how to manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account. Cancelling does not delete anything Biome World stores.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data, including the Keychain entry, from your device.

App Store privacy label

Biome World's App Store privacy label declares:

  • Data used to track you: None.
  • Data linked to you: Identifiers (the Sign in with Apple user identifier) and Contact Info (name and email, only if you choose to share them through Apple's own sign-in sheet). Both are used for App Functionality only, and both stay in this device's Keychain.
  • Data not linked to you: None.
