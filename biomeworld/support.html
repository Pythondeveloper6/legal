Biome World — Support

Need a hand with Biome World? We're happy to help.

Contact

Email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Telling us your device (iPhone or iPad), your iOS version and the app version — Settings → About — helps us answer faster.

Common questions

What is Biome World? Biome World is a bilingual atlas of the living world for children. Spin a 3-D Earth, tap a glowing biome, and meet the creatures that live there — 94 species across eight biomes. Each creature has a photo, plain-words facts, and its place in the food web: who it eats, and who eats it.

How does the food web work? Open a biome and you'll see its creatures as bubbles joined by arrows pointing from eater to eaten. Tap a creature to highlight its links, and read the same information as sentences in the "Who eats whom" panel — the panel says everything the arrows draw, so it works just as well with VoiceOver or for a child who prefers words to pictures.

What is the quiz? "Who eats whom?" asks your child to build food chains from the biome they're in. It praises effort rather than scoring a child — the celebration screen shows a friendly "N right out of M tries" and nothing is ever written down or sent anywhere.

Does the app need the internet? No. Biome World makes no network requests of its own — every biome, photo, fact and sound ships inside the download, and the whole atlas works with the device in Airplane Mode. Signing in with Apple and Biome World Plus purchases go through Apple's own on-device system frameworks, not through anything of ours.

Is there Arabic support? Yes — the entire app, including right-to-left layout and spoken narration, is available in English and Arabic. Switch languages from Settings, or with the language toggle during onboarding.

How much does Biome World cost? Biome World is free to download. The free tier gives you three complete biomes — Rainforest, Savanna and Wetlands — with every creature in them, their full food webs, search, narration, the daily food chain, and three quiz rounds a day. Biome World Plus is an optional subscription that opens the other five biomes and removes the quiz limit; see below. There are no ads either way.

What does Biome World Plus include? Plus unlocks the five remaining biomes — Desert, Temperate Forest, Coral Reef, Polar Tundra and Mountains — with all of their creatures, removes the three-rounds-a-day limit on the quiz, and unlocks the full food-web explorer across all eight biomes. It's offered as a monthly plan (com.mahmoudahmed.biomeworld.plus.monthly) or a yearly plan (com.mahmoudahmed.biomeworld.plus.yearly), and it's reachable only from behind the parent gate. It never sells quiz answers, hints, accessibility settings, or the ability to erase your child's data — those are always free.

How do I restore a Biome World Plus purchase? Open the Biome World Plus screen (behind the parent gate) and tap Restore Purchases. This asks Apple to re-sync this device with your Apple Account's existing purchases — handy after reinstalling the app or setting up a new device. You don't need to sign in with Apple to restore a purchase.

How do I cancel Biome World Plus, or stop it from renewing? On your device, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Biome World Plus, and cancel or turn off auto-renew there. Subscriptions auto-renew unless turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Payment and refunds are handled entirely by Apple — request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Cancelling doesn't delete any progress; the five Plus biomes simply lock again once the paid period ends.

How do I sign in? Sign in with Apple is entirely optional — it's for the grown-up managing the device, and no part of the atlas requires it. Open Settings, solve the parent gate, and tap the Sign in with Apple button. Your Apple identifier (and a name or email, only if you choose to share them through Apple's own sheet) is saved to this device's Keychain only, and is never sent anywhere — see the Privacy Policy for the details.

How do I sign out? Settings → Sign Out (behind the parent gate). This clears the stored Apple credential from the device; you can sign in again anytime with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete my account (behind the parent gate, with its own confirmation). There is no account on any server of ours, so this clears the local Apple credential from the device's Keychain — immediately and permanently. You can also revoke the app's access from iOS Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple → Biome World. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription; cancel that separately through your Apple Account.

How do I clear my child's progress? Open Settings (behind the parent gate), then tap "Erase my child's progress" and confirm. This clears the record of which creatures your child has visited and the "carry on where you stopped" bookmarks. Biome World stores no personal data about a child, so there is nothing else to remove.

How do I get to Settings? Tap the small gear icon on the globe screen. You'll be asked a quick multiplication question — a gentle gate that keeps grown-up controls (language, narration, sign-in, Biome World Plus, photo-credit links and data deletion) away from little thumbs. It isn't an account or a login.

Can my child hear the facts read aloud? Yes. Tap "Read aloud" on a creature and the app speaks its name and facts in the active language, using Apple's on-device speech. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used. You can turn narration, sounds and ambience off any time in Settings.

Where do the photos come from? Every species photo is licensed and credited in Settings → Photo credits, behind the parent gate, along with a link to its source. Those links are kept off the child-facing screens on purpose.

Does the app ask for the camera, photos, microphone or location? No. Biome World requests no such permissions at all, so your child will never see a permission prompt for them.

Are there ads, or anything my child could tap out of the app into? No. There are no ads, no chat, no community, and no store visible outside the parent gate. Every external link in the app — rating it, contacting support, photo sources, this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use — plus sign-in and Biome World Plus, all live in Settings, behind the parent gate.
