Biome World is designed to be used directly by children, under general parental supervision, and carries a 4+ age rating. Settings — and every external link, the sign-in button, and Biome World Plus — sit behind a parent gate. A parent or guardian downloading Biome World for a child confirms that they are authorised to do so, that they are responsible for any sign-in or purchase made behind the parent gate, and agrees to these Terms on the child's behalf.

Accounts (optional)

Biome World offers an entirely optional Sign in with Apple button, reachable only from Settings behind the parent gate. It is the only sign-in method in the app. Nothing in the atlas requires it, and the app has no server-side account system of its own — your Apple user identifier (and a name or email, if you choose to share them through Apple's sign-in sheet) is stored only in this device's Keychain. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored.

  • Signing out. Settings → Sign Out (behind the parent gate) removes the stored credential from this device. You can sign in again at any time with the same Apple ID.
  • Deleting your account. Settings → Delete my account (behind the parent gate, with its own confirmation) also removes the stored credential. Because there is no server-side account, this is the complete deletion available, and it takes effect immediately. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription — see below.

Biome World Plus subscription (optional)

Biome World Plus is an optional, auto-renewable subscription. It unlocks the five biomes beyond the free tier (Desert, Temperate Forest, Coral Reef, Polar Tundra and Mountains) with all of their creatures, removes the three-rounds-a-day limit on the quiz, and unlocks the full food-web explorer across all eight biomes. It never sells quiz answers, hints, accessibility settings, privacy controls, or the ability to erase data — those remain free for everyone. Biome World Plus is reachable only from behind the parent gate, and buying it is always an adult's deliberate action.

  • Plans. Biome World Plus is offered as a monthly subscription (com.mahmoudahmed.biomeworld.plus.monthly) and a yearly subscription (com.mahmoudahmed.biomeworld.plus.yearly). Both grant the same entitlement; only the price and billing period differ. Current prices are shown on the in-app paywall and the App Store product page before you buy, and may vary by region.

  • Auto-renewal. Subscriptions automatically renew for the same length and price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • Managing and cancelling. You can manage your subscription or turn off auto-renewal at any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device (or in the App Store app → your account → Subscriptions). The developer cannot cancel it for you. If you cancel, access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, and it will not renew after that.

All billing, renewal, and refund handling for Biome World Plus is carried out by Apple through your Apple Account, and is governed by the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions.
