WidgetCanvas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

WidgetCanvas is built so your data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • WidgetCanvas does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • There is no WidgetCanvas server. There is nothing for the developer to store, because there is nowhere for the developer to store it.
  • The widgets you build and the data they show — habits, water logs, to-dos, photos, focus sessions, cycle dates, themes, and preferences — are stored on your device and, where you have iCloud enabled, a few small preferences sync through your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to them.
  • An account is optional. Every widget works without signing in.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What WidgetCanvas stores

Everything you create in WidgetCanvas (widget configurations, habits, water entries, to-do lists, focus sessions, cycle data, photos you pick, and theme/accent preferences) is saved locally using Apple's on-device database. A small set of preferences — the selected theme, the light/dark appearance, the app accent colour, and whether Pro is unlocked — sync through Apple's iCloud key-value store to your private iCloud account so your look-and-feel follows you across devices. This data is controlled by you and your Apple Account; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Optional accounts and Sign in with Apple

WidgetCanvas works completely without an account. Building widgets, placing them, buying Pro, and restoring a purchase never require one — signing in is an option in Settings → Account, and nothing is hidden behind it.

If you choose to sign in, here is exactly what happens.

Sign in with Apple

  • What Apple returns to the app: a stable, opaque user identifier for your Apple Account, plus a name and an email address only if you choose to share them. Apple sends the name and email once — on the very first authorization — and never again.
  • Hide My Email: if you choose Apple's private relay, the app receives a @privaterelay.appleid.com forwarding address rather than your real one, and labels it as a relay address in the Account screen. That is fully supported; the app never needs your real address, and it never sends mail to either one.
  • Where it is stored: the identifier and any name or email are written to your account record in the app's on-device database, and mirrored into the device Keychain — the encrypted store iOS provides for exactly this — so the one-time values survive a reinstall.
  • Where it is sent: nowhere. WidgetCanvas has no server and never transmits your identifier, name, or email to the developer or to anyone else. Apple, of course, knows that you signed in; that is Apple's own record, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not this one.
  • Revocation: if you stop using your Apple Account with WidgetCanvas from iOS Settings, the app notices at its next launch and returns itself to the signed-out state.

Email and password accounts

If you register with an email address and password instead, the address and display name are stored in the app's on-device database and the password is stored only as a SHA-256 hash in the device Keychain. Neither the address nor the password ever leaves your device — there is no server to send them to, no verification email, and no password reset by mail.

Delete Account

Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account from the device. It permanently erases the account record from the on-device database and the matching Keychain entry — the stored password hash for an email account, or the cached Apple identifier, name, and email for a Sign in with Apple account — and returns the app to the signed-out state it was in before.

Two things Delete Account deliberately does not do:

  • It does not erase your widgets, habits, photos, or history. Those were never tied to the account; they are local app data that exists whether you sign in or not. To remove them too, use Settings → Advanced → Delete all data, or delete the app.
  • It cannot clear Apple's own record that you used Sign in with Apple with WidgetCanvas. Because the app has no server, it cannot call Apple's token-revocation API — that call requires a server-signed secret and a serverless app has nowhere to sign it. To remove Apple's record as well, go to Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → WidgetCanvas → Stop Using Apple Account on your device.

Because nothing ever leaves your device, there is no copy of your account for the developer to hold, export, or erase, and no deletion request you need to send. If you have a question about any of it, email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com.

Purchases

WidgetCanvas Pro is sold as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. Every purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.

  • Apple bills your Apple Account. The developer never sees, receives, or stores your name, billing address, card number, or any other payment information — none of it passes through WidgetCanvas.
  • What the app keeps is a single on-device flag recording whether Pro is currently unlocked, recomputed from the App Store's own entitlement records. It is not a receipt, not an account, and not linked to you. It is one of the small values that can sync through your own iCloud account so your other devices know too.
  • Buying and restoring are the only moments a WidgetCanvas screen involves the network, and that exchange is Apple's, between your device and the App Store.
  • Apple's handling of the transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer.

Apple services and device data WidgetCanvas relies on

WidgetCanvas only reads the data sources you explicitly add to a widget, and only after you grant permission:

  • Calendar (EventKit) — to show your next event in the Calendar widget. Read-only.
  • Photos (PhotoKit) — only the specific photos you choose through the system picker for the Photo widget. WidgetCanvas does not read your wider photo library.
  • Location (Core Location) & Weather (WeatherKit) — your location is used to fetch local conditions for the Weather widget. Apple handles weather data under its own privacy policy.
  • Health (HealthKit) — only the specific metrics you choose (for example steps, heart rate, or sleep) so the Health widget can display them. Read-only; WidgetCanvas never writes to Health.
  • Notifications (UserNotifications) — only if you turn on a reminder for a widget. Reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent through a server.
  • iCloud key-value store — to sync the small preferences listed above within your own account.
  • StoreKit — for the optional Pro purchase, described above.
  • Sign in with Apple (AuthenticationServices) and the device Keychain — for the optional account, described above.

WidgetCanvas does not include any non-Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any widget at any time, or remove everything in Settings → Advanced → "Delete all data."
  • You can delete your account in Settings → Account → "Delete account."
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. iCloud preference copies are managed through your Apple Account settings.
  • You can revoke Calendar, Photos, Location, Health, or Notification access at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • A Pro purchase is not lost by deleting the app: it lives with your Apple Account and comes back with Restore purchases.

Children

WidgetCanvas does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
