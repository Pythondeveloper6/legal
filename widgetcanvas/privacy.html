WidgetCanvas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

WidgetCanvas is built so your data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • WidgetCanvas does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • The widgets you build and the data they show — habits, water logs, to-dos, photos, focus sessions, cycle dates, themes, and preferences — are stored on your device and, where you have iCloud enabled, small preferences sync through your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What WidgetCanvas stores

Everything you create in WidgetCanvas (widget configurations, habits, water entries, to-do lists, focus sessions, cycle data, photos you pick, and theme/accent preferences) is saved locally using Apple's on-device database. A small set of preferences (theme, appearance, accent, and Pro status) sync through Apple's iCloud key-value store to your private iCloud account so your look-and-feel follows you across devices. This data is controlled by you and your Apple ID; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services and device data WidgetCanvas relies on

WidgetCanvas only reads the data sources you explicitly add to a widget, and only after you grant permission:

  • Calendar (EventKit) — to show your next event in the Calendar widget. Read-only.
  • Photos (PhotoKit) — only the specific photos you choose through the system picker for the Photo widget. WidgetCanvas does not read your wider photo library.
  • Location (Core Location) & Weather (WeatherKit) — your location is used to fetch local conditions for the Weather widget. Apple handles weather data under its own privacy policy.
  • Health (HealthKit) — only the specific metrics you choose (for example steps, heart rate, or sleep) so the Health widget can display them. Read-only; WidgetCanvas never writes to Health.
  • iCloud key-value store — to sync small preferences within your own account.
  • StoreKit — when you purchase Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. WidgetCanvas never receives your payment details.

WidgetCanvas does not include any non-Apple SDKs that collect data.

Your control

  • You can delete any widget at any time, or remove everything in Settings → Advanced → "Delete all data."
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. iCloud preference copies are managed through your Apple ID settings.
  • You can revoke Calendar, Photos, Location, or Health access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

WidgetCanvas does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
