Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device. A small set of preferences (theme, appearance, accent, Pro status) sync privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. No one else can see your data.

Why doesn't my widget show data? Some widgets need permission to read Calendar, Photos, Location, or Health. Grant access when prompted, or enable it later in the iOS Settings app under WidgetCanvas.

What does Pro unlock? Every widget type, all theme packs, per-widget colors and fonts, history and stats, and future Pro features. It's a one-time purchase — no subscription.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here? Open Settings in WidgetCanvas and tap "Restore purchase."

How do I delete my data? Delete a single widget from its context menu, or remove everything in Settings → Advanced → "Delete all data."
