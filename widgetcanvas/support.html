Getting started

How do I actually put a widget on my Home Screen? First build one in the app: open the + (Add widget), pick a type, set it up, and save it. Then leave the app and, on the Home Screen:

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen until the icons start to jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top-left corner, then tap Add Widget.
  3. Search for WidgetCanvas and tap it.
  4. Swipe to the size you want (small, medium, or large) and tap Add Widget.
  5. Tap Done.

The new widget starts on whichever WidgetCanvas widget of that type you saved first. To point it at a different one, touch and hold the placed widget → Edit Widget, tap the Widget row, and choose the saved widget you want. That's also how you swap it later without deleting anything.

How do I put one on the Lock Screen? Lock Screen widgets are the small ones under the clock, and the Battery, Habit, Time, Water, and Cycle widgets are built for them.

  1. Wake the phone and touch and hold the Lock Screen until Customize appears (you'll be asked to unlock with Face ID or your passcode first).
  2. Tap Customize, then tap Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area just below the time.
  4. Find WidgetCanvas in the list, tap the shape you want (circular, rectangular, or the inline one above the clock), then tap Done twice.

To choose which saved widget it shows, tap the placed Lock Screen widget while still in Customize and pick it from the Widget row.

My widget is blank or shows a placeholder. Two usual causes. Either the widget hasn't been pointed at one of your saved widgets yet — touch and hold it, tap Edit Widget, and choose one from the Widget row — or it needs a permission it hasn't been granted; see the next answer. iOS also refreshes widgets on its own schedule, so a brand-new one can take a moment to fill in.

Permissions

Why does my widget say "Allow Health access"? Health data is only readable with your explicit permission, and — this is the part that catches people — the widget runs as a separate process from the app, so granting access has to happen in the app itself. Open WidgetCanvas, open the Health widget you built, and tap Allow Health access, then tick the metrics you want in the Health sheet iOS shows. The widget fills in at its next refresh.

If you tapped "Don't Allow" earlier, iOS won't ask again from the app: go to the Health app → your profile picture → Privacy & Security → Apps → WidgetCanvas and turn the categories on there.

The same principle applies to the other data widgets — Calendar asks for calendar access, Photo asks for the pictures you pick, and Weather asks for your location. Each is requested per widget, only when you add one that needs it, and all of them can be changed later in Settings → WidgetCanvas on your device.

Pro, purchases and subscriptions

What's free, and what's Pro? Free gives you three widgets, using the Clock, Time, To-Do, Water, Quote, and Battery types, on the Vibrant, Editorial, and Grid themes. Pro removes the three-widget limit and unlocks every widget type — Calendar, Weather, Health, Habit, Photo, Charts, Focus, and Cycle — plus the Sunset, Forest, Mono, and Brutalist themes, per-widget colours and fonts, history and stats, reminders and nudges, and iCloud sync across your devices.

What does Pro cost? US$2.99 per month, US$19.99 per year (with a 7-day free trial), or US$39.99 once for Pro Lifetime, which never renews. Other storefronts show the local equivalent, and the app always shows the price before you buy.

I already bought Pro — how do I get it on another device? Open the Pro screen in WidgetCanvas and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. There's nothing to type and no account needed — the purchase lives with your Apple Account.

How do I switch between Monthly and Yearly? On your device open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, tap WidgetCanvas, and choose the other plan. Apple works out the proration: an upgrade takes effect immediately, a downgrade at the end of the period you've already paid for. You can also start from the Pro screen in the app, which sends you to the same place.

How do I cancel? Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WidgetCanvas → Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it renews once more. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I cancel the free trial? Exactly the same way, at least 24 hours before the 7 days are up. If you cancel inside that window you're not charged.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you purchased with.

What happens to my widgets if Pro ends? Nothing is deleted. Pro features lock again, but every widget, habit, water log, focus session, and piece of history stays on your device, and the free app carries on. Resume Pro and it all comes straight back.

Accounts and data

Do I need an account? No. Every widget, and buying or restoring Pro, works without signing in. Signing in is optional and lives in Settings → Account.

What does Sign in with Apple share? An opaque identifier for your Apple Account, and your name and email only if you choose to share them. Apple's Hide My Email is supported — a private relay address works fine. Whatever the app receives is stored on your device, in its own database and the Keychain, and is never transmitted anywhere.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete account. It erases the account record and its Keychain entry from the device. If you used Sign in with Apple and want Apple's own record gone too, go to Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → WidgetCanvas → Stop Using Apple Account.

How do I delete all my data? Delete a single widget from its context menu, or remove everything in Settings → Advanced → Delete all data — you'll be asked to type DELETE to confirm. That clears every saved widget, habit history, water log, photo copied into a widget, focus session, theme preference, and synced iCloud value. Placed Home Screen widgets go blank until you build new ones. Deleting the app removes its local data too.

Where is my data stored? On your device. A few preferences — theme, appearance, accent colour, and whether Pro is unlocked — sync privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. There is no WidgetCanvas server, so no one else can see any of it.

Does WidgetCanvas track me? No. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.
