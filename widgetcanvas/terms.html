Free and Pro

WidgetCanvas is free to download, and the free app stays free. Without paying anything you can build up to three widgets, using the Clock, Time, To-Do, Water, Quote, and Battery types, on the Vibrant, Editorial, and Grid themes, and place them on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Cancelling later than that takes effect at the end of the next period, not the current one.
