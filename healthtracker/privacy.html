Throughline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Throughline is built so your health data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Throughline does not transmit, sell, or share your health data or personal data with the developer or third parties.
  • Your Apple Health data is read and processed entirely on your device. It never leaves your iPhone and is never sent to the developer.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

Throughline reads health metrics — sleep, heart rate, HRV, activity, workouts, body measurements, and related data — from Apple Health to forecast your day, find your personal patterns, score your vitality, and write your weekly story. All of this processing happens on this device. Your HealthKit data is never uploaded to any server and the developer has no access to it.

When you create a quick log (mood, caffeine, water, body mass, manual sleep, symptoms), Throughline writes it back into Apple Health so your other apps stay in sync. Apple Health data is controlled by you and your Apple ID; whether it syncs across your devices is governed by your own iCloud and Health settings, under Apple's privacy policy.

Throughline does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or any use-based data mining, and does not disclose it to third parties.

What Throughline stores on its own

A small amount of app state — your profile (age, sex, schedule, focus areas), goals, earned badges, saved recaps, and your settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database. You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → About → "Delete all app data." Deleting the app also removes this local data.

If you optionally use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides an account identifier and, on the first sign-in only, your name and email or a private relay address. Throughline caches that identity locally so it can recognize the same Apple account. The app has no developer-operated account server, analytics service, or advertising SDK, so these values are not uploaded to us.

Other device permissions

  • Calendar — read-only, to gauge your meeting load for a more accurate daily forecast. Throughline never writes to your calendar.
  • Location — used only to fetch local weather and daylight, which influence energy and mood. Processed on device.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — optional voice notes, transcribed on this device. Audio is discarded after transcription.

These are used on device to power features; none of this data is sent to the developer.

Apple services Throughline relies on

  • HealthKit — the source of truth for your health data, on your device.
  • StoreKit — when you subscribe to Throughline Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. Throughline never receives your payment details.
  • WeatherKit (when enabled) — Apple returns local conditions for your location under Apple's own privacy policy.

Throughline does not include any non-Apple SDKs that collect data.

Children

Throughline does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
