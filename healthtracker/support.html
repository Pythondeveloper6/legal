Common questions

Where does my health data go? Nowhere. Throughline reads your Apple Health data and does everything on your iPhone. Your health data never leaves the device and is never sent to the developer.

Throughline isn't showing my data. Make sure you granted Health access. Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Throughline, and enable the categories you want it to read. You can also re-check this in Throughline under You → Permissions.

What does Throughline Pro include? The full app: Pattern Hunter rules, the weekly Story, monthly recaps, and the Pro home/lock-screen widgets. It's an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly, with a free trial for new subscribers on the yearly plan).

I subscribed on another device — how do I get Pro here? Open the You tab, tap the Pro card, and use Restore purchases on the paywall.

How do I cancel? In the App Store: your account → Subscriptions → Throughline → Cancel. You can also tap "Manage subscription" on the paywall.

How do I delete my data? Go to You → About → "Delete all app data." This erases Throughline's local data and returns you to onboarding. Your Apple Health data is not affected — manage that in the Health app.
