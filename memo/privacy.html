Memo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Memo is built so your memories stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Memo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your memories — photos, titles, notes, dates, places, and moods — are stored on your device and, if you’re signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Memo stores

Everything you create in Memo (photos, text, dates, locations, moods, and favorites) is saved locally using Apple’s on‑device database. If iCloud is enabled on your device, this data also syncs via Apple’s CloudKit to your private iCloud account so it’s available on your other devices. This data is controlled by you and your Apple ID; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services Memo relies on

  • iCloud / CloudKit — to sync and back up your memories within your own account.
  • MapKit — when you search for a place to attach to a memory, the text you type is sent to Apple to return location suggestions. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.
  • StoreKit — when you purchase Memo Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. Memo never receives your payment details.

Memo does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Photos

Memo only accesses the specific photos you choose through the system photo picker. It does not read your photo library beyond the items you pick.

Your control

  • You can delete any memory, or all memories, at any time inside the app (Settings → “Delete all memories”).
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. iCloud copies are managed through your Apple ID settings.

Children

Memo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
