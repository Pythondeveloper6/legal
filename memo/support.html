Common questions

Where are my memories stored? On your device, and synced privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. No one else can see them.

How many memories can I keep for free? Up to five. Memo Pro is a one‑time purchase that unlocks unlimited memories.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here? Open Settings in Memo and tap “Restore purchase.”

How do I delete my data? Delete individual memories from each memory’s menu, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all memories.”
