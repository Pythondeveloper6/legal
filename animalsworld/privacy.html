Animals World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Animals World is made for children, so it is built to collect nothing about them. This policy describes exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

The short version

  • Animals World does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data — about a child or a grown-up.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no field anywhere in the app that accepts personal information.
  • The app makes no network requests at all. Every animal, photo and fact is bundled inside the download, and the app works with the device in Airplane Mode.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no trackers, no in-app purchases.
  • The only thing saved is which animals have been opened, stored on the device, and a grown-up can erase it at any time.

What Animals World stores on the device

  • Progress — the identifiers of the animals your child has opened (for example lion, blue-whale), plus the last animal opened so the app can offer "keep exploring". These are fixed identifiers from the app's own catalogue of 64 animals; they are not names, not scores, and not anything about the child.
  • Settings — language (English/Arabic or Follow device), whether facts are read aloud, day/night globe preference, and whether the welcome screen has been seen. This is app configuration, never personal information.
  • All of the above is kept in the app's own local UserDefaults on that one device. It is never uploaded, never backed up to a server of ours, and never shared with anyone. It travels only inside your own Apple device backup, if you use one.
  • No camera, no photo library, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no notifications, no health data, no identifiers for advertisers are requested or accessed. The app declares no privacy-sensitive permissions at all, so iOS never shows your child a permission prompt.

Reading facts aloud

When "Read facts aloud" is on, the app speaks the animal's name and fact using Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. No text or audio is sent off the device to produce that speech. If the device has no installed voice for the chosen language, or another app is holding the audio session, the narration button disables itself and says why rather than failing silently.

Photos

Every animal photograph ships inside the app under a free licence (Creative Commons or public domain). Nothing is fetched from the internet while your child is using the app. Each photographer and licence is listed under Grown-ups → About → Photo credits. The source addresses are shown there as plain, selectable text on purpose — nothing in this app opens a web browser.

The grown-ups area

Settings, photo credits and the erase control sit behind a parent gate: a spelled-out arithmetic question a young child can't read or solve, which generates a new question after a wrong answer. This is a soft barrier that keeps grown-up controls away from little thumbs. It is not an account, it stores no password, and nothing entered there is transmitted anywhere.

Your control

  • Grown-ups → Progress → Erase progress removes every animal your child has met and the resume point — every child-specific thing the app stores. The grown-up's own settings (language, narration) are deliberately kept.
  • Grown-ups → Progress → Show the welcome screen again replays onboarding.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from the device.

Children

Animals World is designed to be used directly by children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There is no way to type a name, birthdate, photo or any other identifying detail anywhere in the app, and no way for a child to reach the internet, another person, a store, or an advertisement from inside it. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it, in the same release as the behaviour it describes.
