Common questions

What is Animals World? A spinning 3-D globe with eight habitats on it — savanna, Amazon, polar, outback, Asian jungle, European forest, American mountains and the ocean. Tap a place, meet its eight animals, and each one has photos, a baby name, a size, what it eats, and a "did you know?" fact. Sixty-four animals in all.

Does it need the internet? No. Animals World never makes a network request — everything is inside the download. It works in Airplane Mode, on a plane, in a car, anywhere.

How much does it cost? Animals World is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

Is it safe for my child alone? Yes. There is no way for a child to reach the internet, a store, another person, or an advertisement from inside the app. Settings, photo credits and the erase control sit behind a parent gate — a spelled-out arithmetic question a young child can't read or solve.

How do I get to the grown-ups area? Tap the lock button on the globe screen and answer the spelled-out question. A wrong answer simply gives you a new question.

Is it in Arabic? Yes — every animal name, fact and label is written in English and Arabic, the layout flips to right-to-left, and numbers are shown in Eastern Arabic digits. Switch language from the globe's language button, or in Grown-ups → Settings.

Can it read the facts aloud? Yes, using the voices already on your device — turn "Read facts aloud" on or off in Grown-ups → Settings. If your device has no voice installed for the chosen language, the narration button tells you so instead of doing nothing.

What is the animal of the day, and the widget? Each day the app picks one animal to feature, the same one for everybody, and rolls over at your device's own local midnight. Add the Animals World widget to your Home Screen to see it there and tap straight through to that animal.

Does it work with VoiceOver? Yes. Every animal card announces whether it has been met, the globe has an "All places" list as a full alternative for anyone who can't use a 3-D globe, all controls are at least 44 points, and Reduce Motion stops the globe's spin, the confetti and the auto-advancing fact carousel.

How do I erase my child's progress? Grown-ups → Progress → Erase progress. It forgets every animal met; your language and narration settings stay as they are.

Where did the photos come from? From photographers who released them under free licences. Every photo's author, licence and source address is listed in Grown-ups → About → Photo credits, shown as plain text because nothing in this app opens a browser.
