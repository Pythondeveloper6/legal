Parity — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Parity is built so your money stays your business. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Parity does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your salary, expenses, budgets, goals, and categories are stored on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers, and no account to create.

What Parity stores

Everything you enter in Parity (salary, currencies, expenses, budgets, savings goals, and categories) is saved locally on your device as a private file. This data is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it. Parity does not sign you in and does not upload your financial data anywhere.

Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, Parity fetches public, mid‑market exchange‑rate data from open.er-api.com. Only a currency code (for example, “EGP”) is included in the request so the right rates come back. No personal or financial information is ever sent. Rates are cached on your device so the app works offline.

Apple services Parity relies on

  • StoreKit — when you purchase Parity Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. Parity never receives your payment details.

Parity does not include any non‑Apple analytics or advertising SDKs.

Your control

  • You can edit or delete any expense, budget, or goal at any time inside the app.
  • You can erase everything at once in Settings → “Reset all data.”
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Parity does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
