Common questions

Where is my financial data stored? On your device only. Parity has no account and uploads nothing. No one else can see it.

Where do the exchange rates come from? Public mid‑market rates from open.er-api.com. Only a currency code is sent — never any personal or financial information. Rates are cached so the app works offline.

What’s free, and what’s in Parity Pro? Free includes up to two budgets and one savings goal. Parity Pro is a one‑time purchase that unlocks unlimited budgets and goals, the parallel‑market rate override, custom categories, home‑screen widgets, and accent themes.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here? Open Settings in Parity and tap “Restore.”

How do I delete my data? Edit or delete any item inline, or erase everything in Settings → “Reset all data.”
