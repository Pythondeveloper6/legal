Common questions

Where is my reading data stored? On your device. Signing in with Apple is optional and stays on this device — nothing about your reading is ever uploaded to us.

Do I need an account? No. Every feature works without signing in. Signing in with Apple only puts your name in the app and ties a Leaf Premium purchase to your Apple Account so restoring it on a new device is one tap.

What does Leaf Premium include, and how much does it cost? Leaf Premium removes the 5-book free limit and unlocks the full Stats tab, Year in Reading, reading goals, the full widget family, and appearance extras. It's available as Monthly ($2.99/month), Yearly ($14.99/year, with a free trial for new subscribers), or Lifetime ($34.99, once). Prices are shown in US dollars; your local price is set by your App Store storefront and may vary. Manage or cancel a subscription any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. If Premium lapses, nothing you've already saved is ever deleted, hidden, or locked.

How do I add a book? Tap the + in your Library, then scan the barcode, search by title or author, or enter the details by hand.

The book details look wrong — can I fix them? Yes. Book information comes from Open Library and can be incomplete. Add a book by hand to type the exact title, author, and page count.

How do reading streaks work? Log your progress on a day and it counts toward your streak. A few pages a day keeps it alive.

How do I delete my data? Delete an individual book by swiping it in your Library, or remove everything in Settings → "Delete all data."
