Leaf — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Leaf is built so your reading stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Leaf does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your library — books, reading progress, sessions, notes, and stats — is stored on your device. The developer has no access to it.
  • Signing in with Apple is entirely optional and unlocks nothing on its own; when used, it is stored only on this device and is never transmitted.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Leaf stores

Everything you create in Leaf (books, total pages, reading sessions, notes, dates, and the covers you add) is saved locally using Apple's on‑device database. This data stays on your device and is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Your account (optional)

Leaf never requires an account. If you choose to sign in with Sign in with Apple, here is exactly what happens and what does not:

  • Leaf requests only your name (fullName). It never requests, receives, or stores your email address — Leaf has no server, so there would be nothing legitimate to do with one.
  • Your name and the identifier Apple assigns are saved only to this device's Keychain, protected so they never sync to iCloud Keychain and never leave in a device backup. They are never transmitted anywhere — Leaf has no server to send them to.
  • Signing in unlocks nothing. Every feature, including everything in Leaf Premium, works identically whether you are signed in or not. Signing in only puts your name in the app and ties a Leaf Premium purchase to your Apple Account so restoring it on a new device is one tap.
  • Your library, reading sessions, and notes are never linked to your account. They existed before you sign in and remain untouched if you sign out or delete your account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Leaf account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account record — your name and the Sign in with Apple identifier — from this device's Keychain. It does not touch your library: no book, reading session, or note was ever tied to the account, so deleting it leaves your library exactly as it was. To remove your library too, use Settings → "Delete all data" separately.

Leaf has no server and cannot call Apple's Sign in with Apple revoke API on your behalf, so deleting your account inside Leaf does not remove Apple's own record that you used Sign in with Apple with this app. To remove that record yourself, go to Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Leaf → Stop Using Apple Account on your device.

Network use

Leaf is mostly an offline app, with two optional online features:

  • Book search & covers — when you search for a book or scan a barcode, Leaf queries Open Library (openlibrary.org and covers.openlibrary.org), a public catalogue operated by the Internet Archive. This is a third party outside Leaf, and their own privacy policy applies to the request. Only the text you search or the ISBN you scan is sent, together with the ordinary network information any web request carries (such as your IP address). Nothing about you, your library, your reading sessions, or your device identity is attached, and Leaf sends no identifier of any kind.
  • These lookups are entirely optional. You can add every book by hand and Leaf will never contact Open Library. Search and barcode scanning are conveniences, not requirements — if you are offline or a lookup fails, manual entry is always available.
  • Leaf keeps recent search results in memory only, so repeating a search doesn't make another request. That cache is never written to disk and is discarded when the app closes.
  • That's it. Leaf includes no non‑Apple analytics or advertising SDKs.

Leaf ships an Apple privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declaring no tracking, no collected data types, and the reasons for the only sensitive APIs it touches (its own preferences, and writing an export file you asked for).

Camera

Leaf uses the camera only when you choose to scan a book's barcode, to read the ISBN and look the book up for you. Leaf does not record or store video, and does not access the camera at any other time.

Photos

If you add a cover image, Leaf only accesses the specific photo you choose through the system photo picker. It does not read your photo library beyond the item you pick.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reading reminder, Leaf schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Your control

  • You can export your whole library — every book and every reading session — as a CSV or JSON file at any time (Settings → "Your data"). The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.
  • You can delete any book, or all of your data, at any time inside the app (Settings → "Delete all data").
  • If you signed in with Apple, you can delete your account record at any time (Settings → Account → Delete Account) — see "Deleting your account" above.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Children

Leaf does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
