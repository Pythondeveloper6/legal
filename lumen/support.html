Common questions

How do I set a wallpaper? Design something in the Create tab, then use Apply as wallpaper (in the app or from the menu‑bar item). Lumen sets it on every connected display.

What is a live wallpaper? Lumen can animate your design directly on the desktop, behind your icons. Toggle it with Start live wallpaper in the menu‑bar item, or press ⇧⌘L.

Can I rotate wallpapers automatically? Yes. Open the studio settings (gear), enable Auto‑rotate, choose a source collection and an interval, and Lumen will change your wallpaper on a schedule.

Where are my designs stored? On your Mac, inside the app’s own container. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I export an image or video? Use Export in the Create tab. You choose exactly where the file is saved.

How do I delete everything? Open Settings (⌘,) → Reset all Lumen data.
