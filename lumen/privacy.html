Lumen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Lumen is a wallpaper studio that runs entirely on your Mac. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Lumen does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Everything you make — designs, palettes, collections, and saved wallpapers — stays on your Mac.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third‑party trackers.

What Lumen stores

Your saved designs, color palettes, collections, preferences, and the wallpaper images Lumen generates are written to the app's own container on your Mac (inside ~/Library/Containers/Application Support). This data never leaves your device and the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

When you choose Export…, Lumen writes an image or video file to the exact location you pick in the system save panel — and nowhere else.

When you apply a wallpaper, Lumen renders an image into its own container and asks macOS to set it as your desktop picture using Apple's standard NSWorkspace API.

Apple services Lumen relies on

  • None that transmit your data. Lumen uses Metal (on‑device rendering), AppKit, and the system desktop‑picture API. It does not use iCloud, analytics, or any non‑Apple SDKs.

Network

Lumen does not make network requests. It works fully offline.

Your control

  • Delete any saved design or collection at any time inside the app.
  • Remove everything at once with Settings → Reset all Lumen data.
  • Deleting the app removes its container and all of its data from your Mac.

Children

Lumen does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
