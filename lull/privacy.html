Lull — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Lull is a sleep-schedule and chronotype coach built so your health data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Lull does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your sleep, heart rate, and HRV are read from Apple Health on your device and stay on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There is no backend, no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What Lull reads

With your explicit permission, Lull reads the following from Apple Health, read-only:

  • Sleep analysis — to build your morning report, sleep stages, and trends.
  • Resting heart rate — shown alongside each night.
  • Heart rate variability (HRV) — shown alongside each night.

Lull never writes to Apple Health. Permission is requested with an empty write scope, so the app is technically incapable of adding to or changing your Health record.

Where your data lives

Everything Lull computes stays on your device. There is no server, so your health data is never transmitted off the device — not to the developer, not to anyone.

  • The nightly summaries Lull computes are cached locally in an App Group container (group.com.mahmoudahmed.lull) so the app and its home-screen widget can read the same data quickly. The App Group is a private area of your device's storage; nothing in it leaves the device.
  • Your schedule preferences (wake time, sleep need, chronotype, appearance) are stored locally on your device.

Health data is never used for advertising

In line with Apple's HealthKit requirements, and as a matter of how the app is built:

  • Lull does not use health data for advertising, marketing, or any use-based data mining.
  • Lull does not disclose or sell health data to third parties, including data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics providers.
  • Lull contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Lull schedules local notifications for your wind-down and wake times. These are scheduled by iOS on your device. No push server is involved and no notification content is sent anywhere.

Apple services Lull relies on

  • HealthKit — to read the sleep and heart data above, only after you grant permission. Access is read-only and you can revoke it at any time in the Health app.
  • Local notifications — only if you enable reminders.

Lull does not include any non-Apple SDKs. Lull has no servers, no accounts, and no in-app purchases.

Your control

  • You can revoke Lull's access to Health data at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices, or inside the Apple Health app.
  • You can clear Lull's locally cached data at any time inside the app (Settings → "Delete all data").
  • You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data, including the App Group cache, from your device. Your Apple Health data is unaffected and remains in the Health app.

Children

Lull does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not a medical device

Lull is informational only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Talk to a qualified health professional about any health concern.

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
