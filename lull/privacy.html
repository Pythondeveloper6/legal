Lull — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Lull is built so your health data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Lull does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your sleep, heart rate, and HRV are read from Apple Health on your device and stay on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third‑party trackers.

What Lull reads

With your permission, Lull reads the following from Apple Health, read‑only:

  • Sleep analysis — to build your morning report, sleep stages, and trends.
  • Resting heart rate — shown alongside each night.
  • Heart rate variability (HRV) — shown alongside each night.

Lull never writes to Apple Health, and it never uploads this data anywhere. The nightly summaries Lull computes are cached locally on your device (in an app group container) so the app and its widget can show them quickly. Your schedule preferences (wake time, sleep need, chronotype, appearance) are stored locally on your device.

Apple services Lull relies on

  • HealthKit — to read the sleep and heart data above, only after you grant permission. Access is read‑only and you can revoke it at any time in the Health app.

Lull does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data. Lull has no servers and no in‑app purchases.

Your control

  • You can revoke Lull's access to Health data at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices, or inside the Apple Health app.
  • You can clear Lull's locally cached data at any time inside the app (Settings → "Delete all data").
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Lull does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

Lull is not a medical device. It provides general sleep guidance based on published research and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
