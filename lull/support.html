Common questions

Where does my sleep data come from? From Apple Health. With your permission, Lull reads your sleep analysis, resting heart rate, and HRV — read-only. For sleep stages and heart data, wear an Apple Watch to bed.

Does my data leave my device? No. Lull has no server, no account, and no analytics. Your health data is never transmitted off the device, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with third parties.

Lull isn't showing any data. Make sure you granted Health access (Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Lull), and that you have recent sleep recorded in Apple Health. Sleep stages and HRV require an Apple Watch. Give it a night or two of recorded sleep, then reopen the app.

How do I change my wake time or sleep need? Open Settings in Lull and adjust the Schedule section.

My reminders aren't arriving. Lull's wind-down and wake reminders are local notifications. Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Lull, and that reminders are switched on in Lull's own Settings. A Focus mode or Sleep Focus can also hold them back.

Why does the widget show different numbers from the app? The app and its home-screen widget share one on-device cache. Widgets are refreshed by iOS on its own schedule, so the widget can lag briefly. Opening the app refreshes both.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in Lull and tap "Delete all data" to clear Lull's locally cached summaries and reset your preferences. To revoke Health access, use Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device; your Apple Health data is unaffected.

Is Lull a medical device? No. Lull is informational only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.
