Common questions

Where does my sleep data come from? From Apple Health. Lull reads your sleep, resting heart rate, and HRV read‑only, with your permission. For sleep stages and heart data, wear an Apple Watch to bed.

Does my data leave my device? No. Your health data stays on your device. Lull has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

Lull isn't showing any data. Make sure you granted Health access (Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Lull), and that you have recent sleep recorded in Apple Health. Sleep stages and HRV require an Apple Watch.

How do I change my wake time or sleep need? Open Settings in Lull and adjust the Schedule section.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in Lull and tap "Delete all data" to clear Lull's locally cached summaries and reset your preferences. To revoke Health access, use Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Is Lull a medical device? No. Lull provides general sleep guidance and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.
