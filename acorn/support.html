Common questions

Where is my children's data stored? On your device only. Acorn has no accounts and no server — every child, plan, skill, session, and milestone you add stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

How do I add a plan or a skill? Select a child in the sidebar, tap the "+" button to create a new plan, give it a subject icon, color, and dates, then add the skills you want to track within it. You can add, reorder, or remove skills any time from the plan's Edit screen.

How do I log a session? Open a plan and tap "Log session." Pick a duration, an optional mood, rate any skills you worked on by tapping their stars, add a note, and attach a photo if you'd like — then save.

How do goals and milestones work? When you create a plan, you can add starter goals — a number of sessions, a total number of minutes, or an average or peak skill rating. Acorn checks your goals every time you log a session, and the moment one is reached it automatically records the achievement date and adds a milestone.

What's the streak on the dashboard? It counts the consecutive days you've logged at least one session for that child, across all of their plans. It resets if a day is skipped, and shows next to this week's total minutes at the top of the dashboard.

Can I pause or finish a plan without losing its history? Yes. Open the plan and use the status menu to pause it until a date or mark it finished — all of its skills, sessions, and goals stay intact, and you can switch it back to active at any time.

How do I remove a photo I attached to a session? Open the session from the plan's session log and remove or replace the photo — deleting the session removes it entirely.

How do I delete a child or all of their data? Swipe to delete a child from the sidebar. This permanently removes that child and everything associated with them — plans, skills, sessions, goals, and milestones.
