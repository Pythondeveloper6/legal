Acorn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Acorn is built to keep everything about your children on your own device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Acorn does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data.
  • Every child, plan, skill, session, goal, and milestone you create is stored only on your device. The developer has no access to any of it.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What Acorn stores

  • Children, plans, skills, sessions, goals, and milestones — everything you add for each child, including session notes, mood, and star ratings — is saved locally using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData), inside the app's own sandboxed container.
  • Session photos — if you attach a photo of your child's work to a logged session, it is downscaled and saved as part of that session's local record. It never leaves your device and is only ever shown back to you inside Acorn.
  • Settings — your light/dark/system appearance preference is saved locally with UserDefaults, scoped to Acorn's own app data.
  • Widget data — Acorn shares a small on-device snapshot (one child's name, weekly minutes, and streak) with its home-screen widget through an App Group container. This stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. You choose which child the widget follows in Settings, and the snapshot is rewritten whenever that child changes or is deleted.

Sharing and exporting

Two things in Acorn can produce a file that leaves the app, and both are started by you:

  • Progress reports — a PDF you can print, save, or send. Before it is created you choose the period it covers, whether it names your child or shows initials only, and whether it includes skill ratings. Session notes and photos are never included in a report.
  • Data export — a readable JSON file containing every child, plan, session, goal, and milestone, so you can keep your own copy. Session photos are not included.

Both actions, and "Delete all data", are behind a short arithmetic question so a child using the device cannot share or erase their own record by accident. Acorn never uploads either file anywhere; where it goes afterwards is entirely up to you and the share sheet you choose.

Photo access

When you choose to attach a photo to a session, Acorn uses Apple's Photos picker to let you select an image. Acorn only ever sees the photo you explicitly pick — it does not get broad access to your photo library.

Your control

  • You can delete any child, plan, session, skill, goal, or milestone at any time inside the app; deleting a child or a plan removes everything associated with it.
  • You can correct or delete an individual session by tapping it in a plan's session log; Acorn recalculates that plan's goals and milestones to match.
  • You can export everything to a JSON file, and you can erase everything from this device in one step, from Settings → Your data.
  • You can remove a session's photo at any time by editing or deleting that session.
  • Deleting the app removes its local database, widget data, and settings from your device.

Children

Acorn is designed for parents and caregivers to track their own children's learning progress. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone — everything you enter about your child stays on your device and is never sent anywhere, including to the developer.
