Common questions

What is Puzzle World? A puzzle app for children aged 2 to 6 with 180 bright pictures in 12 packs (Shapes, Farm, Wild Animals, Sea, Dinosaurs, Cars & Trucks, Planes & Sky, Space, Food, Home, Toys, and Bugs). Children play shape-fit puzzles and jigsaws of 4 to 12 pieces. There is nothing to read.

Which packs are free? Shapes, Farm, and Cars & Trucks. The rest unlock with one one-time purchase, "Unlock All". There are no subscriptions and no ads.

How do I open the Parents corner? Press and hold the small gear button for about one and a half seconds, then answer the multiplication question. The same gate protects purchases.

How do I change jigsaw difficulty, sound, voice, or language? In the Parents corner. Difficulty can be automatic (it grows as your child succeeds) or fixed at 4, 6, 9, or 12 pieces. The app supports English and Arabic.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the Parents corner and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in to the Apple ID that made the purchase.

My purchase did not unlock the packs. Check your internet connection, then use Restore Purchases. If it still does not work, email us with the date of purchase.

Does Puzzle World need the internet? No. It works fully offline. Only the purchase and restore steps use Apple's App Store.

What data does the app collect? None. See the Privacy Policy.
