Puzzle World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Puzzle World is a puzzle app for children aged 2 to 6. It is built so that a parent can hand over the device without worrying about data. This policy says exactly what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

  • Puzzle World does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, from children or adults. There are no accounts and nothing to type.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own. The only online service it touches is Apple's StoreKit, used only if a parent buys "Unlock All".
  • Everything the app remembers stays on the device.

What Puzzle World stores, and where

To remember your child's place between visits, the app saves a few small items on the device, in Apple's standard UserDefaults storage inside the app's own sandbox:

  • Which pictures have been completed, in which mode, and the automatic jigsaw difficulty level
  • Which puzzle mode (shape-fit or jigsaw) was played last, so the next picture opens the same way
  • Settings: sound on or off, voice on or off, language (English or Arabic), and the chosen jigsaw difficulty

None of this is a name, email, photo, location, or any identifier. The developer never receives it. Deleting the app removes it.

On-device services

  • Spoken names (text-to-speech) — when a puzzle is finished, the picture's name is read aloud using the system's built-in, on-device voices. No audio is recorded, no microphone is used, and nothing is uploaded. It can be turned off in the Parents corner.
  • Sounds and haptics play locally on the device.

Purchases

Puzzle World offers one optional one-time purchase, "Unlock All" (all 12 picture packs). The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details, name, or Apple ID, and cannot see who bought. A parental gate (a multiplication question in words) must be answered before any purchase screen or the Parents corner opens.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K, and similar laws)

Puzzle World is designed for children and is in the App Store's Kids category. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it needs no verifiable parental consent and has nothing to disclose, share, or delete on our side. It contains no ads, no behavioural tracking, no chat or user-generated content, and no links a child can follow: the gate protects every link and purchase. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your control

Nothing leaves the device, so there is nothing to request, export, or delete from us. Deleting the app erases everything it stored.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change.
