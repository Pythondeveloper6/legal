Common questions

What is Halo? A glanceable, interactive HUD that lives in your Mac's menu‑bar notch (or a simulated notch on Macs and external displays without one). It shows Now Playing, a file shelf, widgets, clipboard history, notifications, a launcher, and sticky notes & timers.

Where is my data stored? On your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Halo/. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — Halo has no server and no account.

The notch is empty / I don't see it. Use the menu‑bar icon → "Toggle Notch", or your show/hide shortcut. On displays without a real notch, enable "Simulated notch on non‑notch displays" in Settings → General.

Now Playing or brightness controls don't work. These use Apple system frameworks that can change between macOS versions; Halo degrades gracefully when they aren't available. Make sure something is actually playing.

How do I change keyboard shortcuts? Settings → Shortcuts. Every action is rebindable.

How do I start Halo automatically? Settings → General → "Launch at login".

How do I delete my data? Clear individual items in each module, or use Settings → "Reset all Halo data" to wipe everything and return to defaults.
