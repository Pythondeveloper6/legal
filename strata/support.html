Common questions

What does Strata do? Strata shows what's using your disk — as an interactive treemap, a largest-files list, a type/age breakdown, and a duplicate finder — and helps you reclaim space by moving caches and files you choose into the Trash.

Is my data safe? Everything happens on your Mac. Strata reads only file metadata (and file contents for duplicate checksums), nothing leaves your device, and removals go to the Trash so you can undo them. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does Strata ask for Full Disk Access? macOS hides protected folders (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, other apps' data) unless an app has Full Disk Access. Grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access if you want Strata to measure and clean those places. You can run Strata without it on the folders you pick.

Will Strata delete something important? Strata refuses to touch system-critical paths and any folder on your exclusion list, moves everything to the Trash instead of deleting it, and asks you to review before removing. You stay in control. Still, keep a backup (Time Machine) before large cleanups.

How do I undo a cleanup? Open the Activity view to restore items Strata moved to the Trash, or recover them from the Trash directly until you empty it.

How do I remove Strata's own data? Open Settings (⌘,) → "Reset Strata" to clear its exclusion list, activity log, schedule, and preferences.
