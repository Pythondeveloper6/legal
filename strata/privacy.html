Strata — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Strata is a disk-cleanup and visualization tool that runs entirely on your Mac. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Strata does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data.
  • Everything Strata reads — file names, sizes, dates, and folder structure — is processed locally on your Mac. Nothing about your files ever leaves the device.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third-party trackers.

What Strata reads

To show you what is using your disk and to find reclaimable space, Strata reads file metadata — names, byte sizes, modification dates, and the folder tree — from the locations you scan. It needs to read this metadata to build the treemap, the largest-files list, the type/age breakdown, and the duplicate report.

For duplicate detection, Strata reads the contents of candidate files to compute a local checksum so it can tell identical files apart. This happens only on your Mac and the contents are never stored or transmitted.

All of this stays on your Mac. Strata has no servers and makes no network requests.

Full Disk Access

macOS protects certain folders (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Mail, and other apps' data) behind a permission called Full Disk Access. If you want Strata to measure or clean those locations, you grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access. This permission is enforced by macOS and can be revoked at any time. Strata uses it only to read metadata and to move items you choose into the Trash.

What Strata stores

Strata keeps a small amount of data on your Mac only:

  • An exclusion list of folders you mark as "never delete."
  • An activity log of items it has moved to the Trash (so it can offer one-click restore).
  • Your preferences (such as appearance and the optional weekly schedule).

These live in your local Application Support folder and are never uploaded.

Deleting and the Trash

When Strata removes files it moves them to the Trash rather than deleting them outright, so you can recover them until you empty the Trash. You can clear Strata's own stored data at any time from Settings → "Reset Strata."

Children

Strata does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
