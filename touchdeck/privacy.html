TouchDeck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

TouchDeck is built so that everything stays on your Mac. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • TouchDeck does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.
  • TouchDeck has no account, no login, and no servers. Nothing ever leaves your Mac.

What TouchDeck stores

Your bar layout, buttons, presets, snippets, and preferences are saved locally in a plain JSON file inside the app's Application Support folder on your Mac. TouchDeck does not use iCloud and does not back this data up anywhere off your device.

Accessibility access

TouchDeck asks for macOS Accessibility permission. This is used only to:

  • send keystrokes and keyboard shortcuts to the app you are currently using, and
  • read the frontmost app's menu‑bar shortcuts so it can offer them as buttons.

TouchDeck does not record what you type, log your keystrokes, take screenshots, or read the contents of any window or document. Nothing it sends or reads is stored, transmitted, or shared — every action happens locally, on your Mac, in the moment you tap a button.

Automation (Apple Events)

Some built‑in buttons (volume, lock screen, and any AppleScript or Shortcut you configure yourself) send Apple Events to macOS or to the apps you choose. These commands run locally and are not recorded or transmitted.

Your control

  • You can edit or remove any button, preset, or snippet at any time in Settings.
  • You can reset everything to defaults in Settings → About → Reset to defaults.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your Mac. You can also revoke Accessibility access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Children

TouchDeck does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
