Skillet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Skillet is built so your meal plans and shopping lists stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Skillet does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your household, meals, weekly plan, and shopping list are stored on your device. If iCloud is available, this data syncs through your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to it.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What Skillet stores

Everything you create in Skillet — your household name, meal slots (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or any you add), meals and their ingredients, your weekly plan, and the shopping list Skillet builds from it — is saved locally using Apple's on-device database (Core Data). If iCloud is signed in and available, this same data syncs via Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud account so it's available on your other devices. This data is controlled by you and your Apple ID; the developer cannot see or retrieve it. If CloudKit is unavailable (for example, no iCloud account or no network), Skillet falls back to storing data locally on that device only — it never fails to work.

Skillet does not include any non-Apple SDKs that collect data. It does not use your location, your contacts, your camera, or your photo library.

Apple services Skillet relies on

  • iCloud / CloudKit — to sync your household, meals, plan, and shopping list within your own private iCloud account, so the same data appears on every device you're signed into.

Your control

  • You can edit or delete any meal, planned meal, or shopping item at any time inside the app.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. iCloud copies are managed through your Apple ID settings.

Children

Skillet does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
