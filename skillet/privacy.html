Skillet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Skillet is built so your meal plans and shopping lists stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Skillet is developed and published by Mahmoud Ahmed, an independent developer, reachable at contact.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com. This policy covers the Skillet iOS app. It does not cover Apple's own services (the App Store, iCloud), which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The short version

  • Skillet does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your household, meals, weekly plan, and shopping list are stored on your device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — not to the developer, and not to any cloud service.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • If you subscribe to Skillet Plus, Apple handles the payment. Skillet never sees your payment method, and it keeps only a local yes/no record of whether the subscription is active.

Skillet's App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected: no data is collected by the developer, and none is linked to you or used to track you.

What Skillet stores

Everything you create in Skillet — your household name, meal slots (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or any you add), meals and their ingredients, your weekly plan, and the shopping list Skillet builds from it — is saved locally using Apple's on-device database (Core Data). It stays on that device. This version of Skillet has no cloud sync, no server, no account, and no sign-in. Apart from Apple's own in-app purchase sheet, which appears only if you choose to subscribe, the app makes no network request at all: it works entirely offline, and it never transmits your content off the device.

If you subscribe to Skillet Plus, the app also stores a single local flag recording whether the subscription is currently active. It is a yes-or-no value kept on the device alongside your settings, and it holds nothing about you and nothing about your payment.

Sync is not part of this version. If optional sync through your own private iCloud account is ever added, this policy will be updated in the same release, and the data would still be controlled by you and your Apple ID rather than by the developer.

Skillet does not include any non-Apple SDKs that collect data. It does not use your location, your contacts, your camera, or your photo library.

Apple services Skillet relies on

Two, both of them Apple's own, and neither of them a place your content goes:

  • The App Store, which distributes the app to you.
  • Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit), and only if you choose to subscribe to Skillet Plus — see the next section.

Skillet uses no other Apple online service, and no third-party service of any kind. Apple's part is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, linked above.

Purchases and subscriptions

Skillet Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription. If you buy one, the entire transaction belongs to Apple and happens in Apple's own purchase sheet:

  • Apple processes the payment. The developer is not part of the transaction.
  • Skillet never sees your payment method. No card number, no billing address, no Apple Account email, no name, and no receipt or order details reach the app or the developer. There is nothing of the sort for Skillet to store, and it stores none of it.
  • All Skillet learns is yes or no — whether an active subscription entitles this device to the Plus features. That answer is kept on the device as a local flag, exactly like your meals and your plan.
  • Skillet transmits nothing about your purchase. The app has no server of its own and makes no network request of its own, so no purchase information is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Adding the subscription therefore does not change Skillet's App Store privacy label. It stays Data Not Collected: the app collects nothing, transmits nothing, and links nothing to you.

Your control

  • You can edit or delete any meal, planned meal, or shopping item at any time inside the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that is a complete erasure.

Your rights

Because Skillet sends nothing to the developer, there is no personal data held about you for the developer to access, export, correct, or erase. Everything the app holds is visible inside the app and under your own control, and deleting the app erases it. If you are in the EEA or UK and believe otherwise, email contact.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com.

Children

Skillet does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
