WindowManager — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

WindowManager is built so that what happens on your Mac stays on your Mac. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • WindowManager does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.
  • Everything the app needs — your saved layouts, window rules, and keyboard shortcuts — is stored locally on your Mac.

Accessibility permission

macOS requires Accessibility permission before any app can move or resize the windows of other apps. WindowManager uses this permission for one purpose only: to move and resize windows on your Mac, locally, when you ask it to (via a keyboard shortcut, a menu command, an edge‑snap drag, or a window rule).

WindowManager does not read your window contents, take screenshots, log what you type, or record which apps you use. Nothing about your windows is stored or sent anywhere. The permission is used purely to reposition windows in the moment.

What WindowManager stores

  • Saved layouts and window rules are written to ~/Library/Application Support/WindowManager/ on your Mac.
  • Custom keyboard shortcuts and preferences are stored in the app's local user defaults.

This data never leaves your device. The developer has no access to it.

Global keyboard shortcuts

WindowManager registers global hotkeys so its commands work from any app. It only listens for the specific shortcut combinations you assign — it is not a keylogger and does not capture or store your keystrokes.

Your control

  • You can delete any saved layout from the menu, or remove everything at once in Settings → Reset all settings & saved layouts.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your Mac.

Children

WindowManager does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
