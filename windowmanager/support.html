Common questions

Why does WindowManager need Accessibility permission? macOS only lets an app move or resize other apps' windows after you grant Accessibility access. WindowManager uses it solely to reposition windows locally — it never reads your window contents or records anything. Grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

The menu‑bar icon's shortcuts don't seem to fire. Make sure Accessibility permission is granted (Settings → Privacy & Accessibility). If you recently granted it, quit and relaunch WindowManager.

How do I change a keyboard shortcut? Open Settings → Shortcuts and click any shortcut to re‑record it.

How do I make WindowManager start automatically? Turn on Launch at login in Settings → General.

How do I remove my saved layouts and reset everything? Open Settings → General → Reset all settings & saved layouts.
