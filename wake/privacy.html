Wake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Wake is built so your swim data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Wake does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your swims — distances, lengths, strokes, paces, and heart-rate readings — are stored on your device. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What Wake stores

Each swim you record is saved locally on your device. Wake keeps these swims in a private container shared between the iPhone app, its Home Screen widget, and the Apple Watch companion. This data is controlled by you; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services Wake relies on

  • HealthKit — Wake reads your swim workouts and heart rate from the Health app to build your post-swim analysis. This data is provided by Apple Health under your control and is never sent anywhere by Wake. With your permission, Wake can also save a recorded swim back to the Health app.
  • Apple Watch / WatchConnectivity — when you record a swim on your Apple Watch, the workout is transferred directly to your iPhone over Apple’s on-device connection.

Wake does not include any non-Apple SDKs that collect data.

Health data

Wake only accesses the specific Health data types you allow: swim workouts, heart rate, swimming distance, swimming stroke count, and active energy. You can review or revoke this access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Wake. Wake never uses Health data for advertising or shares it with any third party.

Your control

  • You can delete any swim, or all swims, at any time inside the app (Settings → “Delete all swims”).
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Data stored in Apple Health is managed through the Health app.

Children

Wake does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
