Common questions

How do I record a swim? Start a pool swim workout on your Apple Watch. When you finish, Wake transfers it to your iPhone automatically, where you can see your full analysis.

Where is my swim data stored? On your device. Wake reads your swims and heart rate from Apple Health, and keeps a private copy on your device for the app and its widget. No one else can see it.

Why does Wake ask for Health access? Wake reads your swim workouts, heart rate, distance, stroke count, and energy from Apple Health to build your post-swim analysis. You can review or revoke this access in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Wake.

Is Wake free? Yes. Wake is completely free, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

How do I delete my data? Remove a single swim by swiping it away in the list, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all swims.”
