Forge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Forge is built so your training data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Forge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your data — profile, routines, logged sets, sessions, and body‑weight entries — is stored on your device. The developer has no access to it.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Forge stores

Everything you create in Forge (your profile, routines, workout sessions, logged sets, and body‑weight history) is saved locally using Apple's on‑device database. This data lives on your device and is controlled by you. The developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Forge can:

  • Read your body weight and height from Apple Health to prefill your profile so you don't enter them twice.
  • Write your completed workouts to Apple Health so other apps and Fitness can see them.

Health data is handled by Apple's HealthKit under your control. Forge only reads the specific data types it asks for, and only if you allow it. You can change or revoke this access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. Forge never sends your health data anywhere.

Apple services Forge relies on

  • HealthKit — to read profile measurements and write completed workouts, only with your permission, as described above.

Forge does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data. There is no account, no login, and no in‑app purchase.

Your control

  • You can delete any session or routine inside the app, or erase everything at once in Settings → "Delete all data".
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Any workouts already written to Apple Health are managed through the Health app.

Children

Forge does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Forge offers training suggestions and estimates, not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting any exercise program.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
