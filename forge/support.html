Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device. Your profile, routines, sessions, and body‑weight history never leave your iPhone or iPad. No one else can see them.

Does Forge cost anything? No. Forge is free and has no in‑app purchases or subscriptions.

How does Apple Health work with Forge? If you allow it, Forge reads your weight and height to prefill your profile, and writes your completed workouts back to Health. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual sessions from the History tab, or erase everything at once in Settings → "Delete all data."

Is Forge medical advice? No. Forge offers training suggestions and estimates only. Consult a qualified professional before starting any exercise program.
