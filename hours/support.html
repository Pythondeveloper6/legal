Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device, and synced privately through your own iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. No one else can see it.

Why does Hours ask for calendar access? So its capacity math can account for the meetings already on your calendar. Access is read‑only — Hours never changes your calendar.

How do reminders work? Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing leaves your device.

How do I delete my data? Delete individual tasks or projects from their menus, or remove everything in Settings → “Delete all data.”
