Hours — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Hours is built so your plans and your time stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Hours does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your tasks, projects, areas, time blocks, focus sessions, and reflections are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled, this data syncs through your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to it.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

What Hours stores

Everything you create in Hours — tasks, projects, areas, estimates, schedules, time blocks, focus sessions, daily reflections, and settings — is saved locally using Apple’s on‑device database. If iCloud is enabled on your device, this data may also sync via Apple’s CloudKit to your private iCloud account so it’s available on your other devices. This data is controlled by you and your Apple ID; the developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services Hours relies on

  • EventKit (Calendar) — with your permission, Hours reads your calendar events so its capacity math knows about meetings. Access is read‑only; Hours never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events. Event details are used only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • User Notifications — if you enable reminders, Hours schedules local notifications on your device. No notification content leaves your device.
  • iCloud / CloudKit — to sync and back up your data within your own account.

Hours does not include any non‑Apple SDKs that collect data.

Documents you attach

If you attach a file to a task, Hours imports the copy you choose through the system document picker. It does not browse your files beyond the items you pick.

Your control

  • You can delete any task or project at any time, or remove everything at once in Settings → “Delete all data.”
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. iCloud copies are managed through your Apple ID settings.

Children

Hours does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
