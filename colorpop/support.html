Common questions

Does ColorPop collect any information about my child? No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and the app makes no network connections of its own. Drawings and profiles stay on your iPad. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I add another child? Open the parent area (the grown-up sum appears first) and add a profile under Children. Each child gets their own drawings, stickers and daily time. Up to six profiles are supported.

How do I set or change the daily time limit? In the parent area, edit the child's profile and choose a daily limit. When time runs out the child sees a rest screen, and a grown-up can add a little more time behind the gate.

How do I unlock all the categories? The Draw page and three categories are always free. Tap a locked category, pass the grown-up sum, and follow the unlock screen. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If you bought it before or reinstalled, choose Restore Purchase with the same Apple Account.

How do I save a drawing to Photos? Open the drawing's menu in the gallery, choose to save it to Photos, and pass the grown-up sum. The first time, iPadOS asks for permission to add pictures to Photos. If you said no, turn it on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > ColorPop.

The voice is silent or in the wrong language. Check that Voice is on in the parent area and that the iPad is not muted by volume. If the iPad has no installed voice for the app's language, ColorPop stays silent rather than speak in a different language.

Which languages are supported? English and Arabic. The app follows your iPad's language.

How do I print a drawing? Long-press it in My Drawings, choose Print, and a grown-up answers the question.

How do I delete drawings? Delete one drawing from the gallery, or delete all of a child's drawings from the parent area. Removing a profile removes that child's drawings too. Deleting the app removes everything from the device. We keep no copy on any server.

I forgot the grown-up sum answer, or the gate is locked. Three wrong answers lock the gate for one minute. Wait and try again; the sum changes each time.
