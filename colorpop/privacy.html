ColorPop — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

ColorPop is a coloring app for young children. It is built for the Kids Category, so it is built to ask for as little as possible. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your family's information.

The short version

  • ColorPop does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data about you or your child.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network connections of its own. The only online service it talks to is Apple's App Store (StoreKit), for the one-time purchase.
  • Everything your child makes stays on your iPad. The developer cannot see it.

What ColorPop stores on your iPad

A profile name is just a label so siblings can tell their pages apart. It is never sent anywhere. We suggest a nickname rather than a full name.

Photos

ColorPop can save a finished drawing to your Photos library, but only when a grown-up asks for it. The app first shows the parental gate (a grown-up sum). Only after the gate is passed does it ask iOS for add-only access to Photos. With add-only access the app can put a picture into your library; it cannot see, read or list any of your existing photos. You can turn the permission off at any time in Settings.

ColorPop requests no other permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, location or tracking permission.

Printing

If a grown-up chooses Print (after the parental gate), the drawing is handed to Apple's system print sheet and sent only to the printer they select. ColorPop does not send it anywhere else and needs no extra permission.

Voice and sounds

The friendly voice and the sound effects are played on the device. They use recorded clips that ship inside the app and, where needed, the voices already installed on your iPad (Apple's speech synthesis). Nothing is recorded, and nothing is sent to a server. The microphone is never used.

The parental gate and daily time limits

A simple grown-up sum protects purchases, saving to Photos, printing and the parent area. Three wrong answers pause the gate for one minute. The gate state and each child's daily time limit are stored only on the iPad. When a child's daily time is used up, the app shows a rest screen; a grown-up can add time behind the gate.

Purchases

ColorPop is free with three free categories, and the Draw page (a blank page to draw on) is always free. A single one-time purchase unlocks all the categories. It is not a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; ColorPop never sees your payment details or your Apple Account. Purchases are started only behind the parental gate. See the Terms of Use.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your iPad until you delete it.

  • Delete one drawing from the gallery.
  • Delete all of a child's drawings from the parent area (behind the gate).
  • Removing a child profile also removes that child's drawings, stickers and time ledger.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data from the device.

The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing to request from us and nothing we can recover. Device backups (for example iCloud or a computer backup) are governed by your device settings, not by ColorPop.

Children

ColorPop is made for children and collects no data from them. There is no account, no chat, no social feature, no advertising and no link that leads a child outside the app without the parental gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. Because ColorPop collects no data, a change would mean a change in the app's behaviour, which we would describe here before it ships.
