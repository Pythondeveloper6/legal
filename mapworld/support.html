What the app does

Map World is a friendly geography atlas for kids. Your child spins a gentle 3D globe, taps a continent, and drills into a country to find out where it sits on Earth, its capital city, its national animal, a landmark, its neighbours, the story behind its flag, and one "wow" fact. There are six continents holding forty-eight countries, plus the five oceans, six habitats, world records, a landmarks gallery and a country-size comparison. Seven calm map games, a daily challenge with a streak, collectible badges and a passport of stamps give a reason to keep exploring. Everything is bilingual English and العربية, and everything works offline.

Common questions

Does it need the internet? No. Map World works 100% offline. Every country, map, flag and fact is included in the download, and the app makes no network requests at all — it behaves identically in Airplane Mode. If something looks like it is "loading", it is only the globe drawing itself.

Is it free? Are there ads or in-app purchases? It is free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Nothing inside the app asks for money.

Does it collect any data about my child? No. Nothing is collected, and nothing ever leaves the device. Progress and settings live only in the app's local storage. See the Privacy Policy.

How does the parent gate work? Settings, the grown-up dashboard, the erase control and any link that leaves the app sit behind a multiplication question — easy for an adult, a wall for a young child. Answer it correctly to get in; a wrong answer generates a new question. It is not an account and no password is stored. If you leave the grown-up area and come back, you will be asked again.

How do I switch between English and العربية? Open Settings through the parent gate and choose Language. You can pick English, العربية, or Follow device, which uses whatever language iOS is set to. Arabic switches the whole app to right-to-left, including every country, capital, animal and fact — not just the menus.

How do I reset my child's progress? Open the parent gate, go to the grown-up dashboard, and choose Erase all progress, then confirm. That clears every country visited, starred place in "My places", passport stamp, badge, explorer level, daily streak and game record. Your own settings — language, appearance, narration — are kept on purpose. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

Can I turn the read-aloud narration or the sounds off? Yes. Both are toggles in Settings, behind the parent gate. Narration uses the voices already installed on your device. If a country is not read aloud, check that your device is not on silent, that the volume is up, and that a voice for the chosen language is installed under iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Can I choose light or dark mode? Yes — Settings offers Light, Dark, or Follow device.

What are break reminders? An optional, gentle in-app nudge suggesting it is a good moment to put the globe down. It is off by default and can be switched on in Settings. It sends no push notifications; the app has no notification permission at all.

How does the "Country of the day" widget work? Add it from the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget gallery. It picks a country from the date on your device, so it changes each day and works with no network. Tapping it opens that country in the app.

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored only on the device, so it does not follow you to a new iPhone unless it is included in an Apple device backup that you restore. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts fresh — there is no server copy for us to restore.

I found a mistake in a fact, a flag, or a translation. Please tell us. Email the country and what looks wrong, and we will fix it in the next update.
