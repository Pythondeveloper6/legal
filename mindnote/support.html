Common questions

Where are my notes stored? On your Mac, in the app’s on‑device database and Application Support folder. MindNote does not use the internet, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I back up my notes? Your Mac’s Time Machine backups include MindNote’s data. You can also export any document to Markdown or PDF from the editor.

How do I delete my data? Move individual documents to the Trash and empty it, or remove everything at once in Settings → “Delete all data.”

Does MindNote sync between devices? Not in this version. Your notes live on the Mac where you create them.
