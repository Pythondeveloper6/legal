MindNote — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

MindNote is built so your notes stay yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • MindNote does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your notes — documents, folders, blocks, tags, and any images you add — are stored on your Mac. The developer has no access to them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.
  • MindNote does not connect to the internet.

What MindNote stores

Everything you create in MindNote (projects, documents, folders, blocks, tags, and images you import) is saved locally on your Mac using Apple’s on‑device database (SwiftData) and your app’s Application Support folder. This data never leaves your machine unless you explicitly export it. The developer cannot see or retrieve it.

Apple services MindNote relies on

MindNote uses only on‑device Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, AppKit). It does not use iCloud, CloudKit, MapKit, analytics, or any non‑Apple SDK that collects data.

Images

When you add an image to a document, MindNote copies the file you choose through the standard system open panel into its own storage. It does not read your photo library or any files beyond the ones you pick.

Export

When you export a document to Markdown or PDF, the file is written only to the location you choose in the system save panel. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Your control

  • You can delete any document, or empty the Trash, at any time inside the app.
  • You can remove all data at once in Settings → “Delete all data.”
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your Mac.

Children

MindNote does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
