What the app does

Past World is a friendly history atlas for kids. Your child spins a gentle timeline-globe, slides the ribbon from 3100 BCE to 1500 CE, and taps a civilization to step inside everyday life. There are ten civilizations — Ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, Ancient China, Ancient Greece, Ancient Rome, the Maya, the Vikings, Feudal Japan, Medieval Knights & Castles, and the Age of Explorers — each told through eight daily-life themes (Homes, Food, Clothes, Work, Games & Play, Gods & Beliefs, Writing & Art, and Wonders) and real museum photographs. Along the way kids "Meet someone" from history and can hear every story read aloud. "Browse by Theme" lets you compare how different peoples lived, side by side. Everything is bilingual English and العربية, and everything works offline.

Common questions

Does it need the internet? No. Past World works 100% offline. Every civilization, story, photo and fact is included in the download, and the app makes no network requests at all — it behaves identically in Airplane Mode. If something looks like it is "loading", it is only the globe drawing itself.

Is it free? Are there ads or in-app purchases? It is free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Nothing inside the app asks for money.

Does it collect any data about my child? No. Nothing is collected, and nothing ever leaves the device. Progress and settings live only in the app's local storage. See the Privacy Policy.

How does the parent gate work? Settings and the photo credits list sit behind a simple question — easy for an adult, a wall for a young child. Answer it correctly to get in; a wrong answer generates a new question. It is not an account and no password is stored. If you leave the grown-up area and come back, you will be asked again.

How do I switch between English and العربية? Open Settings through the parent gate and choose Language. You can pick English, العربية, or Follow device, which uses whatever language iOS is set to. Arabic switches the whole app to right-to-left, including every civilization, theme and story — not just the menus.

How do I reset my child's progress? Open the parent gate, go to Settings, and choose Erase exploration record, then confirm. That clears every civilization, theme and "Meet someone" story your child has opened. Your own settings — language, appearance, narration — are kept on purpose. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

Can I turn the read-aloud narration off? Yes. It is a toggle in Settings, behind the parent gate. Narration uses Apple's on-device voices. If a story is not read aloud, check that your device is not on silent, that the volume is up, and that a voice for the chosen language is installed under iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Can I choose light or dark mode? Yes — Settings offers Light, Dark, or Follow device.

Where do the photographs come from? Every photograph is a real, public-domain or Creative Commons museum image, bundled inside the app — not an illustration and not fetched from the internet. Full credits for every photo are listed in Settings → Photo Credits.

How does the "Discovery of the day" widget work? Add it from the Home Screen widget gallery. It picks a discovery from the date on your device, so it changes each day and works with no network. Tapping it opens that discovery in the app.

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored only on the device, so it does not follow you to a new iPhone unless it is included in an Apple device backup that you restore. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts fresh — there is no server copy for us to restore.

I found a mistake in a fact, a photo credit, or a translation. Please tell us. Email what looks wrong and we will fix it in the next update.
