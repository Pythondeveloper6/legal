Common questions

Where is the Vitals window? Vitals is a menu-bar app — it has no Dock icon and no main window. Look for the live CPU … RAM … readout in your menu bar and click it to open the dashboard.

Does Vitals send my data anywhere? No. Everything is read from your Mac and shown to you in real time. Vitals makes no network connections and has no analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

Temperature and fans show "unavailable" — why? Vitals reads those from the System Management Controller (SMC). The classic SMC keys are an Intel-era feature; most Apple Silicon Macs don't expose them, so the section reports "unavailable." This is expected and not a bug.

How do I change which metrics show in the menu bar? Open the dashboard from the menu bar, or open Settings (⌘,), then choose under "Show in menu bar."

How do I make Vitals start automatically? Turn on "Launch at login" in Settings.

How do I reset everything? Settings → "Reset all settings" restores every preference to its default.
