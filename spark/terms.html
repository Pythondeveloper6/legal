Price, and what you get for it

Spark is free to download. It includes the Starter Lab — one complete experiment in each of the twelve science families, twelve experiments in all — free forever. The Starter Lab is not a trial: it does not expire, is not time-limited, and does not stop working.

The Full Lab unlocks all 94 experiments, plus every experiment added in future updates. It is optional, and it is sold in two forms:

There is no free trial and no introductory offer on either product.

Full Lab — Yearly (auto-renewing subscription)

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically for another year unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, or in your App Store account settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not shorten the period you have already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • If the subscription lapses, the twelve Starter Lab experiments and every gold star your child has already earned stay exactly as they are. Nothing your child has already done is deleted, hidden, or taken away because a subscription ended.

Full Lab — Forever (one-time purchase)

  • A single, non-consumable purchase. There is nothing to renew and nothing further to pay, ever.
  • It is tied to the Apple Account that bought it, and can be restored on any device signed in to that Apple Account.

Restoring a purchase

Both products can be restored with Restore Purchases in Settings, behind the parental gate. Use it after reinstalling Spark, or on a new device signed in to the same Apple Account. No account or sign-in is required to restore.

The parental gate

Every screen in Spark that can start a purchase, restore a purchase, or sign in sits behind a parental gate: a short multiplication question intended for an adult. This is required for apps in the Kids Category and is there so a child cannot spend money without a grown-up.

Optional sign-in

Spark works completely without an account. Sign in with Apple is offered in Settings, behind the parental gate, purely as an option; it is never required to use the app, to buy the Full Lab, or to restore a purchase, and it unlocks nothing on its own. If you do sign in, the identifier Apple returns is stored only in your device's Keychain — there is no Spark server. You can delete it from inside the app at any time; see the Privacy Policy for exactly what deletion does and does not remove.
