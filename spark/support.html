Common questions

What is Spark? Spark is a science lab for kids ages 4–9, on iPhone and iPad: 94 hands-on experiments across electricity, magnets, weather, light, sound, motion, space, matter, life science, air, earth, and machines. Each one is a single live scene your child drives with one gesture — no reading required.

What's free, and what costs money? Spark is free to download and includes the Starter Lab: one complete experiment in every one of the twelve science families — twelve experiments — free forever. They don't expire and they aren't trials.

The Full Lab unlocks all 94 experiments plus every experiment added in future updates. It's optional, and it comes two ways: US$9.99 per year, or US$49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices in your country are shown in the app before you buy.

What exactly does the lifetime purchase include? Everything the yearly plan includes, with nothing to renew: all 94 experiments today, and every new experiment added in future updates, forever, with no further payment. It's a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account.

How do I restore a purchase I already made? Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen), pass the parental gate, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. You don't need to sign in to Spark itself — there's no Spark account involved.

I got a new iPad (or iPhone). Do I have to buy the Full Lab again? No. Both the yearly plan and the lifetime purchase belong to your Apple Account, not to a device. Install Spark on the new device, sign it in to the same Apple Account, then use Settings → Restore Purchases. Your child's gold stars, however, are stored only on the old device and don't transfer — Spark has no cloud sync, deliberately.

How do I cancel the yearly plan? Cancelling is done in the App Store, not inside Spark: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Spark → Cancel Subscription on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, or that year will be charged. You keep the Full Lab until the period you already paid for runs out. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What happens when the yearly plan ends? The twelve Starter Lab experiments and every gold star your child has already earned stay exactly where they are. Nothing your child has already done is deleted or taken away — only the other 82 experiments lock again, and they unlock instantly if you resubscribe or buy lifetime.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we're not able to issue one. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If something in the app is actually broken, please email us too, so we can fix it.

Why does the app ask me a multiplication question? That's the parental gate. Every screen that can spend money, restore a purchase, or sign in is behind it, so a child can't get there on their own. It's required for apps in the Kids Category.

Do I need an account? No. Spark works completely without one, including buying and restoring the Full Lab. Sign in with Apple is offered in Settings behind the parental gate purely as an option, and it unlocks nothing on its own. If you've signed in and want it gone, Settings → Delete Account removes it from the device; the app will also point you to Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Spark → Stop Using Apple Account to clear Apple's own record.

Why is Spark landscape-only, and why won't it rotate? Spark is designed as a tabletop "lab bench" your child lays the device down to explore, so every experiment is built and tested in landscape only. Rotating to portrait isn't supported, on iPhone or iPad.

How do I switch between English and Arabic? Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and pick a language under the Language section — the whole app, including layout direction, updates immediately. You can also choose a language on first launch during onboarding.

Why doesn't the app speak in Arabic? On purpose. Spark's spoken narration is English only; in Arabic the app is silent rather than using a synthetic voice we're not happy with. All the Arabic text and the full right-to-left layout are there and complete. Nothing is lost either way — every experiment is nonverbal by design, and the spoken line is decoration on top of a scene that reads perfectly with the sound off.

My child doesn't have headphones — is Spark quiet enough to use around others? Yes. Every sound and spoken word is optional. Open Settings and turn off Sound and Haptics independently; every experiment still shows its full visual reaction, since sound and haptics are always decoration layered on top of the visuals, never required to understand what's happening.

What are the gold stars for? The trophy button on the home screen opens the Collection, showing every experiment your child has completed at least once. It's just a friendly way to see progress — there are no scores, timers, or competition.

Does Spark need the internet, or collect any data about my child? No. Spark works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own; the only network traffic it can cause is Apple's, handling a purchase or a sign-in you started. It stores a few small preferences (completed experiments, language, appearance, sound/haptics settings, and whether the Full Lab is unlocked) only on the device. No payment information ever reaches us. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

I found a bug, or an experiment isn't working right — what do I do? Email apps.mahmoudahmed@gmail.com with the experiment's name (shown in the title chip at the top of its scene) and what happened. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.
