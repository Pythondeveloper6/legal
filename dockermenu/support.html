Common questions

The menu bar icon shows "STOPPED" — what's wrong? DockerMenu reads the local Docker daemon at /var/run/docker.sock. If Docker Desktop (or your engine) isn't running, start it and the status will update within a few seconds.

Does DockerMenu send any of my data anywhere? No. It talks only to the Docker daemon on your own Mac and makes no internet connections. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I make it launch automatically? Yes — open Settings (⌘,) and turn on "Launch at login."

Removing a container asked me to confirm — is it permanent? Yes. Removing a container permanently deletes it and its writable layer. DockerMenu confirms first, but the action can't be undone.

How do I uninstall it? Quit the app and drag it to the Trash. Nothing is installed system‑wide.
