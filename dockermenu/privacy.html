DockerMenu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

DockerMenu is built to stay out of your way and out of your data. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • DockerMenu does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.
  • Everything it shows — engine status, your containers, and disk usage — is read locally from the Docker daemon already running on your Mac.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no third‑party trackers.
  • The app makes no outbound network connections of its own.

How DockerMenu works

DockerMenu talks only to the local Docker daemon through the Unix socket at /var/run/docker.sock on your own machine. It reads the engine version, the list of containers, and Docker's reported disk usage, and it can start, stop, restart, or remove a container when you ask it to. None of this information leaves your Mac.

DockerMenu does not open any internet connection. It does not phone home, check for updates over the network, or send telemetry of any kind.

What DockerMenu stores

The only thing DockerMenu persists is a small preference (whether it should launch at login), kept in the standard macOS preferences for the app on your device. It stores no container data, no logs, and no personal information.

Apple services

DockerMenu uses no Apple online services — no iCloud, no analytics, no StoreKit. The app contains no non‑Apple SDKs.

Your control

  • Quitting the app stops all activity immediately.
  • Removing the app removes its single preference. Nothing is installed system‑wide.

Children

DockerMenu collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
